# VIEW

- The pen and its kit: `Pen`, the one drawing contract three renderers paint, and the marks a figure builds from it.
- `#view` is `index.ts` and `#view/*` a file here, by the `imports` of `../../../package.json`; the `mrly` plugin of `../../../scripts/build.ts` resolves both in the build, in `../../../scripts/draw.ts` and in `../../../scripts/time.ts`, so the figures of `../../../../research/` reach it from outside `site/`.
- Every figure of the roster (`../../../README.md` has it) is a default export handed a `Pen`, an `Ink` and a time: `../figure.ts` hands it a `canvas` pen, `../../../scripts/draw.ts` an `svg` or a `raster` pen, `../../../scripts/time.ts` a `raster` pen.
- `../plate.ts` paints a route figure's plate with it; `../../apps/slices/scene.ts` and `../../apps/cuts/scene.ts` draw live on a `canvas` pen and export through an `svg` pen; `../sequences/design.ts` draws its hexes with `hex.draw`.

## PEN

- A `Pen` is a `Board` (`width`, `height`, `frame`, `area`) and ten drawing calls: the nine shapes the Rust `board.rs` draws, and `image`; `raster`, `svg` and `canvas` each build one.
- A `Point` is `[x, y]`, x to the right and y down from the top left; every coordinate, length, radius and `thick`, a stroke's full width, is in pixels, and every angle in radians.
- A `Color` is an rgba 4-tuple of bytes, `[r, g, b, a]`, its alpha straight and 255 opaque; every drawing call but `image` takes one, last.
- `frame(margin)` is the largest centred square `Frame` left after a margin of that fraction of the short side; `area(margin)` is the whole pen inset by that fraction of the short side.
- `rect(x, y, w, h, c)` fills an axis-aligned rectangle; `round_rect(x, y, w, h, r, c)` rounds its corners at radius `r`, clamped between zero and half the short side.
- `disc(cx, cy, r, c)` fills a disc; `ring(cx, cy, r, thick, c)` strokes the circle of radius `r`, the stroke centred on it, and a radius of zero or less is the disc of radius `r + thick / 2`.
- `segment(a, b, thick, c)` strokes a straight run with round caps, a dot when the two points meet; `polyline(pts, thick, c)` strokes a chain of points as one stroke with round caps and joins, blended once where it crosses itself, and needs two points.
- `triangle(a, b, c, color)` fills a triangle; `polygon(pts, c)` fills any polygon by the even-odd rule and needs three points.
- `arc(center, r, angles, thick, c)` strokes the circle of radius `r` from the lower of the two angles clockwise on the screen to the higher, with round caps; a full turn or more is a ring and no turn a dot.
- `image(x, y, w, h, pixels)` paints `Pixels` into the box, nearest sampled, each pixel blended at its own alpha times its own cover.
- `Pixels` is `{ shape, colors, cover? }`: `shape` height then width, `colors` the rgba bytes row by row (`Uint8Array` or `Uint8ClampedArray`), `cover` one coverage a pixel clamped to the unit interval, absent meaning one.
- A `Box` is `[x, y, w, h]`, what `Grid.cell` returns.

## FRAME

- A `Frame` is a rectangle of pen space a figure lays itself out in: `x` and `y`, its top left, and `w` and `h`, all in pixels; `frame(x, y, w, h)` builds one.
- `inset(px)` shrinks it by `px` on every side, a negative `px` growing it; `cell(n)` is the width of one of `n` columns; `at(u, v)` maps unit coordinates, zero at the top left and one at the bottom right, to a `Point`; `center()` is `at(0.5, 0.5)`.
- `square()` is the largest square centred inside it; `radius()` is half its short side, the radius of a centred disc that fills it; `cols(n)` and `rows(n)` split it left to right and top to bottom; `panels(rows, cols, gap)` splits it into `rows` by `cols` tiles, row by row, each inset by `gap` pixels.

## INK

- `ink(palette)` resolves a `Palette`, a `Swatch` for each of the eighteen names, into a frozen `Ink`, and throws when a name is missing or its swatch is no colour.
- A `Swatch` is a hex string, `#rrggbb` or `#rrggbbaa` with the `#` optional, a byte array of at least three, or serde's `{ r, g, b, a? }`; a missing alpha is 255.
- The names, each a `Color` on the `Ink`: the surfaces `ground`, `panel`, `line`, `fg` and `dim`, and the hues `red`, `orange`, `yellow`, `green`, `mint`, `teal`, `cyan`, `blue`, `indigo`, `purple`, `pink`, `brown` and `gray`.
- `inks` is the six inks in their fixed order: `blue`, `orange`, `yellow`, `green`, `pink`, `indigo`.
- `mix(a, b, t)` blends two colours channel by channel, alpha too, `t` clamped to the unit interval and each channel rounded to a byte; `fade(c, alpha)` replaces the alpha with that fraction of opaque, clamped the same way; both are top-level exports and members of the `Ink`.
- `new Ramp(stops, ground?)` is a line through its stops, evenly spaced from zero to one: `at(t)` reads it with `t` clamped, no stop reading the ground, clear by default, and one stop that stop.
- The `Ramp` of an `Ink` is its `Ramps`, the ramp class grounded on the ink's `ground`: `new Ramp(stops)`, `heat()` (ground, blue, yellow, fg), `fire()` (ground, orange, yellow, fg), `diverge()` (blue, ground, orange) and `tone(a, b)`.

## FILES

- `index.ts` re-exports the kit: `canvas`, `svg`, `raster`, `frame`, `ink`, `mix`, `fade`, `Ramp`, `Grid`, the namespaces `field`, `grid`, `hex`, `iso` and `plot`, and their types.
- `frame.ts`: `Point`, `Box`, `Board`, `Pen`, `Frame` and `frame`, `CLEAR`, the frozen clear colour every ground defaults to, and `board(width, height)`, the `Board` each renderer spreads into its pen.
- `ink.ts`: `Color`, `Swatch`, `Name`, `Palette`, `Ramps`, `Ink`, `ink`, `mix`, `fade`, `Ramp`, and `clamp` to the unit interval.
- `raster.ts`: `raster(width, height, ground?)`, the `Raster`, the pixel oracle: plain arithmetic over one `Uint8ClampedArray` flooded with the ground (clear by default, each channel rounded); a shape covers a pixel by `0.5` minus the signed distance from its centre, clamped to the unit interval, and blends over it at the colour's alpha times that cover, a tie rounding away from zero as Rust does; a polyline keeps the strongest cover of its segments and blends once; `image` fills only the whole pixels inside its box; `pixels()` returns `{ shape, colors }`, height then width, the raster's own bytes.
- `svg.ts`: `svg(width, height, ground?)`, the `Svg`, a pen that keeps one element for each call that draws, the ground a full `rect` unless its alpha is zero; `text({ png })` resolves the finished SVG; a colour is `#rrggbb`, plus `fill-opacity` or `stroke-opacity` of alpha / 255 when its alpha is below 255, a polygon fills `evenodd`, every stroke but a ring's has round caps and joins; an `image` keeps a copy of its pixels, cover folded into alpha, drawn `pixelated`, and `text` hands it to the `Png` encoder, then required, which answers the PNG bytes or an `ArrayBuffer` (embedded as a base64 `data:image/png`) or a string taken as the href, maybe as a promise.
- `canvas.ts`: `canvas(ctx, width, height, ground?)`, the live pen over a `CanvasRenderingContext2D` or an `OffscreenCanvasRenderingContext2D`: it clears the box and floods the ground unless its alpha is zero, then turns each call into context calls, colours as css `rgba()`, paths filled `evenodd`, strokes with round caps and joins; `image` draws unsmoothed through one shared `OffscreenCanvas`, resized when the shape changes, the cover folded into alpha.
- `pixels.ts`: `Pixels`, `fold(pixels)`, a copy of the bytes with the cover multiplied into the alpha, which `svg` and `canvas` paint, and `patch(x, y, w, h)`, which throws unless the box is whole pixels, its size not negative: a `Patch` is `Pixels` zero until blended, `blend(px, py, color, cover?)` sets the pixel at board coordinates to that colour and cover, one by default, ignoring one outside the box and replacing an earlier blend, and `paint(pen)` hands the whole patch to `image` at its box, so an untouched pixel leaves the ground alone.
- `field.ts`: scalar fields through a `Ramp`, each painted as one nearest sampled `image` of the frame: `draw(pen, frame, width, height, values, ramp)` normalises the `width` by `height` values, row by row, to their own range, a flat field reading the ramp's low end; `draw_range(pen, frame, width, height, values, span, ramp)` normalises them to `span`; `sample(pen, frame, resolution, f, ramp)` reads `f(u, v)` at the cell centres of a `resolution` by `resolution` grid over the unit square; an empty field, or one with fewer values than cells, draws nothing; it re-exports `patch`.
- `grid.ts`: `new Grid(frame, cols, rows, gap)`, a lattice of at least one by one cells, `gap` the share of a cell's short side left between neighbours; `cell(col, row)` is a cell's `Box`, snapped to whole pixels at a gap of zero or less so neighbours meet without a seam; `fill(pen, col, row, color)` fills one cell, `paint(pen, cells, ink)` each cell whose type byte the `ink` callback maps to a colour, from a `Cell`'s `types` or a `Tensor`'s `data` (`shape` height then width), and `carpet(pen, mask, color)` each true cell of a mask; `LOGO` is the MrlyProd logo, a five by five seed, `mask(rows, level)` grows a 0/1 string mask by Kronecker substitution, level one or less being the seed, and `carpet(pen, frame, mask, gap, color)` lays a `Grid` of the mask's size over the frame and fills its true cells.
- `hex.ts`: the triangle meshes of the hexagon world, every triangle pulled in by `gap` pixels: `draw(pen, frame, { cell, start }, gap, ink)` draws one equilateral triangle per site of a hex slice the `ink` callback maps to a colour, in rows when the slice is wider than tall and in columns when not, `start` setting the parity, fitted and centred in the frame, and throws on a square slice; `hexagon(pen, frame, n, gap, ink)` draws the plain hexagon of side `n` centred in the frame, asking the `ink` callback by row, column and `1` for up or `0` for down; `count(n)` is `6 * n * n`; `row_len(n, row)` is the triangles in a row, counted from the top; `at(slice, side, row, col)` reads a slice's type, its rows centred in its width.
- `iso.ts`: the isometric cube: `project(x, y, z)` with x running right and down, y left and down and z up; a `Quad` is `{ normal, verts }`, an outward face's normal and its four corners in winding order, a `Face` is `{ quad, tone, depth }`, its corners projected before any fit; `faces(quads)` drops the faces turned away, tones the rest `0` top, `1` left and `2` right, and sorts them back to front by the mean of their corners' x + y + z, each taken in f32; `fit(faces, frame)` returns the map that fits them into the frame, centred and keeping their proportions; `draw(pen, frame, quads, shade, edge?)` paints the fitted faces in `shade[tone]`, each ringed by a hairline in `edge` when given; `stamp(pen, quads, cx, cy, s, shade, edge, thick)` paints them unfitted, the origin at `cx, cy` and `s` pixels to the unit, each ringed in `edge` at `thick` when given; `cage(pen, cx, cy, s, half, thick, color)` strokes the twelve `EDGES` of the cube of half side `half` about the same origin, each a pair of corners low to high, corner `i` having x as bit 0, y as bit 1 and z as bit 2.
- `plot.ts`: the plain marks: `bars(pen, frame, values, gap, color)` standing on the foot of the frame, heights in proportion to their magnitudes, the largest filling its height, `gap` the share of a slot left empty; `dots(pen, pts, r, color)`; `rings(pen, center, radii, thick, color)`; `staircase(pen, frame, values, thick, color)`, one tread per value across the frame, and `curve(pen, frame, xs, ys, thick, color)`, y against x, each one polyline with every axis of values mapped from its own range into the frame, y up the screen; `axis(pen, frame, color)`, the bare hairline box with no tick or label; `baseline(pen, frame, color)`, its foot alone; `dashed(pen, frame, y, thick, color)`, forty eight dashes across at `y`, each 0.55 of a step.

## TESTS

- `bun test ./src/lib/view` from `site/` runs the five files.
- `raster.test.ts`: each call's cover on the raster, the ground, the layout of `pixels()`, and the lit area of a disc and of a polyline against their true area.
- `canvas.test.ts`: the context calls each pen call makes, on a recording context, with stand-ins for `OffscreenCanvas` and `ImageData`.
- `svg.test.ts`: the markup each call writes, alpha as opacity, the `png` encoder, an image kept as drawn and its cover folded.
- `kit.test.ts`: ink against the Rust theme's colours and the three kinds of swatch, ramps, `mix`, `fade`, frames, grid, hex, iso, patch, plot and field, on a recording pen or the raster.
- `rust.test.ts`: the sha256 of the raster's bytes against what the Rust kit paints, the shapes of `board.rs` and every kit scene; the numbers the Rust kit gives for `mix`, `fade`, ramps, frames, the board, grid cells, masks, hex counts and `project`; and ink from the themes of `@mrlyprod/mrly/core` against ink from `../theme/`.
