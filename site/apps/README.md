# apps

- An app is a folder `apps/<id>/` and a row of `apps.json`: `id`, `title`, `kind`. The row makes the route `/<id>/`, the menu tile, the search row and the `app-<id>` figure; a `saver` row is also an option of the Settings Saver pick. A name is typed nowhere else.
- `kind: tool` is a built page whose kind is its id, `settings` today. The other kinds, `saver` and `app`, are the `app` kind: one `spa` page, `apps/<id>/index.jsx` its entry, served at `/<id>/index.js` from the kit's one `Bun.build`, so every app shares the demos' React.
- An app is `scene.js`, `widget.jsx` and `index.jsx`; the build stops and names the file when one is missing.
- A scene is `make(canvas, view, opts)` returning `{ draw, every?, font?, size?, theme?, trigger?, hold?, windDown?, cue?, wake?, stop? }`: no React, no DOM beyond its canvas.
- `font` names a face; `run` awaits `document.fonts.load` for it before the first draw, so the still and the first frame are in that face.
- `run(canvas, make, opts)` in `lib/scene.js` drives a scene and returns `stop()`; `stop.scene` is what `make` returned, so a host reaches the scene's own methods; `stop.pause()` halts the loop with `t` frozen and `stop.play()` resumes from that `t`. A paused scene draws only to repaint a resize or a theme.
- `view` is `{ rand, look, still, w, h, dpr, t, wake }`: `rand` the xorshift seeded by `opts.seed`, `look()` the paints (`--accent`, or `--<hue>` when `opts.tint` names one of the Settings hues), `still` true under reduced motion, `w` and `h` the backing store, `t` the milliseconds of play the frame loop counted, at most 100 a frame, `wake()` starts the loop under reduced motion once the scene runs (snake's first turn with `play=me`).
- A scene reads no wall clock: its time is `t`, 0 under reduced motion, so one url paints one still.
- A widget is `({ value, onChange, onReady })`. Matrix, sleep, mandelbrot, julia, lightspeed and zoom take `Scene` from `lib/scene.jsx` as their widget: one full-size canvas running the scene, a fresh canvas for each value; `Scene` hands `onReady` the scene's handle, what `make` returned plus its `canvas`, `pause` and `play`, and `null` when the canvas goes; other props go to the canvas.
- Snake's widget is that `Scene` with a swipe on the canvas through the scene's `turn()`, plus two `Bar`s of its own: the score in the status beside the seed, and Retry in the actions at a game over when `play` is `me`. A knob remounts the canvas, so it starts a new game.
- `index.jsx` is `page(Widget, defaults, { spec, keys, actions, gestures })` from `lib/scene.jsx`, the options being the scene's `PAGE`: it takes the pressed still out, starts from `defaults` plus the spec's defaults, keeps only those keys in the url and writes a random `seed` when it has none, binds the value to the query with `useQuery` and passes every value through `tidy(spec, value)` on mount and on change, writing back what tidy changed.
- The page is a `Frame`: the widget on the stage, which takes touch gestures when `gestures` is set; the right bar the spec's `Knobs`, then a Seed group: the seed as a number field that commits on Enter or blur (`tidy` keeps it a whole number from 0 to 2^32 - 1; junk keeps the seed) and a Reroll button; the status `seed <n>` as text, then `paused` while paused; the hints the first rows of the keys map; the actions the app's primary action (the keys row marked `button`, or a widget's own `primary` button, which leads), Export (`save` of the canvas as `<id>-<seed>`) and Set as screensaver. The subheader holds crumbs and the Zen button, the `top` slot of `Bar`.
- Zen (`z`, the subheader button, Escape or the corner button to leave) writes `data-zen` on `html`: `chrome.css` hides the header, the subheader, the bars, the hints, the actions and the footer, the frame takes the whole screen and the status (seed, paused, score, rec) floats over the canvas at the bottom left. Never persisted; unmount clears it.
- `page(..., { record: true })` adds Export rows Video 9:16 and Video 16:9, the key `v` Record, the status `rec 0:12 9:16 late 0` and a Stop button leading the actions; Space during a take ends it, and a take begun paused plays first. A take is `record()` in `../lib/space/record.js`, driving `stop.fix({ frame, step, after })` (`stop.fix()` with nothing returns to the box, and a reduced-motion scene the take woke goes still again). `run` takes host opts `step` (ms of `t` a frame) and `frame` (`[w, h]` store px), so `step` and `frame` are never knob keys; under a frame `view.fixed` is true.
- The keys map is `keymap(keys)`: Space first, pause and play on every app (the status says `paused`; under reduced motion nothing moves either way), then the app's rows, then `r`, which rerolls the seed through `onChange`, `z`, zen, and `?`, which opens the keys card; an app row on `r` or `?` replaces that base row, and no app binds Space. An app's primary action takes Enter. An app's `actions` map an `act` to a function of the scene's handle and the key event. Keys on a control in the right bar stay with it (a slider, segment or select keeps its arrows and Space), the bar blurs a control only after a pointer changes it, and a kit `Btn` a pointer clicks lets go of focus after its action, so Space and Enter reach the page.
- Set as screensaver fires the window `saver` event with `<id>?<query>`, and `chrome.js` keeps that as the pick; under 40rem it leaves the bottom bar, and Export and every button of class `short` (voyage's Sound and Music, sfx's and techno's Sound and WAV, each an icon and a label) keep their icon alone, so the seed keeps its room.
- The lock (`lib/lock.js`) adds `leave(go)` to the stop, and `enter()` in `chrome.js` calls it before the unlock glide. A scene with `hold()` holds while input comes: a tap, a paging key or any other key calls `hold()`, and 2 s without one releases it; a wheel or a swipe releases it at once. On release, or at once without `hold()`, the lock calls `windDown(onDone)` if the scene has one and glides on its `onDone` or after 1.5 s, whichever is first. Under reduced motion it glides at once.
- A value is one small JSON of twelve keys at most: the seed and, for lightspeed six knobs, for snake nine, for matrix five, for sleep six, for sfx six, for techno seven, for zoom ten, for mandelbrot four, for julia four, for planets six, for voyage seven; its url form is the share link. Its keys are numbers and strings, a switch as 0 and 1, since `stamp()` drops `false`.
- The page is `appPage` in `scripts/site.ts`: the chrome, an empty `#root` naming the entry and the app, then the still: the title, the `app-<id>` figure pair and one `<noscript>` line. Wide, bare, `app-<id>` as og:image.
- Colours come from the custom properties on the canvas: `--art` (with `--ground` behind it) is the ground, `--accent` the ink. `--accent` is the Tint setting, so switching tint recolours a running scene on the window `theme` event; a `tint` key in the value naming a hue reads `--<hue>` instead, on the page, in the lock and in every saver.
- `run` sizes the canvas to its CSS box times `min(devicePixelRatio, 2)`, pauses offscreen and on `document.hidden`, repaints on `theme` and a `prefers-color-scheme` change, and under `prefers-reduced-motion` draws one frame and stops until the scene calls `view.wake()`.
- A canvas keeps its first context, so a 2d scene and a WebGL one never share an element.
- Mandelbrot and julia hand back `blank` when there is no WebGL2 context or the dive does not build.

## SAVERS

- `matrix`: MrlyFont rain in `--accent` or a hue per drop, one glyph of the chosen sets per column per tick, trails erased by the ground at 5% alpha. Only 5 by 5 glyphs enter the pool (the 10 larger specials, `$ ( ) @ [ ] { } © ®`, stay out). It opens as the legacy did: every column a drop at the top, all falling together and filling the screen top-down, then the trickle. Under reduced motion the still is the whole field, each column lit with the trail fading up from the bottom, the same on every draw.
- `sleep`: the DVD bouncer, one mrlytile of `min(w, h) * size` at `speed` px a second over a transparent ground, filled cells in `--accent`, re-rolling its design, its number and its level on every wall where each is mixed.
- `mandelbrot`: `deep` on the set, an endless dive to 1e-30 in its own styling: the classic palette, the ground inside and bands of ground to accent outside, drawn straight to the canvas with no post chain, no HUD and no markers. Knobs: `speed`, `path` 0 (1 draws the planned curve ahead of the camera, a 3 px accent line on a 5 px ground line, with a ring at the centre and at each stop ahead, the next one half filled; px times dpr, so the rings keep their size through the zoom), `palette` '' Classic (seeded, accent, fire, ice, mono), `tint` '' Site (a hue for the accent the classic and accent palettes read).
- `julia`: the same over `{-1.5, 1.5, -1.5, 1.5}` with c from six presets: the seed picks the first and each dive turns to the next.
- `lightspeed`: a WebGL2 starfield drifting toward the eye, and a voyage on the film clock of `../lib/space/hyper.js`: `trigger(onDone, onJump)` winds back, stretches the stars into streaks, piles them with a second star set and a fan of the tint, flashes, and calls `onJump` as the tunnel opens. The tunnel holds until `exit()`: an fbm cylinder in three parallax layers around a hot core, with swirl, flicker, drift, roll and sparse streaks. The exit blooms the core, goes white, calls `onDone` at the white, then decays: the streaks of the next sky (seed + n) shorten over a field going from the pale of the tint through the tint to black, and snap to points. `phase()` is `cruise`, `wind`, `jump`, `hyper` or `exit`; `stage()` names the film phase. On the page Enter jumps and, once under way, exits. A trigger while one plays queues its callbacks for the same voyage; `stop()` fires every one still waiting and lets the context go. `exit()` in the jump blooms at once and in the tunnel waits out its 1.2 s; `windDown(onDone)` never waits, so `onDone` comes within 0.4 s of play from any phase. `hold()` jumps from cruise and keeps a voyage in the tunnel until `exit`. Under reduced motion `trigger` paints the tunnel and calls both at once, and `exit` paints cruise. `hyper` `yes` voyages after 12 s of cruise, `mix` after 6 to 30 s, each holding the tunnel 4 to 10 s, all from its own xorshift of the seed; `no` never, and never under reduced motion. Without WebGL2, or when a shader fails to compile at `make`, the scene is `blank`: black, and every callback fires at once; a tunnel that fails to compile later falls back to `classic`, and a call after `stop()` fires its callbacks at once.
- `snake`: the game played on a centred square of whole cells; head, body and food are mrlytiles in `--accent`, the food clipped round, the snake's ends and turns rounded by `rounds()`. `play` `me` moves every tick from the first frame (under reduced motion from the first turn), turns on `turn(dir)` and holds a game over for `restart()`; `smart` and `silly` pick a turn by `policy.js` each tick and restart a second after a game over. `smart` goes for the nearest food when it can still reach its tail after the eat, counting a food on the way as an eat, else chases its tail; after `size * size` steps without food it goes for the food anyway, and with no way to any the game ends `starved`. In the lock no one plays, so `me` falls back to `smart`. A host passes `player`, `onScore` and `onOver` in `opts`. Under reduced motion it paints the starting board; a `draw` without new time only repaints, so Export never steps the game.
- `zoom`: an endless flight into any 3D mrlybang code: code 0 draws nothing, every other code flies, its mode read on the path flown: corridor, trench, pillars, or skim, the outside dive over a face for the 8 codes with no way in. The flight is `route` of `../lib/space/bang.js`, the picture `march.js`, the rail `trails.js`.
- Zoom's route: a free-space graph over the void cells of one tile, each exit reached by the shortest path with the fewest turns and the most enclosure, a uniform C2 B-spline through the cell centres at 1.15 cells a second x `speed`, flown as `pilot` says; the auto pick commits about 2 cells before its door, and the render frame stays two levels above the camera and steps when it does, so float32 never sees a scale below n^-3; a pure function of seed, pilot and choices, so a jump in `t` lands where play would. `path(tau, span)` samples the camera's own flight ahead, carried into the frame at `tau`, rebases included.
- Zoom's pilots (`PILOTS` in `bang.js`): `steady` the mean of the spline over the last 0.6 s, aimed 1.5 s of arc ahead, banked 0.15 x the lateral acceleration (cap 0.35 rad) plus a slow 0.05 rad roll, eased 20% over 2 cells into a turn; `fighter` a 0.3 s window, aimed 1 s ahead, banked 0.4 x the acceleration 0.5 s either side (cap 0.9 rad), eased 10% over 1 cell, and a full barrel roll over 2.5 s on a straight of 3 cells or more, at most one per 6 cells, alternating sides; `coaster` a 0.1 s window on the rail, aimed 1.2 s ahead, a coordinated bank of 0.6 x the acceleration 0.8 s either side (cap 1.2 rad), no roll, constant speed.
- Zoom's path (`path` 1): the route's own flight 8 s ahead in 32 samples, 0.2 cells below the camera along the route's up, so it reads as the rail the camera rides; `trails.js` draws it as one line in the accent at 0.35 with a pulse that leaves the camera at 6 cells a second every 1.5 s, its tail 1.2 cells, the line fading over its last 2 cells; additive, hidden where the march's hit distance is nearer.
- Zoom's looks, compiled by `#define` in `march.js`: `graphic` a flat palette colour per face axis with dark outlines on the cells of the hit level, faded as they near the pixel size; `studio` (the default) a key light from above-left with soft shadows, a fill at 0.35 and AO; `haze` a headlight on a fast fog, a dim key on a fog a quarter as dense, AO and the next door glowing in `--accent`. Graphic and studio darken with depth from 12 to 30 cells, haze fades into its fog. `hyper` 1 draws lightspeed's tunnel behind any look (`hyper.js` with `floor: 'cloud'`, triggered 4 s before `t` 0 and held). Each look has its own post grade (`GRADES`). The HUD is MrlyFont cells inside the canvas, so PNGs and recordings carry it; the cell is 3 px times dpr, shrunk in whole pixels until the longest line fits between the side margins.
- Zoom's walls: the march sees the 5x5x5 blocks around the render frame (`near` of the route, 125 bits from its chain of children) and, beyond them, every block inside the root kept and filled with the design, so the sponge never ends; outside the root is void. A ray starts where it enters the root's box and steps to the far side of the empty box it is in, so a grazing ray crosses a corridor in one step. Alpha is hard: 0 only for a ray that misses the root or leaves it, 1 on a hit, a ray lost to the look's far distance or one out of steps, so blocks are never see-through and the tunnel shows only through true openings. Past the pixel limit a level's voids narrow from their inner sides to nothing over one level, so detail fades in and out without a pop; a frame's `floor` (a cell size) caps the levels the same way.
- The deep dive (`../lib/space/deep.js`): the centre is BigInt fixed point, one reference orbit runs on the main thread a slice a frame, and the GPU iterates each pixel's float32 delta from it with Zhuoran rebasing; the reference is a minibrot nucleus near the target (ball period, Newton, then two BigInt Newton steps), so it never escapes; the view narrows half an octave a second x `speed`.
- The dive's wayfinder plans ahead: stop k sits at octave k x `STEP` of the dive, and the probe of stop k is the view the camera will see there, issued `LEAD` (2) stops early, read back and scored in 8x8 tiles. The next stop is the boundary pixel within reach whose tile scores best, less a turn penalty `BEND` x (1 - cos) / 2 against the last chord, plus `PACE` x its share of the reach; a dead end (every tile rejected, or a view all interior) heads for the best boundary pixel, the last one seen if none shows, at most a reach away. Past octave 24 each probe also aims for a minibrot within reach (searched from the stop, the next stop and the probe's best tiles, three tiles apart, one a frame, the winner polished) whose framing lands between 1e-30 and 4e-27 (any one in the last 30 octaves); the stops close on it, and at the first stop within 5 octaves of its framing the dive turns it upright at 40% of the short side, holds 2 s and cross-fades 0.8 s into the home view; without one the floor at 1e-30 fades into the next dive.
- The dive's glide: the centre runs one C1 spline through the stops, a cubic Hermite per stop in the frame of the next stop measured in view widths (the short side) at that moment, so the zoom is factored out and the drift on screen has a lateral speed, in widths a second, that is continuous through every stop; a stop's tangent is the chord-weighted mean of its two chord speeds (zero at a reversal or a stop that stays), and a chord is at most `FIT` x `CAP` x `speed`, so the speed stays under `CAP` (0.12 widths a second at speed 1) and the arrival is the only faster move. The plan always holds two stops ahead; a probe the path needs now reads at once, so a jump in `t` lands on the centre that playing reaches, for the same probe results.
- The deep palettes: the smooth count (its log plus a slow linear term, so the colours keep cycling at depth) through a seeded cosine palette (or `accent` black to accent to white, `fire`, `ice`, `mono`) drifting 1/64 a second, dimmed far from the set and lifted on its filaments by the distance estimate, interior black.
- Lightspeed's ground is black in both themes; `--art` never reaches it. `tint` '' is the site accent (`--accent`): `hyper` reads `view.look().accent` once at make, and a hue picks its own paint through the host; the ramp, the streaks, the halos and the fields are all shaded from that one colour, so no Film blue is left. `look` `classic` drops the cloud and keeps white streaks. The flash and the white are exposure through the post chain, never a white fill.
- `planets`: one world in space traced per pixel, its sphere, a shell of air, a ring plane and up to three moons composited front to back over a dim seeded sky (`sky.js`) and through `post.js`. Surface (albedo and ocean mask), relief (normal offset and night lights) and clouds are cube maps baked by one shader from 7-octave gradient fbm with ridges and domain warp on the sphere; a draw only reads them. `planet(gl, view, params)` bakes them all at make; with `{ staged: true }` make only starts the links (KHR_parallel_shader_compile where there is one) and each `ensure()` bakes at most one map, waiting while its link is pending, the last call adding the mips, the ring strip and the draw program; a draw on an unfinished planet finishes it at once. The bake programs are kept per context by map and kind. Oceans glint by GGX times Schlick; clouds turn on a frame of their own and shade the ground at q + 0.02 sun; city lights fade in across the terminator, dimmed under clouds. The air is single scattering, Rayleigh plus Mie (g 0.76), sunlight reaching each sample and the ground through Chapman optical depth, so the limb is blue, the terminator orange and twilight red. Gas giants are bands swaying by latitude (a bounded shear, so storms hold their shape for hours) with a storm and turbulence gathered on the belt edges, darkened toward the limb by mu^k, with no air and no rim; Venus is a cloud deck; the Moon and Mars shade by Lommel-Seeliger. Rings read a baked 1D strip (opacity, dust, tone): the planet's shadow falls on them, theirs on the planet, the unlit face glows where the rings are thin and dust scatters forward; Saturn's faint E ring (`ring.halo`) lights up only against the sun. Moons shadow the planet and fall into its shadow. The sun is a disc 400 times the lit planet, so its glow is the post chain's bloom plus, on a desk, the flare's one blue horizontal streak (none on a phone). `draw(cam, sun, t, k)` scales the whole output by k, so a host can fade the world in. Black in both themes; no WebGL2 gives `blank`.
- `voyage`: a short film grown from the seed, playing on start and looping: leave planet A, jump to lightspeed, fly inside the corridors of the ship's own mrlybang code, exit at planet B, fade to black and roll again. Two worlds, one ship, one clock; no HUD, no picker, no tap. We jump with the ship: it never stretches, the camera holds behind it with the destination a bright point ahead where the streaks converge, and in hyperspace we see the whole sponge, then fly into it, with no cut: one renderer and one camera path from the departure to the corridors. One WebGL2 context: hyper's star field, tunnel and post chain (`floor: 'cloud'`, `far: true`, `idle: 0`, its tint the accent, no audio of its own); the worlds by `planet.js` through hyper's `beneath` (A in cruise, fading out in the pile; B from the decay, dollied by its `approach`); the destination point (3 px times the dpr, planet B's light times 6) through `beneath` with planet A; the ship's body in every act by one `march.js` on the ship's code and `route` (made with `toFrame: route.toFrame`), drawn before `trip.draw` and laid by `march.layer(1)` in hyper's `over`, so its colour is the tunnel's before, during and after the jump: in the bookends and the approach the world camera is taken into the cube's frame (`inside`) and the march sees the cube from outside at its true distance with the route's entry frame (`outside: true`; `hull: 1`, so the look's dim and far count from the hull; `floor` 1/9, three levels of holes, so the hull reads solid; no doors; fog 0; planetshine from the world below, `shine`: its star's light at a quarter times the lit fraction over the squared distance); the march, its resolve and its layer are scissored to the cube's screen box plus 3 px, so the cost follows the ship's size; the history drops whenever `outside` flips, at 14 s and at the white; its nose heat is a `ship.js` glow drawn under the layer, so the hull covers it and it shows round the hull and through holes, and its plume one drawn over it; in the tunnel `trip.draw(aim, null, over, march.scale(), grade)` lays it over the cloud, `aim` the chase's heading carrying the camera's roll alone, so the tunnel shows only through the corridor openings; a black quad fades each loop in and out. Black in both themes; no WebGL2 gives `blank`.
- Voyage's lock: `hold()` from the departure re-times the loop to 9.5 s with a 0.4 s blend of the camera and the ship, so the jump fires 0.4 s later, and pins the tunnel (OUT infinite); in the jump or the tunnel it only pins; during the exit it waits for the arrival; from the arrival or the black it starts the next loop at 9.5 s; it is idempotent. `windDown(onDone)` is hyper's (bloom and white over any act, `onDone` 0.4 s later): before the tunnel opens the loop ends 1.7 s later, in the tunnel it is an early exit with the whole arrival after it, and in the black the veil lifts so the white shows. `phase()` is hyper's. Under reduced motion the still is the departure at 6 s; `hold` does nothing and `windDown` lands at once. Enter (Again) restarts the loop from the departure, winding hyper down when it is busy.
- Voyage's sound: `voyage/score.js` holds the clock and the score. `arrange(L)` maps film ms to the `FILM` sections of `audio.js`: intro to 3.6 s, rise to 6.4 s, hats to 9.9 s (swell rising 0 to 0.4), boost to 12 s (swell 0.5 to 1), cut to 12.45 s, hyper (swell 0.55 to 1 over 10 s) with a 1.9 s breakdown (swell 0.2) centred on the flight's middle, cut from OUT + 0.4 s to OUT + 0.8 s, outro (kick to ARRIVE + 2.6 s, hat to + 3.5 s, the arp to + 6 s, then the pad and sub alone to the end of the fade at L, swell 0.3), then a `cut` through the 1.5 s of black, so the gate seals it silent until the next loop's intro. Each draw sends `audio.at(t, { name, song, arrange })` with `song(seed, loop)` made once a loop (a seeded key, scale and 122 to 130 bpm); the scene books `riser` at 9.6 s `{ dur: 2.4 }` and at OUT `{ dur: 0.4 }`, `hit` at 12 s and at OUT + 0.4 s, each 120 ms ahead, and drops a cue more than 250 ms late, so a catch-up plays nothing old. `sound` is the effects and `music` the score: the widget's bus drops cues while `sound` is 0; the engine is made only inside a gesture (Sound, Music, `m`, Again, a take, or a pointer down when either is 1); a start that throws shows `no sound`; the scene gets a value without `sound` and `music`, so toggling them never restarts the film; a take's audio track is the engine's `stream()`.

## SOUND

- `sfx` and `techno` are `kind: app`: pages only, never savers, so the lock stays silent.
- The engine is `../lib/space/audio.js`. A widget makes one `bus()` and hands it to its scene as `opts.audio`; the first Play, Sound or `m` (a gesture) makes `engine({ seed })`, starts it and attaches it. `set({ sound, music })`: `sound` the effects, `music` the score, the output open while either is 1; techno's Sound sends `music` 0 with it, so it mutes the loop. Every buffer (noise, IR) is made at the context's own rate; `render` keeps its OfflineAudioContext at 44.1 kHz. The scene calls `audio.at(t, mood)` every draw and `audio.cue(name, t, args)` for an effect, both no-ops until attached; a mood is a name or `{ name, flicker, song, arrange }`.
- The clock: `at` maps `t` to context time 50 ms ahead; a drift over 80 ms (a pause, a slow frame, a remount) re-anchors; steps due within 120 ms are booked, steps already past are skipped; a draw with the same `t` books nothing, so a paused scene falls silent; `music` 0 books no step, so a muted score books nothing.
- The graph: drums to the music gate; the acid line through a tanh `drive` shaper into the music, the music through the duck to the gate; the delay (3 sixteenths, feedback 0.45, 2.8 kHz lowpass) returns to the gate, the reverb (3.2 s seeded IR) to the master, so effect tails outlive Stop; gate and sfx to the master; the effects reach the reverb and the delay through sends of their own, so `sound` 0 ramps the sfx bus and both sends to 0 in 0.3 s; a compressor (-10 dB, 12:1, 3 ms, 150 ms), the mute (0.3 s ramps), the speakers and a MediaStream for recording.
- Noise and the IR come from the seed's xorshift, never `Math.random`; a mood lands on the next bar line, at once from silence, and a mood with `arrange` is read at each step's own time instead.
- The film's score: `arrange(ms)` returns `{ name, kick, hat, swell }` for a scene time and the player reads it at each step, so a section lands within one sixteenth of its mark and never early; the sections are the `FILM` parts of `audio.js`: `intro` pad and sub, `rise` adds an arp on every other step, `hats` adds hats, `boost` kick, hats and an arp on every step, `cut` nothing (the gate ramps to 0 in 10 ms at its start and back to 1 at its end, so the tails stop too), `hyper` kick, hats, bass, arp at 0.14, pad and sub, `breakdown` hyper without the kick, `outro` pad, sub and an arp on every other step at 0.1, with kick and hat only while their flags are up. `swell` opens the arp's lowpass to 500 + 3200 swell Hz and the bass's to half that. The voices: `arp` two saws at -7 and +7 cents through a lowpass (Q 2, closing to 0.3 of its cutoff in 0.25 s), `bass` a saw and a half-level square an octave down through two lowpasses (Q 3); the progression pad is `pad` once a bar on the chord and its root an octave up. `pattern` gains `arp` (16 picks of the chord), `skip` and `octave` from a second stream (`seed ^ REEL`), so techno's grid and loop are unchanged.
- `sfx`: the effect is rendered once on an OfflineAudioContext and drawn as a bar waveform over its spectrogram (48 log bands from 40 Hz to 16 kHz, 60 dB); Play cues it at `t` and a playhead sweeps it once, the bars behind it in `--accent`, those ahead at 45%.
- `techno`: a 16-step grid of kick, hat, clap and bass rows in four beats, lit at the step heard (`t` less 50 ms), the bars of the loop as a strip below; a closed hat and an unaccented note draw inset, a slide bridges to the step before. The widget leads with its own Play / Stop; Space pauses the grid and the music; the music loops over `bars`.
- WAV, in the actions, renders the same score offline (`render`, `wav`, `download`) and saves `sfx-<effect>-<seed>.wav` or `techno-<seed>.wav`, the loop of `bars` bars and a 2 s tail.
