# apps

- An app is a folder `apps/<id>/` and a row of `apps.json`: `id`, `title`, `kind`. The row makes the route `/<id>/`, the menu tile, the search row and the `app-<id>` figure; a `saver` row is also an option of the Settings Saver pick. A name is typed nowhere else.
- `kind: tool` is a built page whose kind is its id, `settings` today. The other kinds, `saver` and `app`, are the `app` kind: one `spa` page, `apps/<id>/index.jsx` its entry, served at `/<id>/index.js` from the kit's one `Bun.build`, so every app shares the demos' React.
- An app is `scene.js`, `widget.jsx` and `index.jsx`; the build stops and names the file when one is missing.
- A scene is `make(canvas, view, opts)` returning `{ draw, every?, font?, size?, theme?, trigger?, hold?, windDown?, cue?, wake?, stop? }`: no React, no DOM beyond its canvas.
- `font` names a face; `run` awaits `document.fonts.load` for it before the first draw, so the still and the first frame are in that face.
- `run(canvas, make, opts)` in `lib/scene.js` drives a scene and returns `stop()`; `stop.scene` is what `make` returned, so a host reaches the scene's own methods; `stop.pause()` halts the loop with `t` frozen and `stop.play()` resumes from that `t`. A paused scene draws only to repaint a resize or a theme.
- `view` is `{ rand, look, still, w, h, dpr, t, wake }`: `rand` the xorshift seeded by `opts.seed`, `look()` the paints (`--accent`, or `--<hue>` when `opts.tint` names one of the Settings hues), `still` true under reduced motion, `w` and `h` the backing store, `t` the milliseconds of play the frame loop counted, at most 100 a frame, `wake()` starts the loop under reduced motion once the scene runs (snake's first turn with `play=me`).
- A scene reads no wall clock: its time is `t`, 0 under reduced motion, so one url paints one still.
- A widget is `({ value, onChange, onReady })`. Matrix, sleep, mandelbrot, julia and lightspeed take `Scene` from `lib/scene.jsx` as their widget: one full-size canvas running the scene, a fresh canvas for each value; `Scene` hands `onReady` the scene's handle, what `make` returned plus its `canvas`, `pause` and `play`, and `null` when the canvas goes; other props go to the canvas.
- Snake's widget is that `Scene` with a swipe on the canvas through the scene's `turn()`, plus two `Bar`s of its own: the score in the status beside the seed, and Retry in the actions at a game over when `play` is `me`. A knob remounts the canvas, so it starts a new game.
- `index.jsx` is `page(Widget, defaults, { spec, keys, actions, gestures })` from `lib/scene.jsx`, the options being the scene's `PAGE`: it takes the pressed still out, starts from `defaults` plus the spec's defaults, keeps only those keys in the url and writes a random `seed` when it has none, binds the value to the query with `useQuery` and passes every value through `tidy(spec, value)` on mount and on change, writing back what tidy changed.
- The page is a `Frame`: the widget on the stage, which takes touch gestures when `gestures` is set; the right bar the spec's `Knobs`, then a Seed group: the seed as a number field that commits on Enter or blur (`tidy` keeps it a whole number from 0 to 2^32 - 1; junk keeps the seed) and a Reroll button; the status `seed <n>` as text, then `paused` while paused; the hints the first rows of the keys map; the actions the app's primary action (the keys row marked `button`, or a widget's own `primary` button, which leads), Export (`save` of the canvas as `<id>-<seed>`) and Set as screensaver. The subheader holds crumbs and the Zen button, the `top` slot of `Bar`.
- Zen (`z`, the subheader button, Escape or the corner button to leave) writes `data-zen` on `html`: `chrome.css` hides the header, the subheader, the bars, the hints, the actions and the footer, the frame takes the whole screen and the status (seed, paused, score, rec) floats over the canvas at the bottom left. Never persisted; unmount clears it.
- `page(..., { record: true })` adds Export rows Video 9:16 and Video 16:9, the key `v` Record, the status `rec 0:12 9:16 late 0` and a Stop button leading the actions; Space during a take ends it, and a take begun paused plays first. A take is `record()` in `../lib/space/record.js`, driving `stop.fix({ frame, step, after })` (`stop.fix()` with nothing returns to the box, and a reduced-motion scene the take woke goes still again). `run` takes host opts `step` (ms of `t` a frame) and `frame` (`[w, h]` store px), so `step` and `frame` are never knob keys; under a frame `view.fixed` is true.
- The keys map is `keymap(keys)`: Space first, pause and play on every app (the status says `paused`; under reduced motion nothing moves either way), then the app's rows, then `r`, which rerolls the seed through `onChange`, `z`, zen, and `?`, which opens the keys card; an app row on `r` or `?` replaces that base row, and no app binds Space. An app's primary action takes Enter. An app's `actions` map an `act` to a function of the scene's handle and the key event. Keys on a control in the right bar stay with it (a slider, segment or select keeps its arrows and Space), the bar blurs a control only after a pointer changes it, and a kit `Btn` a pointer clicks lets go of focus after its action, so Space and Enter reach the page.
- Set as screensaver fires the window `saver` event with `<id>?<query>`, and `chrome.js` keeps that as the pick; under 40rem it leaves the bottom bar, and Export and every button of class `short` (voyage's Sound and Music, sfx's and techno's Sound and WAV, each an icon and a label) keep their icon alone, so the seed keeps its room.
- The lock (`lib/lock.js`) adds `leave(go)` to the stop, and `enter()` in `chrome.js` calls it before the unlock glide. A scene with `hold()` holds while input comes: a tap, a paging key or any other key calls `hold()`, and 2 s without one releases it; a wheel or a swipe releases it at once. On release, or at once without `hold()`, the lock calls `windDown(onDone)` if the scene has one and glides on its `onDone` or after 1.5 s, whichever is first. Under reduced motion it glides at once.
- A value is one small JSON of ten keys at most: the seed and, for lightspeed seven knobs, for snake nine, for matrix five, for sleep six, for sfx six, for techno seven, for zoom six, for planets six, for voyage eight; its url form is the share link. Its keys are numbers and strings, a switch as 0 and 1, since `stamp()` drops `false`.
- The page is `appPage` in `scripts/site.ts`: the chrome, an empty `#root` naming the entry and the app, then the still: the title, the `app-<id>` figure pair and one `<noscript>` line. Wide, bare, `app-<id>` as og:image.
- Colours come from the custom properties on the canvas: `--art` (with `--ground` behind it) is the ground, `--accent` the ink. `--accent` is the Tint setting, so switching tint recolours a running scene on the window `theme` event; a `tint` key in the value naming a hue reads `--<hue>` instead, on the page, in the lock and in every saver.
- `run` sizes the canvas to its CSS box times `min(devicePixelRatio, 2)`, pauses offscreen and on `document.hidden`, repaints on `theme` and a `prefers-color-scheme` change, and under `prefers-reduced-motion` draws one frame and stops until the scene calls `view.wake()`.
- A canvas keeps its first context, so a 2d scene and a WebGL one never share an element.
- A fractal that gets a context but no shader program still hands back a `stop()` that calls `WEBGL_lose_context`.

## SAVERS

- `matrix`: MrlyFont rain in `--accent` or a hue per drop, one glyph of the chosen sets per column per tick, trails erased by the ground at 5% alpha. Only 5 by 5 glyphs enter the pool (the 10 larger specials, `$ ( ) @ [ ] { } © ®`, stay out). It opens as the legacy did: every column a drop at the top, all falling together and filling the screen top-down, then the trickle. Under reduced motion the still is the whole field, each column lit with the trail fading up from the bottom, the same on every draw.
- `sleep`: the DVD bouncer, one mrlytile of `min(w, h) * size` at `speed` px a second over a transparent ground, filled cells in `--accent`, re-rolling its design, its number and its level on every wall where each is mixed.
- `mandelbrot`: the set under `{-2, 1, -1.5, 1.5}`, zooming a whole cycle into a boundary point the wayfinder found.
- `julia`: the same engine over `{-1.5, 1.5, -1.5, 1.5}` with one of six c presets the seed picks.
- `lightspeed`: a WebGL2 starfield drifting toward the eye, and a voyage on the film clock of `../lib/space/hyper.js`: `trigger(onDone, onJump)` winds back, stretches the stars into streaks, piles them with a second star set and a blue fan, flashes, and calls `onJump` as the tunnel opens. The tunnel holds until `exit()`: an fbm cylinder in three parallax layers around a hot core, with swirl, flicker, drift, roll and sparse streaks. The exit blooms the core, goes white, calls `onDone` at the white, then decays: the streaks of the next sky (seed + n) shorten over a field going pale blue to navy to black, and snap to points. `phase()` is `cruise`, `wind`, `jump`, `hyper` or `exit`; `stage()` names the film phase. On the page Enter jumps and, once under way, exits. A trigger while one plays queues its callbacks for the same voyage; `stop()` fires every one still waiting and lets the context go. `exit()` in the jump blooms at once and in the tunnel waits out its 1.2 s; `windDown(onDone)` never waits, so `onDone` comes within 0.4 s of play from any phase. `hold()` jumps from cruise and keeps a voyage in the tunnel until `exit`. Under reduced motion `trigger` paints the tunnel and calls both at once, and `exit` paints cruise. `hyper` `yes` voyages after 12 s of cruise, `mix` after 6 to 30 s, each holding the tunnel 4 to 10 s, all from its own xorshift of the seed; `no` never, and never under reduced motion. Without WebGL2, or when a shader fails to compile at `make`, the scene is `blank`: black, and every callback fires at once; a tunnel that fails to compile later falls back to `classic`, and a call after `stop()` fires its callbacks at once.
- `snake`: the game played on a centred square of whole cells; head, body and food are mrlytiles in `--accent`, the food clipped round, the snake's ends and turns rounded by `rounds()`. `play` `me` moves every tick from the first frame (under reduced motion from the first turn), turns on `turn(dir)` and holds a game over for `restart()`; `smart` and `silly` pick a turn by `policy.js` each tick and restart a second after a game over. `smart` goes for the nearest food when it can still reach its tail after the eat, counting a food on the way as an eat, else chases its tail; after `size * size` steps without food it goes for the food anyway, and with no way to any the game ends `starved`. In the lock no one plays, so `me` falls back to `smart`. A host passes `player`, `onScore` and `onOver` in `opts`. Under reduced motion it paints the starting board; a `draw` without new time only repaints, so Export never steps the game.
- `zoom`: an endless flight; `kind` bang flies into a 3D mrlybang code, `set` and `julia` dive into the Mandelbrot set or a seeded Julia set by perturbation down to 1e-30 and end on a minibrot.
- Zoom's route rides the void cells of one level, enters a kept cell through a face-centre door and flies on inside it at 1/n the scale; the render frame stays two levels above the camera and every point is re-expressed as `n (p - cell)` on entry, so float32 never sees a scale below n^-3.
- Zoom's markers: rings numbered 1 to 4 on the doors of the next kept cell, the auto pick filled, a door hidden behind a wall drawn faint; a tap within 44 CSS px, a digit or Enter chooses for that cell, so the corridor never jumps; with `auto` 0 the flight slows over its last 0.6 s and holds 0.45 s before each door until a pick; a tap hits within 44 CSS px on a fixed frame too, by the store px per CSS px `fit` returns.
- Zoom's look: black ground in both themes, walls by the palette per face, a headlight on a fast fog, a dim key light on a fog a quarter as dense so far rooms read lit, a ray that leaves the 3x3x3 block of render cubes shaded as the block face it exits through, in a thin haze, AO, the next door glowing in `--accent`; a code the gate refuses orbits the design from outside with `NO WAY IN`. The HUD (name, n, level, pick) is MrlyFont cells inside the canvas, so PNGs and recordings carry it.
- Zoom's dive (`set`, `julia`): the centre is BigInt fixed point, one reference orbit runs on the main thread a slice a frame, and the GPU iterates each pixel's float32 delta from it with Zhuoran rebasing; the reference is a minibrot nucleus near the target (ball period, Newton, then two BigInt Newton steps), so it never escapes; the view narrows half an octave a second x `speed`.
- Zoom's wayfinder: at every octave a probe of the view is read back and scored in 8x8 tiles; the best tiles, three tiles apart, become rings on detail and `auto` glides to the lit one over 1.5 s; a dead end (every tile rejected, or a view all interior) steers to the best boundary pixel, the last one seen if none shows, even with `auto` 0. Past octave 24 the dive commits to a minibrot whose framing lands between 1e-30 and 4e-27 (any one in the last 30 octaves), turns it upright at 40% of the short side, holds 2 s and cross-fades 0.8 s into the home view; without one the floor at 1e-30 fades into the next dive. Julia's c is seeded per dive inside a bulb of period 2 or more. The aim tests one candidate a frame and polishes only the winner; a jump in `t` lands at the same depth but not on the same path, since the probe reads the current reference.
- Zoom's 2D look: the smooth count (its log plus a slow linear term, so the colours keep cycling at depth) through a seeded cosine palette (or `accent` black to accent to white, `fire`, `ice`, `mono`) drifting 1/64 a second, dimmed far from the set and lifted on its filaments by the distance estimate, interior black; the HUD reads the kind, the depth as a power of ten and the pick.
- Lightspeed's totem: a `totem` of 0 to 255 rides that mrlybang code (n 3, level 3) as a small solid at (0, -0.35, 1.6) in camera space, in the lower third of a wide frame, turning by the seed; `bang.js` `totem()` raymarches it inside its scissored screen box, over the sky and under the post chain, through hyper's `over`. `ride()` lights it by a fixed sun in cruise, the tunnel blues of hyper's `flow()` in the tunnel and a pale flash at the flash and the white, and pulls its front half along the axis by 1 + 0.6 (e^(11 x) - 1), x 0 to 1 over the stretch and pile (0.6 and 0.4), snapping back under the flash and shrinking back over the decay, with a plume of 14 gaussians behind it. -1 or an empty code builds nothing, and the scene imports `bang.js` only for a totem of 0 or more, so the lock and a page without one load no bang code; `ready` resolves once the totem is built.
- Lightspeed's ground is black in both themes; `--art` and `--accent` never reach it. `tint` '' is the Film blues (#0c1869, #2d62d5, #b3e1f3); a hue tints the ramp, the streaks and the fields from that hue. `look` `classic` drops the cloud and keeps white streaks. The flash and the white are exposure through the post chain, never a white fill.
- `planets`: one world in space traced per pixel, its sphere, a shell of air, a ring plane and up to three moons composited front to back over a dim seeded sky (`sky.js`) and through `post.js`. Surface (albedo and ocean mask), relief (normal offset and night lights) and clouds are cube maps baked by one shader from 7-octave gradient fbm with ridges and domain warp on the sphere; a draw only reads them. `planet(gl, view, params)` bakes them all at make; with `{ staged: true }` make only starts the links (KHR_parallel_shader_compile where there is one) and each `ensure()` bakes at most one map, waiting while its link is pending, the last call adding the mips, the ring strip and the draw program; a draw on an unfinished planet finishes it at once. The bake programs are kept per context by map and kind. Oceans glint by GGX times Schlick; clouds turn on a frame of their own and shade the ground at q + 0.02 sun; city lights fade in across the terminator, dimmed under clouds. The air is single scattering, Rayleigh plus Mie (g 0.76), sunlight reaching each sample and the ground through Chapman optical depth, so the limb is blue, the terminator orange and twilight red. Gas giants are bands swaying by latitude (a bounded shear, so storms hold their shape for hours) with a storm and turbulence gathered on the belt edges, darkened toward the limb by mu^k, with no air and no rim; Venus is a cloud deck; the Moon and Mars shade by Lommel-Seeliger. Rings read a baked 1D strip (opacity, dust, tone): the planet's shadow falls on them, theirs on the planet, the unlit face glows where the rings are thin and dust scatters forward; Saturn's faint E ring (`ring.halo`) lights up only against the sun. Moons shadow the planet and fall into its shadow. The sun is a disc 400 times the lit planet, so its glow is the post chain's bloom plus, on a desk, the flare's one blue horizontal streak (none on a phone). `draw(cam, sun, t, k)` scales the whole output by k, so a host can fade the world in. Black in both themes; no WebGL2 gives `blank`.
- `voyage`: a star system grown from the seed (`voyage/system.js`) and flown on hyper's film clock: arrive in orbit, stay with the picker live, charge (the camera swings clear of the planet and turns to the target over 1.2 s), jump, hold the tunnel, exit with the destination drawn by hyper's `beneath` from the decay, snap, arrive. One WebGL2 context: hyper's star field and post chain, every other world a phase-lit disc at its direction from here, every bang world a small turning cube, star-lit with glowing accent edges, the world in orbit by `planet.js` with the star as its HDR sun, a bang world by `bang.js` (`voyage/cube.js`), the HUD by `hud.js`. A `totem` of 0 to 255 rides as lightspeed's does, through hyper's `over`, lit by the system's star (its colour times 1.5, ambient 0.035) in cruise; -1 builds nothing. `bang.js` is in voyage's static closure anyway for the bang worlds, so the totem adds no chunk. Black in both themes; no WebGL2 gives `blank`.
- Voyage's picker: rings numbered by orbit, then the bang worlds, 1 to 9 by digit and the rest by the arrows or the list, the names beside them, spread apart where they crowd, held inside `safe(view)` and below the HUD lines (those off screen at its edge, faint), pushed with their names out of the sun's glare (0.15 rad), those behind the planet faint, the pick filled. A tap within 44 CSS px, the right bar's Destinations, the arrows or a digit picks; Enter, a second tap or a second click jumps. Markers return 4 s after arrival; `hud` 0 hides text and markers. Inside a bang world the markers are the route's doors, numbered 1 to 4 with the auto pick filled: a digit or a tap picks a door, while the list and the arrows still pick a world and Enter jumps out. The HUD reads `SYSTEM <STAR>`, `<WORLD> <KIND>` (`<NAME> N 3 LEVEL 4` inside a bang world) and `PICK A DESTINATION`, `<AU> AU TO <WORLD>` or `DEEP SPACE TO <WORLD>` when either end is a bang world, `HYPERSPACE ETA 3.1` or, on arrival, `ORBIT <AU> AU FROM <STAR>` or `FAR BEYOND <STAR>`.
- Voyage's lock: `hold()` from a stay jumps to the pick after a 0.4 s charge and pins the tunnel; `windDown(onDone)` exits at once from any state, `onDone` at the white 0.4 s later, and from any state before the tunnel opens the exit lands back on the same world; `phase()` is hyper's. Under reduced motion the still is the arrival 9 s in (a bang world's frame cube from outside), a jump lands at once and windDown fires at once; both work from inside a bang world. `mode` `auto` is the saver, and so is any `make` without `opts.player` (the widget passes `player: true`, the lock and the Settings pick do not): a pure function of the seed and `t`, every missed mark (jump, exit, arrival) caught up in order inside one draw. A pick from the list in auto replaces the planned jump.
- Voyage's sound: the scene holds a bus, hands it to hyper (riser, hit, drone, swell, white hit) and cues a tick on a pick and the arrival pad at the landing. Each draw sends the engine the mood of the voyage 120 ms ahead with a seeded song (`voyage/score.js`: the seed, a seeded key and scale, 128 bpm): stay `drift`, the first 8 bars after a planet's landing `land`, a bang world `acid`, charge and jump `build`, tunnel `drive`, exit to landing `drop`; the engine lands each on the next bar line and plays each cue at its own t. Auto charges so the tunnel opens on a bar line, and every exit (bloom) is put on the nearest bar line, so the drop's silence starts with the bloom and the white hit lands 0.4 s into it. `sound` is the effects and `music` the score, passed to the engine as they are: the widget's bus drops cues while `sound` is 0, the engine ramps a playing effect out in 0.3 s, and its output is open while either is 1. The widget makes the engine only inside a gesture (Sound, Music, `m`, a take, or the first tap, list click or Enter when either is 1; a value change only sets an engine already made); a start that throws shows `no sound` in the status and the gesture still acts. It gives the scene a value without `sound` and `music`, so toggling them never restarts the voyage; a take's audio track is the engine's `stream()`, which carries both.

## SOUND

- `sfx` and `techno` are `kind: app`: pages only, never savers, so the lock stays silent.
- The engine is `../lib/space/audio.js`. A widget makes one `bus()` and hands it to its scene as `opts.audio`; the first Play, Sound or `m` (a gesture) makes `engine({ seed })`, starts it and attaches it. `set({ sound, music })`: `sound` the effects, `music` the score, the output open while either is 1; techno's Sound sends `music` 0 with it, so it mutes the loop. Every buffer (noise, IR) is made at the context's own rate; `render` keeps its OfflineAudioContext at 44.1 kHz. The scene calls `audio.at(t, mood)` every draw and `audio.cue(name, t, args)` for an effect, both no-ops until attached.
- The clock: `at` maps `t` to context time 50 ms ahead; a drift over 80 ms (a pause, a slow frame, a remount) re-anchors; steps due within 120 ms are booked, steps already past are skipped; a draw with the same `t` books nothing, so a paused scene falls silent; `music` 0 books no step, so a muted score books nothing.
- The graph: drums to the music gate; the acid line through a tanh `drive` shaper into the music, the music through the duck to the gate; the delay (3 sixteenths, feedback 0.45, 2.8 kHz lowpass) returns to the gate, the reverb (3.2 s seeded IR) to the master, so effect tails outlive Stop; gate and sfx to the master; the effects reach the reverb and the delay through sends of their own, so `sound` 0 ramps the sfx bus and both sends to 0 in 0.3 s; a compressor (-10 dB, 12:1, 3 ms, 150 ms), the mute (0.3 s ramps), the speakers and a MediaStream for recording.
- Noise and the IR come from the seed's xorshift, never `Math.random`; a mood lands on the next bar line, at once from silence.
- `sfx`: the effect is rendered once on an OfflineAudioContext and drawn as a bar waveform over its spectrogram (48 log bands from 40 Hz to 16 kHz, 60 dB); Play cues it at `t` and a playhead sweeps it once, the bars behind it in `--accent`, those ahead at 45%.
- `techno`: a 16-step grid of kick, hat, clap and bass rows in four beats, lit at the step heard (`t` less 50 ms), the bars of the loop as a strip below; a closed hat and an unaccented note draw inset, a slide bridges to the step before. The widget leads with its own Play / Stop; Space pauses the grid and the music; the music loops over `bars`.
- WAV, in the actions, renders the same score offline (`render`, `wav`, `download`) and saves `sfx-<effect>-<seed>.wav` or `techno-<seed>.wav`, the loop of `bars` bars and a 2 s tail.

## CONSTANTS

- Matrix's spec: Glyphs: `set` a list of `l` letters A-Z, `d` digits, `s` specials (MrlyFont's other 5 by 5 glyphs in `kit/font/font.json`, 35 of 45, no lowercase), default `lds`; Colour: `color` `tint` or `rainbow` (a hue per drop, rolled from the seed as each drop starts), `tint`; Motion: `tick` "Tick" 33 ms a step (16 to 120); Grid: `cell` "Size" 20 px (8 to 48, even). Columns are `floor(w / cell)` whole device-pixel cells centred by `board()`; the first drops all start at row 1 and fall together, a column added by a resize starts at a seeded row, and a drop resets past the floor when the roll clears 0.975.
- Sleep's spec: Shape: `design`, `number` (3 5 7 9) and `level` (1 2), each `''` Mixed (rolled per wall); Motion: `speed` 240 px a second (60 to 600, by 20) on the 45-degree diagonal; Size: `size` 0.15 of the smaller axis (0.08 to 0.4), the side snapped to whole device pixels a tile cell; Look: `tint`.
- Mrlytiles: `carpet` is on where at most one coordinate is odd, `net` where at least one is, `htree` on the even rows, `vtree` on the even columns, `void` where the two share a parity; level 2 is the level-1 grid Kroneckered with itself.
- Fractal: cycle 65536ms, fade 2048ms, zoom `pow(128, t)`, rotation `1/2048` a frame in a random direction, colour `1/256` a frame, start scale `1/2`, escape radius 128, `maxIter = 100 + floor(50 * log2(zoom))` under a hard cap of 1000.
- Every cycle re-rolls the target, the spin direction and the starting rotation; `stop()` clears the inline opacity the fade wrote.
- Fractal colour: inside is the ground, outside is `mix(ground, accent, 0.5 + 0.5 * cos(3.0 + sl * 0.15 + t))` over the smooth count `sl`.
- Wayfinder: 200 random points at 150 iterations a cycle, keeping the highest escape count that still escapes, which is the boundary.
- Lightspeed's spec, the value's keys: Jump: `speed` "Speed" 1 (0.5 to 2 by 0.1, streak length and tunnel flow), `idle` "Cruise" 0.015 (0 to 0.1 by 0.005); Sky: `stars` "Density" 1 (0.25 to 3 by 0.25), `tint` '' (Film) or a hue, `look` `cloud` or `classic`; Saver: `hyper` `no`, `yes` or `mix`, default `mix`; Look: `totem` "Totem" -1 (-1 none, 0 to 255 a mrlybang code).
- Lightspeed timings (`FILM`, ms): wind 300, stretch 900, pile 600, flash 300, tunnel at least 1200, bloom 400, white 200, decay 900, snap 200. The old host keys, read from `opts` but kept out of the url, map onto it: `wind` the wind, `run` stretch and pile (0.6 and 0.4), `punch` the flash, `snap` the bloom, `flash` the white, `fade` decay and snap (9 to 2); a number that is missing, empty, negative or not a number takes its default.
- Lightspeed: 6000 stars on a desk, 2500 on a phone, times `stars`; the tunnel renders at 0.75 of the store on a desk and 0.5 on a phone or a 4K store, and the governor lowers it a notch at a time, down to `classic`; a fixed frame (a take, a film sheet) pins the first notch. `onJump` at the tunnel (2.1 s), `onDone` at the white (0.4 s after the exit begins).
- Julia presets: `-0.4+0.6i`, `-0.8+0.156i`, `0.285+0.01i`, `-0.7269+0.1889i`, `-0.1+0.651i`, `0.355+0.355i`.
- Snake's spec: Board: `size` 16 (8 to 32, even), `wrap` 1, `apples` 1 (1 to 10); Pace: `tick` "Tick" 150 ms a step (50 to 500, by 25); Play: `play` `me`, `smart` or `silly`; Pieces: `head`, `body` and `food` `''` Random (a design each, rolled per game from the seed); Look: `tint`. Each part also rolls its number, 3, 5, 7 or 9, per game. Keys: Space pauses the game and the player with it, arrows and WASD turn, `r` a new game (a new seed), `?` the card. Board 92% of the short side by `board()`, ground `--accent` at 7%, the pieces at 35% during a game over, hold 1 s.

The gate: fill 0.4 to 0.9, at least 2.2 kept face-neighbours per void cell on average, one face-connected piece, a door table left; connectivity cuts the prototype's 100 and 52 passing codes to 61 at n 3 and 32 at n 5, pruning cuts none; at n 5 none has bit 0 set, since n 5 puts every face centre in class 0.
