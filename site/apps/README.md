# apps

- An app is a folder `apps/<id>/` and a row of `apps.json`: `id`, `title`, `kind`. The row makes the route `/<id>/`, the menu tile, the search row and the `app-<id>` figure; a `saver` row is also an option of the Settings Saver pick. A name is typed nowhere else.
- `kind: tool` is a built page whose kind is its id, `settings` today. Any other kind is the `app` kind: one `spa` page, `apps/<id>/index.jsx` its entry, served at `/<id>/index.js` from the kit's one `Bun.build`, so every app shares the demos' React.
- An app is `scene.js`, `widget.jsx` and `index.jsx`; the build stops and names the file when one is missing.
- A scene is `make(canvas, view, opts)` returning `{ draw, every?, font?, size?, theme?, trigger?, hold?, windDown?, stop? }`: no React, no DOM beyond its canvas.
- `font` names a face; `run` awaits `document.fonts.load` for it before the first draw, so the still and the first frame are in that face.
- `run(canvas, make, opts)` in `lib/scene.js` drives a scene and returns `stop()`; `stop.scene` is what `make` returned, so a host reaches the scene's own methods; `stop.pause()` halts the loop with `t` frozen and `stop.play()` resumes from that `t`. A paused scene draws only to repaint a resize or a theme.
- `view` is `{ rand, look, still, w, h, dpr, t, wake }`: `rand` the xorshift seeded by `opts.seed`, `look()` the paints (`--accent`, or `--<hue>` when `opts.tint` names one of the Settings hues), `still` true under reduced motion, `w` and `h` the backing store, `t` the milliseconds of play the frame loop counted, at most 100 a frame, `wake()` starts the loop under reduced motion once the scene runs (snake's first turn with `play=me`).
- A scene reads no wall clock: its time is `t`, 0 under reduced motion, so one url paints one still.
- A widget is `({ value, onChange, onReady })`. Matrix, sleep, mandelbrot, julia and lightspeed take `Scene` from `lib/scene.jsx` as their widget: one full-size canvas running the scene, a fresh canvas for each value; `Scene` hands `onReady` the scene's handle, what `make` returned plus its `canvas`, `pause` and `play`, and `null` when the canvas goes; other props go to the canvas.
- Snake's widget is that `Scene` with a swipe on the canvas through the scene's `turn()`, plus two `Bar`s of its own: the score in the status beside the seed, and Retry in the actions at a game over when `play` is `me`. A knob remounts the canvas, so it starts a new game.
- `index.jsx` is `page(Widget, defaults, { spec, keys, actions, gestures })` from `lib/scene.jsx`, the options being the scene's `PAGE`: it takes the pressed still out, starts from `defaults` plus the spec's defaults, keeps only those keys in the url and writes a random `seed` when it has none, binds the value to the query with `useQuery` and passes every value through `tidy(spec, value)` on mount and on change, writing back what tidy changed.
- The page is a `Frame`: the widget on the stage, which takes touch gestures when `gestures` is set; the right bar the spec's `Knobs`, then a Seed group: the seed as a number field that commits on Enter or blur (`tidy` keeps it a whole number from 0 to 2^32 - 1; junk keeps the seed) and a Reroll button; the status `seed <n>` as text, then `paused` while paused; the hints the first rows of the keys map; the actions the app's primary action (the keys row marked `button`, or a widget's own `primary` button, which leads), Export (`save` of the canvas as `<id>-<seed>`) and Set as screensaver. The subheader holds crumbs only.
- The keys map is `keymap(keys)`: Space first, pause and play on every app (the status says `paused`; under reduced motion nothing moves either way), then the app's rows, then `r`, which rerolls the seed through `onChange`, and `?`, which opens the keys card; an app row on `r` or `?` replaces that base row, and no app binds Space. An app's primary action takes Enter. An app's `actions` map an `act` to a function of the scene's handle and the key event. Keys on a control in the right bar stay with it (a slider, segment or select keeps its arrows and Space), the bar blurs a control only after a pointer changes it, and a kit `Btn` a pointer clicks lets go of focus after its action, so Space and Enter reach the page.
- Set as screensaver fires the window `saver` event with `<id>?<query>`, and `chrome.js` keeps that as the pick; under 40rem it leaves the bottom bar and Export keeps its icon alone.
- The lock (`lib/lock.js`) adds `leave(go)` to the stop, and `enter()` in `chrome.js` calls it before the unlock glide. A scene with `hold()` holds while input comes: a tap, a paging key or any other key calls `hold()`, and 2 s without one releases it; a wheel or a swipe releases it at once. On release, or at once without `hold()`, the lock calls `windDown(onDone)` if the scene has one and glides on its `onDone` or after 1.5 s, whichever is first. Under reduced motion it glides at once.
- A value is one small JSON of ten keys at most: the seed and, for lightspeed five knobs, for snake nine, for matrix five, for sleep six; its url form is the share link. Its keys are numbers and strings, a switch as 0 and 1, since `stamp()` drops `false`.
- The page is `appPage` in `scripts/site.ts`: the chrome, an empty `#root` naming the entry and the app, then the still: the title, the `app-<id>` figure pair and one `<noscript>` line. Wide, bare, `app-<id>` as og:image.
- Colours come from the custom properties on the canvas: `--art` (with `--ground` behind it) is the ground, `--accent` the ink. `--accent` is the Tint setting, so switching tint recolours a running scene on the window `theme` event; a `tint` key in the value naming a hue reads `--<hue>` instead, on the page, in the lock and in every saver.
- `run` sizes the canvas to its CSS box times `min(devicePixelRatio, 2)`, pauses offscreen and on `document.hidden`, repaints on `theme` and a `prefers-color-scheme` change, and under `prefers-reduced-motion` draws one frame and stops until the scene calls `view.wake()`.
- A canvas keeps its first context, so a 2d scene and a WebGL one never share an element.
- A fractal that gets a context but no shader program still hands back a `stop()` that calls `WEBGL_lose_context`.

## SAVERS

- `matrix`: MrlyFont rain in `--accent` or a hue per drop, one glyph of the chosen sets per column per tick, trails erased by the ground at 5% alpha. Only 5 by 5 glyphs enter the pool (the 10 larger specials, `$ ( ) @ [ ] { } © ®`, stay out). It opens as the legacy did: every column a drop at the top, all falling together and filling the screen top-down, then the trickle. Under reduced motion the still is the whole field, each column lit with the trail fading up from the bottom, the same on every draw.
- `sleep`: the DVD bouncer, one mrlytile of `min(w, h) * size` at `speed` px a second over a transparent ground, filled cells in `--accent`, re-rolling its design, its number and its level on every wall where each is mixed.
- `mandelbrot`: the set under `{-2, 1, -1.5, 1.5}`, zooming a whole cycle into a boundary point the wayfinder found.
- `julia`: the same engine over `{-1.5, 1.5, -1.5, 1.5}` with one of six c presets the seed picks.
- `lightspeed`: a starfield drifting toward the eye, and a voyage: `trigger(onDone, onJump)` winds back, jumps to top speed with a bloom, and calls `onJump` as hyperspace is reached. Hyperspace holds until `exit()`: long streaks converging on a vanishing point that drifts and rolls, over a glowing ground and a square tunnel of rings. The exit blooms to a flash, calls `onDone` at it, then holds the flash and fades it out through a shrinking bloom to cruise. `phase()` is `cruise`, `wind`, `jump`, `hyper` or `exit`. On the page Enter jumps and, once under way, exits. A trigger while one plays queues its callbacks for the same voyage; `stop()` fires every one still waiting. `windDown(onDone)` is `exit`; from cruise it runs the exit alone, so `onDone` comes within 0.5 s of play. `hold()` jumps from cruise and keeps a voyage in hyperspace until `exit`. Under reduced motion `trigger` paints hyperspace and calls both at once, and `exit` paints cruise. `hyper` `yes` voyages after 12 s of cruise, `mix` after 6 to 30 s, each holding hyperspace 4 to 10 s, all from its own xorshift of the seed; `no` never, and never under reduced motion.
- `snake`: the game played on a centred square of whole cells; head, body and food are mrlytiles in `--accent`, the food clipped round, the snake's ends and turns rounded by `rounds()`. `play` `me` moves every tick from the first frame (under reduced motion from the first turn), turns on `turn(dir)` and holds a game over for `restart()`; `smart` and `silly` pick a turn by `policy.js` each tick and restart a second after a game over. `smart` goes for the nearest food when it can still reach its tail after the eat, counting a food on the way as an eat, else chases its tail; after `size * size` steps without food it goes for the food anyway, and with no way to any the game ends `starved`. In the lock no one plays, so `me` falls back to `smart`. A host passes `player`, `onScore` and `onOver` in `opts`. Under reduced motion it paints the starting board; a `draw` without new time only repaints, so Export never steps the game.
- Lightspeed's ground is the paper and its ink the opposite of the paper from the palette; halos are `--accent` and `--accent` lifted 30% toward the ink. On a dark paper the streaks add (`lighter`), on a light one they multiply.

## CONSTANTS

- Matrix's spec: Glyphs: `set` a list of `l` letters A-Z, `d` digits, `s` specials (MrlyFont's other 5 by 5 glyphs in `kit/font/font.json`, 35 of 45, no lowercase), default `lds`; Colour: `color` `tint` or `rainbow` (a hue per drop, rolled from the seed as each drop starts), `tint`; Motion: `tick` "Tick" 33 ms a step (16 to 120); Grid: `cell` "Size" 20 px (8 to 48, even). Columns are `floor(w / cell)` whole device-pixel cells centred by `board()`; the first drops all start at row 1 and fall together, a column added by a resize starts at a seeded row, and a drop resets past the floor when the roll clears 0.975.
- Sleep's spec: Shape: `design`, `number` (3 5 7 9) and `level` (1 2), each `''` Mixed (rolled per wall); Motion: `speed` 240 px a second (60 to 600, by 20) on the 45-degree diagonal; Size: `size` 0.15 of the smaller axis (0.08 to 0.4), the side snapped to whole device pixels a tile cell; Look: `tint`.
- Mrlytiles: `carpet` is on where at most one coordinate is odd, `net` where at least one is, `htree` on the even rows, `vtree` on the even columns, `void` where the two share a parity; level 2 is the level-1 grid Kroneckered with itself.
- Fractal: cycle 65536ms, fade 2048ms, zoom `pow(128, t)`, rotation `1/2048` a frame in a random direction, colour `1/256` a frame, start scale `1/2`, escape radius 128, `maxIter = 100 + floor(50 * log2(zoom))` under a hard cap of 1000.
- Every cycle re-rolls the target, the spin direction and the starting rotation; `stop()` clears the inline opacity the fade wrote.
- Fractal colour: inside is the ground, outside is `mix(ground, accent, 0.5 + 0.5 * cos(3.0 + sl * 0.15 + t))` over the smooth count `sl`.
- Wayfinder: 200 random points at 150 iterations a cycle, keeping the highest escape count that still escapes, which is the boundary.
- Lightspeed's spec, the value's keys: Jump: `max` "Jump speed" 14 (4 to 30), `idle` "Cruise" 0.015 (0 to 0.1 by 0.005); Sky: `stars` "Density" 1 (0.25 to 3 by 0.25), `tint` '' (Site) or a hue; Saver: `hyper` `no`, `yes` or `mix`, default `mix`.
- Lightspeed timings, read from `opts` for a host or a saver query but kept out of the url: `wind` 0.28 s, `run` 1 s (the jump; its bloom starts at 0.82 of it), `punch` 0.45 s (the flash into hyperspace and the tunnel fading in), `snap` 0.5 s (the exit bloom to the flash), `flash` 0.25 s held, `fade` 1 s; a number that is missing, empty, negative or not a number takes its default, and a `hyper` that is none of the three is `mix`.
- Lightspeed: `stars` times one star per 1100 px² between 400 and 1400; `onJump` once play time passes `wind + run`, `onDone` once it passes `snap` after the exit began. In hyperspace the stars slow to 40% of top speed with their streaks held at full length, and the vanishing point drifts up to 7% of the width and 6% of the height.
- Julia presets: `-0.4+0.6i`, `-0.8+0.156i`, `0.285+0.01i`, `-0.7269+0.1889i`, `-0.1+0.651i`, `0.355+0.355i`.
- Snake's spec: Board: `size` 16 (8 to 32, even), `wrap` 1, `apples` 1 (1 to 10); Pace: `tick` "Tick" 150 ms a step (50 to 500, by 25); Play: `play` `me`, `smart` or `silly`; Pieces: `head`, `body` and `food` `''` Random (a design each, rolled per game from the seed); Look: `tint`. Each part also rolls its number, 3, 5, 7 or 9, per game. Keys: Space pauses the game and the player with it, arrows and WASD turn, `r` a new game (a new seed), `?` the card. Board 92% of the short side by `board()`, ground `--accent` at 7%, the pieces at 35% during a game over, hold 1 s.

## FILES

- `apps.json`: the list. `apps.test.js`: the rows, their files and the Settings pick; the chrome names no saver.
- `<id>/scene.js`, `<id>/widget.jsx`, `<id>/index.jsx`; `snake/engine.js` the rules, ported from carlomitchener 7fdc1c6 `MrlySnake/logic.ts`; `snake/policy.js` the smart and silly players, new, since legacy had no player.
- `../lib/scene.js`: the xorshift (its seed mixed first, so small seeds differ), the paints, `TINTS` (the options of every app's `tint` pick), the colour parse, `board(view, cols, rows, fit)` (the centred board of whole device pixels a cell an app that draws cells uses) and `run()`. `../lib/scene.jsx`: `Scene`, `page` and `values`.
- `../lib/tiles.js`: the mrlytiles, ported from `pkgs/mrlyrs` `math::two::designs`, and `ink()`, which fills a tile's lit cells as snapped rects; sleep's mark and snake's pieces.
- `../lib/fractal.js`: `fractal(canvas, view, home, c)`, the fit, the escape count and `wayfind`. `../lib/gl.js`: the WebGL2 program, one fragment shader branching on `u_julia`.
- Each test sits beside its code: `../lib/scene.test.js`, `../lib/fractal.test.js`, `../lib/tiles.test.js`, `matrix/scene.test.js`, `sleep/sleep.test.js`, `lightspeed/scene.test.js`, `snake/engine.test.js`, `snake/policy.test.js`, `snake/scene.test.js`.
