# The coprimality spine

- Every fractal in this family counts the same thing on a different grid: the points of a digital design whose coordinates share no common factor.
- Each design has its own density constant, and the constants are one theorem with one input per design; this page is that theorem and the open front behind it.
- The base of the design contributes an exact factor, the bracket, known in closed form at every finite level; every prime away from the base contributes the classical factor `1 - p^(-D)`.
- Above dimension one the join is a theorem on the shelf; at dimension one it is open exactly for primes in one window of exponents, and that window is the standing problem here.
- The Sierpinski gasket is the worked case throughout, because its sequence is [A396934](https://oeis.org/A396934) and its density `16/(3*Pi^2)` is stated in [the ledger](sequences.md).
- Every claim carries one tag: **Proved** means a proof is given or restated here or on the shelf, **Verified** means recomputed by the named study or lane, **Conjecture** means supported and open, **Refuted** means shown false.

## THE OBJECT

- A **design** is a triple `(q, D, F)`: a base `q >= 2`, a dimension `D >= 2`, and a set `F` of filled digit-vectors, `F` a subset of `{0,...,q-1}^D` with `k = |F| >= 2`.
- The level-`n` set is `S_n = { x in [0, q^n)^D : every base-q digit-vector of x lies in F }`, holding exactly `k^n` points, one per string of `n` choices from `F`; it is the substitution rule of [the core](core.md) read on coordinates rather than cells.
- The count of interest is `A(n) = #{ x in S_n : gcd(x_1, ..., x_D) = 1 }`, and the question is what `A(n)/k^n` converges to.
- Let `L` be the subgroup of `Z^D` generated by the differences `F - F` and `m(F)` its index; the design is **spanning** when `m(F) = 1`.
- **Condition (E)**: `F - F` has full rank `D` and every prime dividing `m(F)` divides `q`, i.e. `rad(m(F)) | rad(q)`; spanning is (E) at index 1, and (E) is the hypothesis of every theorem below.
- Non-spanning designs are outside the theory, not anomalies inside it: base 2, `D = 2`, code 9 is a diagonal pair whose whole fractal is the line `i = j`, and its coprime count is stuck at `1` forever. **Proved.**
- Six census lines fail spanning, satisfy (E), and obey the density formula unchanged, as do constructed index-4 designs at bases 4 and 6 (`lab/design-census`). **Verified.**
- When (E) fails at a prime `p` not dividing `q`, the Euler factor at `p` is replaced by a coset-corrected factor that can depend on `n`, and `A(n)/k^n` can fail to converge: base 7, `D = 2`, `F = { v : v_1 + v_2 = 1 mod 3 }`, `k = 16`, index 3, has period-3 subsequential limits `0.698175, 0.698175, 0.465450`, each matched by the corrected constant to `3e-04` (`coprime-density-above-dimension-one`). **Verified.**
- The household failure is the Cantor dust `F = {0,2}^2` at base 3, dimension `log_3(4) = 1.26`, which fails (E) at index 4 and has `A(n) = 0` at every level, every coordinate being even, against a naive `0.512938`; dimension above one does not exempt a design from (E). **Proved.**
- One shear repairs it: dust points are `2*y` with `y` in the `{0,1}^2` design, which satisfies (E) with `k = 4 > 3`, so the dust's `#{gcd = 2}` density is exactly `81/(16*Pi^2) = 0.512938`, the constant surviving one prime down (`coprime-density-above-dimension-one`). **Proved.**

## THEOREM 1: THE BASE IS EXACT

- For every squarefree `e` dividing `rad(q)` and every `n >= 1`: `#{ x in S_n : e | x_i for all i } = k_e * k^(n-1)`, with `k_e = #{ v in F : e divides every component of v }`. **Proved.**
- The proof: `e | q*y` for every integer `y`, so writing `x = v_0 + q*y` the divisibility of `x` by `e` is the divisibility of the last digit-vector `v_0`; that vector is pinned to one of `k_e` corners and the other `n - 1` are free, with no error term at any level.
- At a prime base `q = p` the only digit-vector divisible by `p` is zero, so `x = p*y` maps `{x in S_n : p | x}` bijectively onto `S_(n-1)` with `gcd(x) = p*gcd(y)`, and `#{ x in S_n : p^a | gcd(x) } = a_0^a * k^(n-a)`, `a_0 = 1` if `0` is in `F`, else `0`. **Proved.**
- Every design is its own Euler factor at the base prime, and the factor is self-similar rather than approximate: the Vicsek plus demands digit `1` somewhere at every position, so `a_0 = 0`, no point of the plus has gcd divisible by 3 at any level, and its density is larger than `6/Pi^2`, not smaller. **Proved.**
- The identity is recomputed by direct enumeration on the gasket and or-triangle to `n = 8`, the carpet and Vicsek plus to `n = 6`, the base-6 sample to `n = 5`, the sponge to `n = 4`, and at `n = 4` on all 763 census lines, degenerate ones included, with zero failures; the identity does not need spanning (`lab/design-census`). **Verified.**

## THE BRACKET

- Mobius inversion over the base primes turns Theorem 1 into one exact number, the **bracket** `B(F) = Sum_{e | rad(q)} mu(e) * k_e / k`, and `B(F)*k^n` is exactly the number of points of `S_n` whose gcd is coprime to `q`, at every level. **Proved.**
- At a prime base the bracket is `1 - a_0/k`; at a composite base divisibility by the different base primes is correlated through the corner set, and the bracket is the whole story.
- The **base-6 sample**, `D = 2`, code 34376528265 in the census (`lab/design-census`), has `k = 8` with `k_2 = 3`, `k_3 = 2`, `k_6 = 1`, so `B(F) = 1 - 3/8 - 2/8 + 1/8 = 1/2` while the naive product `(1 - 3/8)*(1 - 2/8) = 0.46875`. **Proved.**
- Treating divisibility by 2 and by 3 as independent is wrong by an exact amount at every finite level, Theorem 1 with inclusion-exclusion over `{2, 3}` (`coprime-density-above-dimension-one`). **Proved.**

| `n` | points of `S_n` | gcd coprime to 6 | `k^n / 2` | naive `0.46875 * k^n` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 512 | 256 | 256 | 240 |
| 4 | 4096 | 2048 | 2048 | 1920 |
| 5 | 32768 | 16384 | 16384 | 15360 |

- The bracket reaches zero and the formula is right there: base 6 on the nine even-coordinate digit pairs, base 10 on the twenty-five, base 12 on the thirty-six, have every digit divisible by `2 | q`, so Theorem 1 pins `k_2 = k`, `B(F) = 0`, and `A(n) = 0` at every level. **Proved.**
- That is exactly the case the Cantor dust is not: there the offending prime does not divide the base, (E) fails, and the naive formula returns `0.512938` against a true `0`; the discriminator is whether the prime divides the base.
- The bracket spreads widely at fixed dimension: at `q = 4`, `k = 8`, `D = 2`, twenty-five designs of Hausdorff dimension `1.5` carry `B(F)` in `{1/2, 5/8, 3/4, 7/8, 1}` and densities in `{0.4052847, 0.5066059, 0.6079271, 0.7092483, 0.8105695}`, and sixteen base-6 designs take `0, 1/3, 5/12, 4/9, 1/2, 6/11, 5/9, 2/3, 13/20, 3/4, 1`; no study regenerates the sweep. **Conjecture.**

## LEMMA A: UNIFORM CONTRACTION

- For `gcd(d, q) = 1`, nonzero `t` in `(Z/d)^D`, and `r = ord_d(q)`, put `f_l(t) = (1/k) * |Sum_{v in F} e(q^l * <t, v> / d)|`, `e(x) = exp(2*Pi*i*x)`.
- **Lemma A.** For every design satisfying (E), `Prod_{l=0}^{r-1} f_l(t) <= c(q, k) := 1 - (2/k)*(1 - cos(Pi/(2*q))) < 1`. **Proved.**
- The proof: (E) gives `v, v'` in `F` with `delta = <t, v - v'>` nonzero mod `d`; let `y_l` be the distance from `q^l * delta / d` to the nearest integer, purely periodic and never `0` since `gcd(q, d) = 1`; whenever `y_l < 1/(2*q)` one has `y_(l+1) = q*y_l`, so some `l_0` in the period has `y_l0 >= 1/(2*q)`, and splitting the character sum there into the pair and the other `k - 2` terms gives `|Sum| <= k - 2 + 2*cos(Pi/(2*q))`.
- Why (E) suffices where spanning was first assumed: `L` has finite index `m(F)` with `rad(m(F)) | rad(q)`, so `m * Z^D` lies in `L`; a character `t` vanishing on `F - F` vanishes on `L`, hence `m * t = 0 mod d`, and `gcd(m, d) = 1` forces `t = 0`; every downstream use of Lemma A is untouched. **Proved.**
- The constant depends only on `q` and `k`, not on which corners `F` holds, and it is far from sharp; being below `1` for every design at once is the point.
- The **or-triangle** is base 2, `D = 2`, code 14, every digit pair but `(0,0)`, i.e. the pairs with `i OR j = 2^n - 1`, of which there are `3^n`.

- The `c(q, k)` column is the formula; the observed column, the gasket's true contraction `1/3` against a bound of `0.80`, is a finite search that no study regenerates. **Conjecture.**
- **Corollary (equidistribution rate).** For `gcd(d, q) = 1` and any `a` in `(Z/d)^D`, `| #{x in S_n : x = a mod d} / k^n - 1/d^D | <= c(q, k)^floor(n / ord_d(q))`, by character orthogonality: the principal character gives `1/d^D`, each other character loses one factor of `c` per complete orbit cycle among the `n` digit positions. **Proved.**
- The gasket at `d = 5`, `n = 10` deviates `0.0015` against a bound of `0.65`; no study regenerates the check. **Conjecture.**
- **Corollary (base peel).** For squarefree `d = e*m` with `e | rad(q)` and `gcd(m, q) = 1`, `T_d(n) = #{x in S_n : d | x_i for all i}` satisfies `T_d(n) / k^n = (k_e / k) * (1 / m^D) + O(c(q,k)^floor((n-1)/ord_m(q)))`, the last digit-vector pinned mod `e` by Theorem 1 and the rest a shifted residue class mod `m`; at a prime base with `0` in `F` it is the exact identity `T_(q*m)(n) = T_m(n-1)`. **Proved.**

## THE MASTER DENSITY

- The formula the whole census is measured against: `delta = B(F) * Prod_{p not dividing q} (1 - p^(-D)) = B(F) * (1/zeta(D)) * Prod_{p | q} (1 - p^(-D))^(-1)`.
- **Upper bound.** For every design satisfying (E), `limsup_n A(n)/k^n <= delta`: fix `z`, sift by the primes up to `z`, handle each of the finitely many squarefree moduli exactly by the base peel, and let `z` grow only after `n`. **Proved.**
- **Lemma B.** Write `T*_p(n)` for the count of nonzero `x` in `S_n` with `p | gcd(x)`, which vanishes for `p >= q^n`; Lemma B is `lim_{z} limsup_n (1/k^n) * Sum_{p > z, p not dividing q} T*_p(n) = 0`, and it is exactly what separates the upper bound from the limit `A(n)/k^n -> delta`.
- The origin must be excluded: `x = 0` lies in `S_n` whenever the zero corner is filled, every prime divides `0`, and the unstarred sum over all `p > z` diverges.
- Lemma B is a uniform equidistribution estimate for digit-restricted sets over moduli growing with the level; the one-dimensional literature is [Erdos, Mauduit and Sarkozy 1998](https://doi.org/10.1006/jnth.1998.2229), [Konyagin 2001](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1015256809636) and [Maynard 2019](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00222-019-00865-6), all three needing `|F| < q`, operating below the gasket's critical band, and treating no vector digit set.
- **Theorem (above dimension one).** For every design with `D >= 2`, condition (E), and `k > q`, `A(n) / k^n -> delta`: the hypothesis is the geometry, dimension `log_q(k) > 1`, and nothing else (`coprime-density-above-dimension-one`). **Proved.**
- The three steps: the box bound `N*_n(m) <= (q+1)^D * k^n * m^(-alpha)`, `alpha = log_q(k)`, `N*_n(m) = 0` for `m >= q^n`, which is the Ahlfors-David regularity of missing-digit sets ([Chow, Varju and Yu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18395)); the Chebyshev sum `G(n) = Sum_{x != 0} log gcd(x) = Sum_m Lambda(m) N*_n(m) <= (q+1)^D * (-zeta'(alpha)/zeta(alpha)) * k^n`, convergent exactly when `alpha > 1`; and the close, `A_z(n) - A(n) <= G(n)/log z` uniformly in `n`, since every point sifted at `z` but not coprime has `log gcd > log z`.
- What that settles: the gasket's `16/(3*Pi^2)` ([A396934](https://oeis.org/A396934)), the or-triangle's `8/Pi^2`, the carpet's `189/(32*Pi^2)`, the Vicsek plus's `27/(4*Pi^2)`, the sponge's `(513/520)/zeta(3)` and both base-6 samples are theorems. **Proved.**
- The proof is qualitative: chaining the steps gives an error of order `(log n)^(1-alpha)`, while the measured convergence is geometric, the carpet's gap halving per level down to `-3.52e-07` at `n = 20` (`lab/dimension-one-ladder`, live to `n = 18` and stored to `n = 20`); nothing here explains the rate. **Conjecture.**
- **Corollary (no linear recurrence).** For any design satisfying the theorem with `B(F) > 0` and `D` even or `D = 3`, `A(n)` is not C-finite: a C-finite sequence with `A(n)/k^n` convergent has a rational limit, and `delta` is a nonzero rational multiple of `1/zeta(D)`, irrational for even `D` by the transcendence of `Pi` and at `D = 3` by Apery. **Proved.**
- At odd `D >= 5` the corollary waits on the irrationality of `zeta(D)`, and holonomic recurrences are excluded only by exhaustive exact fitting with held-out terms. **Conjecture.**
- **The periodic pattern owes Lemma B nothing.** Among nonzero points of `Z^D` visible from the origin whose least residue vector mod `q` lies in `F`, the density is exactly `delta * (k / q^D) = (1/zeta(D)) * Prod_{p | q} (1 - p^(-D))^(-1) * q^(-D) * m_c`, `m_c = k * B(F)` the corners nonzero mod every base prime; a base prime is decided by the residue class, a foreign prime is independent by CRT, and the Mobius tail beyond modulus `z` is `O(z^(1-D))` uniformly in the box, which is where `D >= 2` enters. **Proved.**
- Designs differing only in the zero corner have identical visible density, since the zero corner never counts toward `m_c`; `F` the whole residue cube recovers the classical `1/zeta(D)` of [pi out of the stack](pi.md); the gasket gives `4/Pi^2 = 0.4052847345`, the carpet `21/(4*Pi^2) = 0.5319362141`, the sponge `19/(26*zeta(3)) = 0.6079323107`. **Proved.**
- **The shear theorem.** A unimodular `sigma` with `sigma(F)` inside the digit cube acts digit-wise without carries and preserves gcd, so `F` and `sigma F` have identical `A(n)` at every level; at base 2 the shears `(i, j) -> (i, i+j)` and `(i, j) -> (i+j, j)` carry code 7 to codes 11 and 13, all three on `2, 4, 12, 34, 122, 362`. **Proved.**
- The converse is false: base-3 codes 11 and 161, the gasket and `{(0,0),(1,2),(2,1)}`, have identical `A(n)` at every level and are not shear-equivalent, by the E-decomposition below. **Proved.**

## THE WINDOW AT DIMENSION ONE

- **The E-decomposition.** A point of `S_n(F)`, `F = {v_0, v_1, v_2}`, is a string of corner choices `c_l`; with `E_j = Sum_{l : c_l = j} 3^l` the three `E_j` have disjoint base-3 supports summing to `c_n = (3^n - 1)/2`, `(E_1, E_2)` ranges bijectively over the level-`n` gasket `G_n`, and `x = c_n v_0 + M (E_1, E_2)^T`, `M = (v_1 - v_0, v_2 - v_0)`, with `Delta = det M != 0` exactly when `F - F` has full rank. **Proved.**
- **Theorem (one set).** For every full-rank three-corner design, every `m` coprime to `Delta` and every `n`, `#{ x in S_n : m | x_1, m | x_2 } = #{ (u, v) in G_n : (u, v) = tau(n) mod m }` with `tau(n) = -c_n M^(-1) v_0 mod m`; so Lemma B for every dimension-one line is shifted-target equidistribution of the gasket pair, every bound below is target-uniform, and the 36 lines stand or fall together. **Proved.**
- **Corollary (simplex reduction).** For any base `q` and any full-rank design with `k = D + 1` corners the same decomposition reduces every divisibility count to the base-`q` simplex `{0, e_1, ..., e_D}` with explicit targets; the sub-dimension-one world is a one-parameter family of simplex problems. **Proved.**
- Codes 11 and 161 both have `v_0 = 0`, target `0`, and `Delta` in `{-1, -3}`, so every modulus coprime to 3 gives the same gasket count and the base prime peels identically, whence their collision. **Proved.**
- Under `GL_2(Z)` shears the 36 lines collapse to 11 classes, in 8 of which the fractal is the graph `x_2 = g(x_1)` of a carry-free digit relabeling; the classes `{26, 50, 152}`, `{176}`, `{416}` are graphs of nothing, and the search is finite because a unimodular matrix keeping a full-rank corner set inside the digit cube has entries at most 6 in absolute value; no study regenerates the search. **Conjecture.**
- **The pincer.** Lemma B for the gasket splits by the prime's exponent `beta = log_3(p) / n`: the top range closes by the ray machine at `beta > 0.6402122` (`lemma-b-pincer`), the bottom by the moment ladder below, and the open lemma is pinched between.
- **The moment identities.** For `p != 3` write `f(t) = (1 + e(t_1) + e(t_2))/3` and `F_a(t) = Prod_{l<a} |f(3^l t/p)|`; gasket digits are `{0,1}` per coordinate, so adding two gasket points never carries and the additive energy is exactly `15^a`, while three or four summands carry only inside `{-1,0,1}^2`, a nine-state automaton, giving `Sum_t F_a^2 = p^2 * 3^(-a)`, `Sum_t F_a^4 = p^2 * (5/27)^a`, `E_6` growth `lambda_6 = 57 + 6*sqrt(46) = 97.693980` and `E_8` growth `lambda_8 = 456 + 3*sqrt(11017) = 770.885694`, exact Perron roots of integer transfer matrices (`lab/dimension-one-ladder`). **Proved.**
- The `L2` identity is the box bound again, sharp, so the first `2 log_3 p` digits of the product never save anything and every saving is earned past them. **Proved.**
- **The ladder.** Hoelder across digit blocks, using only the bijection `t -> 3t` and never `ord_p(3)`, gives with `kappa = 3 - log_3 5` and `a = floor(log_3(p/2))`, for every `eta in (0, beta_0)`, `z >= 5`, `n >= 1`: `Sum_{z < p <= 3^((beta_0 - eta) n)} T*_p(n)/3^n <= 2/z + 35 z^(1-kappa) + 40 * 3^(-0.0669 n) + 3 * 3^(-0.884 eta n)`, so Lemma B holds unconditionally and target-uniformly for `p <= 3^((beta_0 - eta) n)`, with `beta_0^(2K) = kappa_2K / (2 kappa_2K + 2 - kappa)` and `kappa_2K = 2K - log_3 lambda_2K`. **Proved.**

## B-VISIBILITY

- Coprimality is the `b = 1` member of a family: `(x, y)` is **b-visible** when no `k > 1` has `k | x` and `k^b | y`, i.e. visible from the origin along the power curve `y = a*x^b`, and the full-lattice density is `1/zeta(b+1)` ([Goins, Harris, Kubik and Mbirika 2018](https://doi.org/10.1080/00029890.2018.1465760)). **Proved.**
- Reproduced at `b = 1, 2, 3, 4` on the `N x N` positive square at `N = 10^4`: `0.60794971, 0.83191407, 0.92394823, 0.96439991` against `0.60792710, 0.83190737, 0.92393840, 0.96438734`; no study regenerates the check. **Conjecture.**

## THE PARITY BAND

- A mrlybang code is a set `P` of parity corners, `P subset {0,1}^3`, and at every base `q >= 2` it induces the design `F_q(P) = { v in {0,...,q-1}^3 : v mod 2 in P }`; the carpet rule "at most one odd" read at base 5, 7 or 46 is one code worn at many bases.
- Three quantities of `P` decide everything: the weight enumerator `W_j = #{ v in P : popcount(v) = j }`, the mod-2 difference span `H = <P - P>` with `s2 = dim H`, and whether the affine span of `P` avoids `0`; write `t = W_0 = [000 in P]`.
- **Theorem (even bases are quantized).** For every nonempty code `P` and every even `q >= 4`, `delta * zeta(3) = (8/7) * (1 - t/|P|)`, independent of `q` and of everything about `P` except `|P|` and whether the origin corner is filled. **Proved.**
- The proof: each parity class holds `q/2` digits so `k = |P| (q/2)^3`; for odd squarefree `m | q` the multiples of `m` alternate parity and number `q/m`, even, so every parity class gets `q/(2m)` of them and `k_m = |P| (q/(2m))^3`; multiples of `2m` are all even so `k_{2m} = t (q/(2m))^3`; Mobius over `rad(q)` collapses to `B = (1 - t/|P|) * Prod_{odd p | q} (1 - p^(-3))`, the odd base primes cancel their own Euler corrections, and only the factor at 2 survives; every parity design at even `q >= 4` satisfies (E), `L` containing `2 Z^3` with index dividing 8, and `k > q`, so the theorem above applies to all 255 nonempty codes.
- The nine values the band takes, times `1/zeta(3)`: `t = 0: 8/7`; `t = 1: 0, 4/7, 16/21, 6/7, 32/35, 20/21, 48/49, 1`. **Proved.**
- Checked exactly for every code and every even `q <= 40`, and by enumeration at `q = 2` and `q = 4`: carpet `0.7129` and `0.7091` against `(6/7)/zeta(3) = 0.7131` at `n = 5, 12`; net `0.9518` against `(8/7)/zeta(3) = 0.9508`; the single-corner code `{100}` at `q = 4` heading to the same `8/7`, denser than the full lattice (`lab/mrlybang-density-classes`). **Verified.**
- At `q = 2` the same rational value holds whenever the code itself satisfies (E) with `|P| > 2`: 151 of the 255 codes are full rank there, all at index 1 or 2, so the hedge is real (`lab/mrlybang-density-classes`). **Verified.**
- In `D = 2` the same proof gives `delta * zeta(2) = (1 - t/|P|)/(1 - 1/4)`, and the parity carpet at every even base lands on `delta * zeta(2) = (2/3)(4/3) = 8/9`, `delta = 16/(3 Pi^2)` exactly: the gasket's constant is not the gasket's, it is the even-base band value of its parity code. **Proved.**
- **Theorem (odd bases are self-similar across bases).** For odd `q` and squarefree `e | q`, the multiples of `e` among `0..q-1` have the parity profile of the base-`q/e` digit set, so with `k_P(n) = Sum_j W_j ((n+1)/2)^(3-j) ((n-1)/2)^j`: `k_e(q) = k_P(q/e)` and `B(q) = Sum_{e | rad(q)} mu(e) k_P(q/e) / k_P(q)`; the design's bracket at base `q` reads the same design at base `q/e`, self-similarity across bases rather than levels. **Proved.**

## COPRIMALITY ON THE SLICES

- Fix a parity design at base `q`, level `n`, and a height `s`; let `N_s` count the design points on the plane `x + y + z = s` and `A_s` the coprime ones; slice coprimality differs from the solid's by one divisibility, the gcd of a slice point divides its height, and everything below follows from that.
- **Theorem (slice coprimality is finite arithmetic).** `gcd(x,y,z) | s` on the plane, so `A_s = Sum_{d | s, d squarefree} mu(d) * N_s^(d)`, exact at every height and level, with `N_s^(d)` the points `d` divides coordinatewise; no zeta function, no tail, no Lemma B, and a slice at prime height is fully visible up to at most the three axis points. **Proved.**
