# even-block-valuations

- Prints `det M_even` exactly and its `v_p` for the base-3 middle-digit diagonal slice at every `dim = 2..40`, and tests the route "Lemma H by one prime": a prime `p` with `v_p(det M_even) = 0` at every `dim`.
- `P(t) = (1 + t^2)^(dim-1)(1 + dim t + t^2)`; the carry matrix is `M[c, c'] = P[c + dim - 3c']` on `abs(c) <= floor((dim-1)/2)`; `M_even` is its reflection-even block of size `ceil(dim/2)`.
- Exact integers throughout: Bareiss on Python `int`, with `Fraction` elimination and sympy as cross-checks; PARI for the factorisation.

## VERBS

- `selftest`: the digit polynomial against the `3^dim` digit census at `dim = 2..10`; the carry matrix against the transitions `c -> (c + dim - s)/3` over all digit vectors at `dim = 2..9`; `M_even` built three ways (row fold, column fold, projection of the full carry matrix) with equal determinants at every `dim = 2..40` by Bareiss and `Fraction` elimination, sympy to `dim = 30`; `det(full) = det(even) det(odd)`; no zero determinant; the controls `dim = 3` (trace 9, determinant 12) and `dim = 4` (`x^2 - 11x - 66`); `v_2 = 7` at `dim = 7`; `v_2 <= n` at odd `dim = 5..39` with violations `5, 7` and equality `9, 15` only.
- `det`: the table `dim, n, digits, v_2, v_3, v_5, v_7`, the first `dim` with `v_p > 0`, the maximum and the zero set for `p = 2, 3, 5, 7`, and the shape of `v_3`: monotone, the pair law `v_3(3k) = v_3(3k+1)`, `v_3(3k) - k^2`, the ratio to `dim^2`.
- `primes`: for every prime `p < 100`, the number of `dim` with `v_p > 0`, the first such `dim`, the maximum; the primes keeping `v_p = 0` throughout; the count a random residue would give.
- `factor`: `det M_even` factored completely by `gp -q` (batched), checked to multiply back and to agree with the direct `v_p` for `p < 100`; the prime factors above 50 at each `dim`, the largest, the primes below 400 dividing no determinant.
- Without a verb the run executes `selftest`, `det` and `primes` in that order; `factor` runs only by name.

## RESULTS

- `det M_even` is a nonzero integer at all 39 dims.
- Every prime `p <= 59` divides `det M_even` at some `dim <= 30`; the least primes dividing none for `dim <= 40` are `61, 67, 73, 97`.
- `v_2 > 0` at 39 of 39 dims, `v_3 > 0` at 38 (not at `dim = 2`), `v_5 > 0` at 19, `v_7 > 0` at 13; `v_3 = 181` at `dim = 40`.
- A prime factor above 50 at 34 of 39 dims: `11971` at `dim = 8`, `2687` at `dim = 9`, a 28-digit prime at `dim = 35`.
