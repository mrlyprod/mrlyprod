Fix a base `b >= 3`, a set `E` of `m >= 1` excluded digits, the rest `F` with `fill = b - m`, and the missing-digit design `S_F` of [mobius](mobius.md): the positive integers whose base-`b` digits all lie in `F`. That page bounds `M_F(x) = sum_(n in S_F, n <= x) mu(n)` unconditionally only at the shape `A_F(x) exp(-c sqrt(log x))`, and [coprime](coprime.md) carries the same shape for the primes of the design; in both, the ceiling is the major arcs, where `mu` and `Lambda` must be read in progressions mod powers of `b` through a zero-free region. This page twists the design by its digit sum and shows that the twisted transform loses a fixed share of its mass per pair of digits at every frequency, so the major arcs need no L-function and the saving becomes a power of `x`. Every number is printed by [lab/py/digit-sum-twist](../lab/py/digit-sum-twist/).

## The objects

- `s_b(n)` is the sum of the base-`b` digits of `n`, `D_k` the digit strings of length `k` over `F` read as integers in `[0, b^k)`, `g_F(t) = sum_(d in F) e(d t)` the digit symbol and `A_F(x)` the count of `S_F` up to `x`, all as on [mobius](mobius.md).
- The twisted weight is `w(n) = 1_(S_F)(n) e(alpha s_b(n))` for a real `alpha`, and its transform at depth `k` is `hat w_k(t) = sum_(n in D_k) e(alpha s_b(n) + n t)`. With `n = sum_j d_j b^j` the phase is `sum_j d_j (b^j t + alpha)`, and the digits are independent, so `hat w_k(t) = prod_(j < k) g_F(b^j t + alpha)`. At `alpha = 0` this is the transform `hat F_k` of [mobius](mobius.md).
- The twisted meters are `M_F(x; alpha) = sum_(n in S_F, n <= x) mu(n) e(alpha s_b(n))` and `psi_F(x; alpha) = sum_(n in S_F, n <= x) Lambda(n) e(alpha s_b(n))`.
- `beta = norm((b - 1) alpha)` is the distance from `(b - 1) alpha` to the nearest integer, and the lattice is the set `(b - 1)^(-1) Z` of `alpha` with `beta = 0`.
- `B_b(F) = sup_t sum_(r mod b) abs(g_F((t + r)/b))` is the one-step constant of [mobius](mobius.md), a supremum over every shift `t`. The certificate (W) of that page's unconditional dissection is `B_b(F) <= fill b^(alpha_1)` with `alpha_1 < 1/5`, read from the per-digit constants `PB_b(m)` or `PB'_b(1, e_0)`; `alpha_b = log(fill)/log(b)`.
- `V_F = fill^(-1) sum_(d in F) (d - mean(F))^2` is the variance of the digit set, `K_b(x) = max(sin(pi b x)/sin(pi x), 1/sin(pi/b))` and `x_f = 1/(2(b - 1))`.

## The two-position lemma

- **The lemma. Proved.** Let `F` keep two consecutive digits, put `x_0 = beta/(b + 1)` and let `G_F(x_0) = sup of abs(g_F(v))` over `norm(v) >= x_0`. Then for every real `u` and `alpha`, `abs(g_F(u + alpha) g_F(b u + alpha)) <= fill G_F(x_0)`, and `G_F(x_0) <= min(G_1, G_2, G_3)` with the pair form `G_1 = fill - 4 sin^2(pi x_0/2)`, the kernel form `G_2 = m + K_b(x_0)` and the variance form `G_3 = max(fill sqrt(1 - 16 x_0^2 V_F), min(m + K_b(x_f), fill - 4 sin^2(pi x_f/2)))`. So with `c = c(b, F, beta) = 1 - min(G_1, G_2, G_3)/fill`, which is positive whenever `beta > 0` because `G_1 < fill`, `sup_t abs(hat w_k(t)) <= fill^k (1 - c)^(floor(k/2)) <= (1 - c)^(-1/2) fill^k b^(-gamma k)` at every `k`, with `gamma = log(1/(1 - c))/(2 log b)`.
- **Proof.** Locating: put `v_1 = u + alpha` and `v_2 = b u + alpha`. Then `b v_1 - v_2 = (b - 1) alpha`, so `beta = norm(b v_1 - v_2) <= b norm(v_1) + norm(v_2) <= (b + 1) max(norm(v_1), norm(v_2))`, and one of the two phases has `norm(v_i) >= x_0`; its factor is at most `G_F(x_0)` and the other at most `fill`. The pair form: with `d, d + 1` in `F`, `abs(g_F(v)) <= fill - 2 + abs(1 + e(v)) = fill - 2 + 2 abs(cos(pi v)) = fill - 4 sin^2(pi norm(v)/2)`, which falls as `norm(v)` rises. The kernel form: `g_F = D_b - g_E` with `D_b(v) = sum_(n < b) e(n v)` and `abs(g_E) <= m`; at `x = norm(v)` in `(0, 1/b]`, `abs(D_b(v)) = sum_(n < b) cos(2 pi (n - (b - 1)/2) x)`, each term falling in `x` because `2 pi abs(n - (b - 1)/2) x < pi`, so its supremum over `[x_0, 1/b]` sits at `x_0 <= 1/(2(b + 1)) < 1/b`, and on `[1/b, 1/2]` it is at most `1/sin(pi/b)`. The variance form: `abs(g_F(v))^2 = sum_(d, d' in F) cos(2 pi (d - d') x) = fill^2 - 2 sum_(d, d') sin^2(pi (d - d') x)`, and at `x <= x_f` every `abs(d - d') x <= 1/2`, where `sin^2(pi y) >= 4 y^2`, while `sum_(d, d') (d - d')^2 = 2 fill^2 V_F`; so `abs(g_F(v)) <= fill sqrt(1 - 16 x^2 V_F)`, falling in `x`, on `[x_0, x_f]`, and past `x_f`, which is at most `1/b`, the pair and kernel forms at `x_f` apply. The product: pair the positions `2i, 2i + 1` of `hat w_k(t) = prod_(j < k) g_F(b^j t + alpha)` with `u = b^(2i) t`, bound an odd last factor by `fill`, and use `floor(k/2) >= (k - 1)/2`.
- **The dichotomy is exact. Proved.** On the lattice the lemma cannot hold: at `t = -alpha` every phase is `b^j t + alpha = -(b^j - 1) alpha`, an integer because `b^j - 1` is a multiple of `b - 1`, so `abs(hat w_k(-alpha)) = fill^k` at every `k`. With two consecutive digits the twisted transform loses a fixed fraction of its mass at every frequency exactly when `alpha` is off the lattice.
- **The consecutive pair is needed. Verified.** At base 5, `F = {0, 2}` and `alpha = 1/8`, off the lattice with `beta = 1/2`, every phase `5^j (3/8) + 1/8 = (3 * 5^j + 1)/8` lies in `Z/2`, where `abs(g_F) = 2`, so `abs(hat w_k(3/8)) = 2^k = fill^k` at every `k`; the generator reads `1` to twelve digits at `k = 4, 8, 12` (verb `lemma`).
- **The constants. Verified.** At base 10 missing `7`: `c >= 0.1500` and `gamma >= 0.0352` at `alpha = 1/2`, `c >= 0.0463` at `1/7`, `0.0353` at `1/4`, `0.0224` at `1/5`, `0.0055` at `1/10`, and `5.55 * 10^-5` at `1/900` (`beta = 0.01`), each from the variance form, which beats the pair form by a factor `66` at `alpha = 1/2` and `61` at `1/900`. At large base the kernel form carries `beta = 1/2` and `0.2`, `c >= 0.3633` and `0.0644` at `base 92317`, and the variance form carries small `beta`, `c >= 6.666 * 10^-7` at `beta = 10^-3`: to leading order it gives `c = 8 x_0^2 V_F`, and since `V_F` is about `b^2/12` and `x_0` about `beta/b`, that is `c` about `(2/3) beta^2` at small `beta` and large base, where the pair form alone decays like `beta^2 b^(-3)` (verb `lemma`).

## The twisted peel

- **The peel. Proved.** For every `alpha`, every `i >= 1` and every real `s`, the twisted grid sum `Sigma_i(s) = sum_(a < b^i) abs(hat w_i(s + a/b^i))` obeys `Sigma_i(s) <= B_b(F) Sigma_(i-1)(b s)`, so `Sigma_i(s) <= B_b(F)^i`, and under (W) `Sigma_i(s) <= fill^i b^(i alpha_1)`: the certificate of [mobius](mobius.md) with `C_F = 1`, at every shift, for the twisted transform. Proof: `hat w_i(t) = g_F(t + alpha) hat w_(i-1)(b t)` and `hat w_(i-1)` has period `1`. Write `a = a' + r b^(i-1)` with `a' < b^(i-1)` and `r < b`; then `b (s + a/b^i) = b s + a'/b^(i-1) + r`, so the second factor depends on `a'` alone, and `s + a/b^i + alpha = (T + r)/b` with `T = b (s + alpha) + a'/b^(i-1)`, so the sum over `r` is `sum_(r mod b) abs(g_F((T + r)/b)) <= B_b(F)`. The twist moves only the shift `T`, and `B_b(F)` is a supremum over every shift.
- **The hybrid bound transfers. Proved.** The hybrid `l^1` bound of [mobius](mobius.md) reads the transform through three things: the split `hat F_k(t) = hat F_(i_1)(t) hat F_(k - i_1 - i_2)(V_1 t) hat F_(i_2)(y t/V_2)`, the certificate at a shift or at `s = 0`, and `abs(g_F') <= pi b (b - 1)` at one position. The split holds for `hat w_k = prod_(j < k) g_F(b^j t + alpha)` with the same three factors read for `hat w`, the derivative of `g_F(v + alpha)` in `v` has the same bound, and the shifted grids its proof integrates over are covered by the peel above, as is `int_0^1 abs(hat w_i) <= b^(-i) B_b(F)^i`. So under (W), `sum_(a in R(D, H)) abs(hat w_k(a/y)) <= (1 + pi b) fill^k (V_1 V_2)^(alpha_1)` at every `alpha`, with `R(D, H)`, `V_1` and `V_2` as there.
- **The chain and the window do not transfer as written.** The digit-uniform chain of [coprime](coprime.md), which carries the untwisted certificate down to `base 584`, telescopes a run of positions into one Dirichlet kernel, `prod_(j < l) D_b(b^j u) = D_(b^l)(u)`, which needs consecutive arguments in ratio `b`; the twisted arguments are `b^j t + alpha`, and `b (b^j t + alpha) = b^(j+1) t + b alpha` differs from `b^(j+1) t + alpha` mod `1` unless `beta = 0`. The window certificates of the same page bound a supremum over the one-parameter family of shifts `s`, while a twisted window sees the shift vector `(b^j s + alpha)_j`. Neither is refuted; neither is carried, so only the per-digit (W) serves here.
- **The readings. Verified.** At base 10 missing `7` the grid supremum of `sum_(r mod b) abs(g_F((T + r)/b))` reads `24.702183` on `2 * 10^5` shifts, certified below `24.702781` by the Lipschitz margin, against the floor `2(b - 1) = 18` and the step 3 bound `39.6623`. Over `16` classes of `alpha`, `12` shifts and depths `1..5`, `960` checks, the one-step identity holds to `1.40 * 10^-11` and `Sigma_i(s)/B^i` and `Sigma_i(s)/(B Sigma_(i-1)(b s))` stay below `0.999914`. The twist raises the unshifted mass, `Sigma_5(0)^(1/5)` reading `21.7151` at `alpha = 1/2` and `22.3176` at `1/7` against `19.5656` untwisted: it moves mass toward the shifted supremum, which (W) already pays (verb `peel`).

## The power saving

- **The theorem. Proved.** Let `F` keep two consecutive digits and satisfy (W), so `B_b(F) <= fill b^(alpha_1)` with `alpha_1 < 1/5`, and let `beta = norm((b - 1) alpha) > 0`. With `c = c(b, F, beta)` and `gamma` of the two-position lemma, put `sigma = gamma (1 - 4 alpha_1)/(5 - 4 alpha_1)`, `eta = min(1/5 - alpha_1, sigma)` and `delta_0 = alpha_b eta/4`. Then for every `delta < delta_0` there is an effective `C`, depending on `b`, `F`, `beta` and `delta` alone, with `abs(M_F(x; alpha)) <= C A_F(x) x^(-delta)` and `abs(psi_F(x; alpha)) <= C A_F(x) x^(-delta)` at every `x >= 2`. Since `c` rises with `beta`, the same `C` serves every `alpha` with `norm((b - 1) alpha) >= beta`. The bullets below are the proof; the blocks, the dissection and the minor arcs are those of the unconditional dissection of [mobius](mobius.md) and its von Mangoldt form on [coprime](coprime.md), and only the depth of the blocks and the major arcs change.
- **The blocks, at power depth.** The block split of [mobius](mobius.md) writes `S_F` below `x` as at most `fill + 1` blocks `P y + D_k`, `y = b^k`, at each scale `k < L`, the shorter elements included, and the point `x`. On a block `s_b(P y + t) = s_b(P) + s_b(t)` for `t < y`, so its twisted sum is `e(alpha s_b(P)) Sigma(P, k)` with `Sigma(P, k) = sum_(t in D_k) e(alpha s_b(t)) mu(P y + t) = y^(-1) sum_(a mod y) hat w_k(a/y) S_P(-a/y)`, by orthogonality on `Z/y` applied to `t -> 1_(D_k)(t) e(alpha s_b(t))`, whose transform at `a/y` is `hat w_k(a/y)`, and `S_P(theta) = sum_(P y <= n < (P+1) y) mu(n) e(n theta)`; for `Lambda` read `Lambda` in both. Put `tau = eta/4`. The scales with `b^k < x^(1 - tau)` weigh at most `2 (fill + 1) fill^(k_0)`, `k_0` the largest of them, with `fill^(k_0) < x^((1 - tau) alpha_b)`, which is `<< A_F(x) x^(-tau alpha_b)` by the mass floor `A_F(x) >= fill^(L-1) - 1 >= (x/b)^(alpha_b) - 1`, times `log x` for `Lambda`. Every other block has `r = x/y <= x^tau`.
- **The dissection.** On such a block put `Q = y^(3/5)` and `Z = y^zeta` with `zeta = 2 gamma/(5 - 4 alpha_1)`, which is below `2/5`: each of `G_1`, `G_2`, `G_3` is at least `min(fill - 1, m + b/pi) >= fill/pi`, so `gamma <= log(pi)/(2 log 3) < 0.53` at every base, `zeta < 0.26` and `zeta < gamma/2`; give each `a mod y` its Dirichlet fraction `l/d`, `d <= Q`, and its height `h = abs(a d - l y) <= y^(2/5)`, and cut three regions: A is `d >= y^(2/5)`, B is `d < y^(2/5)` with `max(d, h) >= Z`, and C is `d < Z` and `h < Z`, the union of the C1 and C2 of [mobius](mobius.md).
- **The minor arcs.** For `mu` the bullet The minor-arc input of [mobius](mobius.md) (Basak, Robles and Zaharescu, Theorem 1.4, at both approximations) and for `Lambda` the bullet The minor arcs, at source of [coprime](coprime.md) ([Maynard 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.07711), Lemma 4.2, at a coarser fraction) give, with `x^(o(1))` there replaced by the ratio `r`: on A, `x d^(-1/2) <= x y^(-1/5) = x^(4/5) r^(1/5)` and `(x d)^(1/2) <= x^(4/5)`, so `abs(S_P(a/y)) <<_eps x^(4/5 + eps) r^(1/5)`; on B the same term plus `x (log x)^4 max(d, h)^(-1/2)`, the second approximation's `x (h/y)^(1/2) <= x^(7/10) r^(3/10)` lying below `x^(4/5) r^(1/5)` because `r <= x`.
- **Region A.** By the peel at `s = 0` its residues carry at most `B_b(F)^k <= fill^k y^(alpha_1)`, so region A is `<<_eps y^(-1) fill^k y^(alpha_1) (r y)^(4/5 + eps) r^(1/5) <= fill^k r^(1 + eps) y^(alpha_1 - 1/5 + eps)`.
- **Region B.** Group its residues into the classes `D <= d < 2D`, `H <= h < 2H` or `h = 0` of [mobius](mobius.md), at most `(log_2 y + 2)^2` of them, each with `16 b^2 D (D + H) <= 32 b^2 y^(4/5) <= y` once `y >= (32 b^2)^5` and `max(D, H) > Z/2`. By the transferred hybrid bound a class weighs at most `y^(-1) abs(S_P) (1 + pi b) fill^k (32 b^2 max(D, H)^2)^(alpha_1)`. The first term of `abs(S_P)` with `max(D, H) <= y^(2/5)` gives `<<_eps fill^k r^(1 + eps) y^((4/5) alpha_1 - 1/5 + eps)`, below region A's exponent, and the second with `max(D, H) > Z/2` and `2 alpha_1 - 1/2 < 0` gives `<< fill^k r (log x)^4 Z^(2 alpha_1 - 1/2) = fill^k r (log x)^4 y^(-zeta (1/2 - 2 alpha_1)) = fill^k r (log x)^4 y^(-sigma)`.
- **Region C, with no L-function.** It holds at most `sum_(d < Z) phi(d) (2Z/d + 1) <= 3 Z^2` residues, `abs(S_P) <= y` for `mu` and `<= y log(2x)` for `Lambda`, and the two-position lemma gives `abs(hat w_k(a/y)) <= (1 - c)^(-1/2) fill^k y^(-gamma)` at every residue. So region C is at most `3 (1 - c)^(-1/2) fill^k Z^2 y^(-gamma) log(2x) = 3 (1 - c)^(-1/2) fill^k y^(2 zeta - gamma) log(2x)`, and `gamma - 2 zeta = sigma` by the choice of `zeta`. No Lemma A', no input (M), no Perron integral and no exceptional zero enters: the whole region, C1 and C2 together, is paid by the lemma.
- **Assembly.** The three regions give `abs(Sigma(P, k)) <<_eps fill^k r^(1 + eps) y^(-eta + eps) (log x)^6` with `r <= x^tau` and `y >= x^(1 - tau)`, and the blocks at these scales, at most `fill + 1` per scale, carry `(fill + 1) sum_(k < L) fill^k <= (fill + 1) fill^L <= (fill + 1) fill (A_F(x) + 1)`. So they weigh `<<_eps A_F(x) x^(tau (1 + eps) - (1 - tau)(eta - eps)) (log x)^6`, and at `tau = eta/4` the exponent is at most `-3 eta/4 + eta^2/4 + 2 eps <= -eta/2 + 2 eps` since `eta <= 1/5`; the discarded scales save `x^(-tau alpha_b) = x^(-eta alpha_b/4)`. Every `delta < alpha_b eta/4 = delta_0` follows on taking `eps` small, every step that asked `y` large holding for `x` past an effective point and the rest absorbed into `C`.
- **Where it holds. Proved.** At one excluded digit (W) holds at every `base >= 92317` through the step 3 constant, a half line by the proved floor of the ladder on [mobius](mobius.md); so the theorem is Proved at every one-missing-digit set from `base 92317`. At `m > 1` it holds wherever `PB_b(m) < fill b^(-4/5)`, which admits `m <= 176` at `base 10^7`; the wall itself is certified in interval arithmetic at 200 bits, `1/5 - alpha_1 >= 1.87123612 * 10^-8` at `92317` and `<= -4.82092732 * 10^-8` at `92316` (verb `lemma`).
- **Below `92317` on the scan. Verified.** On the float-scanned chord wall of [mobius](mobius.md) (W) holds from `base 39363` at every excluded digit and from `28352` at `e_0 in {0, b - 1}`, so the theorem holds there on that scan, which is never Proved.
- **The exponent. Verified.** `delta_0 >= 4.678 * 10^-9` at `base 92317` at `beta = 1/2` and at `beta = 0.2`, where region A binds through `1/5 - alpha_1 >= 1.871 * 10^-8`, and `delta_0 >= 3.47 * 10^-10` at `beta = 10^-3`, where region C binds; on the chord wall `delta_0 >= 6.741 * 10^-8` at `base 39363`, `beta = 1/2`; at `base 10^6`, `delta_0 >= 2.819 * 10^-4` at `beta = 1/2` and `4.163 * 10^-5` at `beta = 0.2`. Each is printed with `c` and `gamma` read at the extreme excluded digit, whose variance is the least, and floored at four digits. Region A binds only just above the wall: bisection between `92317` and `10^6` puts the switch to `sigma` at `100084` at `beta = 1/2` and at `93409` at `beta = 0.2`, and `sigma` binds at `10^6` at both (verb `lemma`).
- **The paper.** The theorem, the two-position lemma, the twisted peel and the corollary are written up for an outside reader as [A Power Saving for the Digit Sum on Missing Digits](../papers/digit-sum-twist-power-saving.md).

## Which region needs what

- **The ledger of inputs. Proved,** by the proof above set beside the dissection of [mobius](mobius.md).
- Region A: the certificate (W) through the peel at `s = 0` and the minor-arc exponent `4/5`, unchanged by the twist; it asks `alpha_1 < 1/5` and sets the wall.
- Region B: (W) through the transferred hybrid bound and the second approximation, unchanged; it asks `alpha_1 < 1/4`, and with `Z` a power of `y` its saving `Z^(2 alpha_1 - 1/2)` becomes a power.
- Region C, the old C1 and C2: only the two-position lemma, which asks two consecutive digits and `beta > 0`; in the untwisted dissection C1 needed Lemma A' of [coprime](coprime.md) and C2 needed the input (M) for `mu` or (M_Lambda) for `Lambda`, the zero-free regions of Chang and Martin and of Koukoulopoulos and the finite family of exceptional characters, and none of them is used.
- The blocks: depth `tau log_b x` in place of the `kappa sqrt(log x)` of [mobius](mobius.md), because no region now caps `Z` at `exp(C_0 sqrt(log x))`.
- The certificate: only the per-digit (W) serves, so the theorem's wall at one excluded digit is that of (W), `92317` by proof and `39363` on the float scan, against `584`, `301` and `115` for the untwisted bound on the chain and window certificates.
- So the twist trades the base for the shape: the untwisted bound is Proved from `base 584` and holds from `base 115` on the window certificates, at `exp(-c sqrt(log x))`; the twisted one is Proved from `base 92317`, at `x^(-delta)`.

## The lattice and the ceiling

- **On the lattice the twist is a character. Proved.** If `(b - 1) alpha` is an integer then `e(alpha s_b(n)) = e(alpha n)`, because `n - s_b(n)` is a multiple of `b - 1`; so `hat w_k(t) = hat F_k(t + alpha)`, the untwisted transform translated, and `M_F(x; alpha) = sum_(c mod b-1) e(alpha c) M_F(x; b - 1, c)`, a combination of the untwisted meters in the progressions mod `b - 1`, `M_F(x; b - 1, c)` summing `mu(n)` over the `n` of `S_F` up to `x` with `n = c mod b - 1`. Read through the dissection, the major arcs of that sum sit at `-alpha + l/b^j`, where region C2 needs the input (M) at moduli dividing `(b - 1) b^j`: the zero-free region returns, with the primes of `b - 1` added to the finite family of [mobius](mobius.md), and the section Progressions and every modulus carries that case at `exp(-c sqrt(log x))`.
- **Where the ceiling sits. Proved.** With two consecutive digits, `sup_t abs(hat w_k(t)) = fill^k` at every `k` when `alpha` is on the lattice and `sup_t abs(hat w_k(t)) <= (1 - c)^(-1/2) fill^k b^(-gamma k)` with `c > 0` when it is off, by the dichotomy bullet. The mechanism is the phase map: the phases `v_j = b^j t + alpha` obey `v_(j+1) = b v_j - (b - 1) alpha`, full mass needs every `v_j` integral, and the integers are invariant under that affine map exactly when `beta = 0`. At `alpha = 0` the frequencies of large mass are the `b`-adic preimages `l/b^j` of frequency `0`, the C2 set, and the input of zero-free-region strength in the untwisted dissection is used at C2 and nowhere else. Off the lattice those preimages carry no mass, and the power saving holds at every `alpha` with `beta >= beta_0 > 0`, uniformly.
- **What the statement does not say.** In the untwisted dissection C1 also caps the saving at `exp(-c sqrt(log x))`, through the rate `c'^(floor(2k/(3 m_d)))` of Lemma A' against the `3 Z^2` residues of the region, so the root-log ceiling there has two sources: the zero-free region, which sits exactly on the preimages of `0`, and the sup-times-count pricing of C1, which needs no L-function. The twist removes both at once. Whether the untwisted C1 can be priced at a power is open here.

## Primes of a design by digit sum

- **The corollary. Proved.** Let `F` keep two consecutive digits and satisfy (W) in its per-digit form `PB_b(m) < fill b^(-4/5)`, or at `m = 1` its chord form, and let `q >= 2` be coprime to `b - 1`. Then for every class `c mod q` and every `delta` below the `delta_0` of the theorem at `beta = 1/q`, `sum_(n in S_F, n <= x, s_b(n) = c mod q) Lambda(n) = psi_F(x)/q + O(A_F(x) x^(-delta))`, and `#{p in S_F : p <= x, s_b(p) = c mod q} = (1 + o(1)) pi_F(x)/q` with `pi_F(x)` the count of primes of `S_F` up to `x`: the primes of a missing-digit design equidistribute in the classes of their digit sum, Gelfond's question asked on the design. Proof: orthogonality mod `q` writes the left side as `q^(-1) sum_(r mod q) e(-r c/q) psi_F(x; r/q)`; the term `r = 0` is `psi_F(x)/q`, and at `r != 0` the number `(b - 1) r/q` is not an integer because `gcd(q, b - 1) = 1` and `q` does not divide `r`, so `norm((b - 1) r/q) >= 1/q` and the theorem applies with `beta = 1/q`. The prime count of [coprime](coprime.md), `psi_F(x) = kappa_F A_F(x) + O(A_F(x) exp(-c sqrt(log x)))`, holds under (W), and `kappa_F > 0` because the per-digit form of (W) forces `m < b^(2/5)` on [mobius](mobius.md) while `phi(b) >= sqrt(b)` at `b > 6`, so `F` holds a digit coprime to `b`. The bare inequality `B_b(F) <= fill b^(alpha_1)` does not suffice here: at `b = 6^15` the digits sharing a factor with `6` keep the pair `2, 3` and satisfy it through the triangle inequality on three Dirichlet kernels, yet hold no digit coprime to `b`, so their design holds no prime past `3`; the prime powers of `S_F` weigh at most `x^(1/2) (log x)^2`, below `A_F(x) x^(-delta)` since `alpha_b > 1/2 + delta`, and partial summation turns `Lambda` into the prime count.
- **The other moduli.** When `g = gcd(q, b - 1) > 1` the class of `s_b(p)` mod `g` is the class of `p` mod `g`, so the count asks for the primes of `S_F` in progressions mod `g`, the lattice case above; Mauduit and Rivat carry that case for all primes, with unrestricted digits, in their Theorem 3, and the bullet Gelfond at every modulus below carries it on the design from `base 92317`, with the error `exp(-c sqrt(log x))`.
- **The readings at base 10. Verified.** Base 10 lies far below the wall of (W), so these readings test the shape and not the theorem. Below `10^7` the design missing `7` holds `266823` primes; their digit sums deviate from `1/q` by at most `0.00825`, `0.00880` and `0.00503` of the class size at `q = 2, 4, 5`, but by `0.05348`, `0.10263` and `0.24272` at `q = 7, 8, 10`. The deviation is the design's and not the primes': the elements of the design coprime to `b (b - 1) = 90`, which carry the same mod `3` structure as the primes because `s_b(n) = n mod 3`, deviate by `0.04846`, `0.09911` and `0.23939` on the same moduli, and the primes sit within `0.02213` of that model at every `q` coprime to `9`. The design missing `0` reads the same way, `397866` primes, deviations `0.08164`, `0.14497`, `0.29072` at `q = 7, 8, 10` against the model's `0.07811`, `0.13695`, `0.29155`, primes within `0.02584` of the model. The slow modulus is the one with a small twist near the lattice: at `q = 10` the class `r = 3` sits at `alpha = 3/10 = 1/3 - 1/30`, and the twist `-1/30`, far from the lattice in `beta = 0.3` but small in `norm(alpha)`, mixes by `abs(g_F(1/30))/fill` per digit. At `q = 3` the class `0` holds one prime, `3` itself (verb `gelfond`).

## Against the literature

- **The digit sum of primes. Verified** at source. [Mauduit and Rivat 2010](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2010.171.1591), read in the published text, prove in Theorem 1 that `sum_(n <= x) Lambda(n) e(alpha s_q(n)) = O(x^(1 - sigma_q(alpha)))` with `sigma_q(alpha) > 0` whenever `(q - 1) alpha` is not an integer, and in Theorem 3 the count of primes with `s_q(p) = a mod m`, the main term `(gcd(m, q - 1)/m) pi(x; a, gcd(m, q - 1))`; the digits are unrestricted, `mu` is in none of their theorems, and their proof runs through Type I and Type II sums with a carry truncation, not through major arcs. [Martin, Mauduit and Rivat 2014](https://doi.org/10.4064/aa165-1-2), read at source in the authors' copy, extend Theorem 1 to `e(f(n) + beta n)` for every digital function `f(n) = sum_k alpha_k abs(n)_k` with real `alpha_k`, with the explicit exponent `c_q norm((q - 1) theta)^2` when the `alpha_k` form a progression, which they credit to Drmota, Mauduit and Rivat, and remark that `mu` follows by the same method; a real `f` makes `e(f(n))` unimodular, so the indicator of a missing-digit set is outside that class.
- **The mechanism is in print for the full digit set. Verified** at source. [Maynard 2019 survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.13402), read at source, proves a weak form of Mauduit-Rivat at large base by the circle method: its Lemma 8.4 (2) is the two-position lemma for the full digit set, `abs(hat g(theta)) << b^(k (1 - delta_(alpha, b)))` with `delta_(alpha, b) = norm((b - 1) alpha)^2/(4 b^4)`, by the same locating step `norm(b^i theta + alpha) + norm(b^(i+1) theta + alpha) >= norm((b - 1) alpha)/b`, and its Lemma 10.1, as printed, bounds by that supremum times their count the major arcs whose denominator has a prime outside `b`, while its proof of Theorem 2.4 applies the same supremum-times-count to every small denominator, the mechanism of region C of this page; it treats the missing-digit set and the digit sum as two separate theorems and never their product. So the shape of this page's argument is in print; what is written here is its object: the twist carried onto a missing-digit design, for `mu` and `Lambda`, with an effective constant, from an explicit base, and a two-position constant that does not decay with `b`.
- **The missing-digit side.** [Maynard 2019](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00222-019-00865-6) and [Maynard 2022](https://doi.org/10.1093/imrn/rnab002), as REFS.md records them, carry the primes of a missing-digit set and no digit-sum twist; the dissection of [mobius](mobius.md) came from them.
- **The search, stated as a search.** A web search on the digit sum of primes with missing, restricted or ellipsephic digits, the publication lists of Martin and of Rivat, and the reference lists of Mauduit-Rivat 2010 and of the survey found no theorem on the digit sum of primes, or of `mu`, restricted to a missing-digit set; the nearest found are [Aloui 2015](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10998-014-0066-8), read at the publisher page in its abstract only; its definition of `T_N(alpha, beta)` and its Theorems 2.1, 2.5 and 2.6 are known here through a machine summary of that page and through the citation in Saavedra-Araya, the full text unread, which carries Gelfond's theorem onto the integers of a missing-digit set and their `z`-free members with power-saving errors through the twisted transform `sum_(n in S_F, n < N) e(n theta + alpha s_b(n))`, with no prime and no Mobius sum, the integer neighbour of the corollary here; digit sums of ellipsephic palindromes ([Aloui, Mauduit and Mkaouar 2017](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10474-017-0688-4)), which are not primes, and [Saavedra-Araya 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07418), read at source in its abstract and Theorem A: for the integers of a missing-digit set and `gcd(g, a) = gcd(a, a') = 1`, the classes of `n mod a` and `s_g(n) mod a'` are jointly uniform exactly when `gcd(a a', d_2 - d_1, ..., d_t - d_1) = 1`, by Markov chains; integers only, no primes, the integer neighbour of the corollary here. This page is first in its object as far as that search reaches, and never in its shape.

## Progressions and every modulus

Off the lattice the twist decays; on it the twist is a character of `n mod b - 1`, so the lattice case is the design in progressions. This section proves the Mobius and prime sums of `S_F` in every class mod `N` for every `N` coprime to `b`, at root-log strength, and combines them with the power saving into the law of `s_b(p) mod q` at every `q`.

- **The theorem in progressions. Proved.** Let `F` keep two consecutive digits and carry the shifted-grid certificate of the unconditional dissection of [mobius](mobius.md), `sum_(a < b^i) abs(hat F_i(s + a/b^i)) <= C_F fill^i b^(i alpha_1)` at every `i` and every real `s`, `alpha_1 < 1/5`, and let `N >= 2` with `gcd(N, b) = 1`. Write `M_F(x; N, r)` and `psi_F(x; N, r)` for the sums of `mu(n)` and `Lambda(n)` over the `n` of `S_F` up to `x` with `n = r mod N`, and `kappa_F = (b/phi(b)) #{f in F : gcd(f, b) = 1}/fill` as on [coprime](coprime.md). Then there are `C, c > 0`, effective and depending on `b`, `F` and `N` alone, with `abs(M_F(x; N, r)) <= C A_F(x) exp(-c sqrt(log x))` at every `r`, and `abs(psi_F(x; N, r) - kappa_F A_F(x)/phi(N)) <= C A_F(x) exp(-c sqrt(log x))` at every `r` with `gcd(r, N) = 1`, while at `gcd(r, N) > 1` only powers of the primes of `N` count, at most `omega(N) (log x)^2`. At one missing digit the certificate holds from `base 584` ([mobius](mobius.md)), so the theorem is Proved there at every `N` coprime to `b`, `N = b - 1` among them; the bullets below are the proof.
- **Orthogonality and the blocks.** `M_F(x; N, r) = N^(-1) sum_(a mod N) e(-a r/N) M_F(x; a/N)` with `M_F(x; theta) = sum_(n in S_F, n <= x) mu(n) e(n theta)`. The block split of [mobius](mobius.md) is unchanged, and on a block `e((P y + t) theta) = e(P y theta) e(t theta)`, so its sum is `e(P y theta) y^(-1) sum_(a' mod y) hat F_k(theta + a'/y) S_P(-a'/y)`, by orthogonality on `Z/y` applied to `t -> 1_(D_k)(t) e(t theta)`, whose transform at `a'/y` is `hat F_k(theta + a'/y)`. `S_P` is the untwisted block sum, so the Dirichlet data `l/d`, `h` of `a'/y`, the cut into A, B and C, and the minor-arc bounds on `S_P` are those of the dissection verbatim; only the transform is translated by `theta`.
- **Regions A and B do not change.** Region A reads the certificate at the shift `theta` in place of `0`. The hybrid `l^1` bound reads its top factor on the grid `y theta/V_2 + a'/V_2`, a shifted grid, and its bottom factor on the intervals `theta + J_(l,d)`, still disjoint, through `L^1` norms of `hat F_(i_1)` and its derivative over a period, which a translation does not change. Both bounds hold with the same constants, since the certificate holds at every shift.
- **Region C re-cut: the major arcs move to `l/(N_a b^j)`.** Put `N_a = N/gcd(a, N)`. At a C residue `a'/y = l/d + eta`, `d < Z`, `abs(eta) = h/(d y) < Z/y`, the transform is read at `a/N + l/d + eta`. Write the reduced denominator of `a/N + l/d` as `d''`; then `d'' < N Z`. These are the frequencies where the translated transform can keep full mass: `hat F_k` at the `b`-adic fraction `l''/d''`. Partial summation of `e(n eta)` along the block gives `abs(S_P(a'/y)) <= (1 + 2 pi h/d) d max_(u, r') 2 abs(M(u; d, r')) <= 16 Z max_(u <= x, r') abs(M(u; d, r'))`, whether or not `d` divides `y`, and C2'' holds at most `3 Z N'_Z` residues, `N'_Z <= (1 + log_2 Z)^(omega(N b))` the number of `d < Z` dividing a power of `N b`. So the one new input is (M) at the moduli `d` dividing a power of `N b`, the lemma below, and the assembly of [mobius](mobius.md) runs with `c'`, `c_1` and the block depth read at `N b`.
- **The lemma (M_N). Proved.** Let `R = N b`. Proof. At `u <= x exp(-sqrt(log x))` both are trivial. Otherwise put `g = gcd(r, d)`; every `n = r mod d` has `gcd(n, d) = g`, so `M = 0` unless `g` is squarefree, and then `M(u; d, r) = mu(g) sum_c M(u/g; d_1, c)` over the classes `c mod d_1 = (d/g) s` with `c = r/g mod d/g` and `gcd(c, s) = 1`, `s` the product of the primes of `g` not dividing `d/g`: at most `rad(R)` classes, `d_1 <= d rad(R)` dividing a power of `R`; for `psi` at `g > 1` only powers of the primes of `R` count, at most `omega(R) log u`. Siegel's theorem is never used: the exceptional zeros lie on the real primitive characters of conductor dividing `8 rad(N b)`, the family of [mobius](mobius.md) with the primes of `N` added. The Mobius half is the proof of (M) on that page with `rad(base)` read as `rad(N b)` at each of its four uses: the classes `c`, the count of `w`, the conductor bound and the exceptional family; nothing else in it reads `base`. The von Mangoldt half is (M_Lambda) of [coprime](coprime.md) under the same swap, proved there through the exceptional term `u^(beta_1)` and its effective distance from `1`, and here through the region of Proposition 2.3, which excludes it; the two routes agree.
- **The von Mangoldt main term, through a model. Proved.** Put `lambda_R(n) = (R/phi(R)) 1_(gcd(n, R) = 1)`. - **The local constants split evenly. Proved.** On a block `P y + D_k` with `k >= 1`, `gcd(n, b) = 1` reads the last digit, so with `F*` the digits of `F` coprime to `b`, `#{t in D_k : gcd(t, b) = 1, P y + t = r mod N} = N^(-1) sum_(a mod N) e(-a (r - P y)/N) g_(F*)(a/N) prod_(1 <= j < k) g_F(a b^j/N)`. The term `a = 0` is `#F* fill^(k-1)/N`. At `a != 0`, `a b^j` is nonzero mod `N` because `gcd(b, N) = 1`, so `norm(a b^j/N) >= 1/N` and the pair form of the two-position lemma gives `abs(g_F(a b^j/N)) <= fill rho_N` with `rho_N = 1 - 4 sin^2(pi/(2N))/fill`. Over the at most `fill + 1` blocks per scale the errors sum to `<< (fill rho_N)^L << A_F(x) x^(-log(1/rho_N)/log b)`, and the blocks at scale `0` and the point `x` to `O(b)`. So the count is `(#F*/(fill N)) A_F(x)` up to a power saving, and since `R/phi(R) = (b/phi(b)) (N/phi(N))` the model is `kappa_F A_F(x)/phi(N) + O(A_F(x) x^(-delta_N))`. The design is uniform mod `N` in the limit whatever its digits share with `N`: the consecutive pair puts `1` among the digit differences, the condition of [Saavedra-Araya 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07418), Theorem A. At `N = b - 1` the rate is exact: `hat F_k(a/(b - 1)) = g_F(a/(b - 1))^k`, since `b^j = 1 mod b - 1`.
