# carry-free-mobius

- The design Mobius `nu_F` of the two-digit design `F = {0,1}` is base-free: it is the pushforward of one function `nu*` on the monoid `M*` of products of `0/1` polynomials in `Z[x]`, and the base `q` enters only as the order in which `nu*` is summed.
- `M*` is the multiplicative monoid generated by the nonzero `0/1` polynomials, `S*` is that generating set, and `nu*` is the Dirichlet inverse of `1_(S*)` on `Z[x]`, which is a unique factorisation domain; evaluation `P -> P(q)` is a bijection `S* -> S_F` and a monoid homomorphism with finite fibres at every `q >= 2`, since an element of `M*` has nonnegative coefficients so `P(q) = n` caps every coefficient by `n` and `deg P` by `log_q n`, and therefore `nu_F(n) = sum_(P in M*, P(q) = n) nu*(P)`.
- `lemma`: the carry bound. The maximum coefficient over `M*` at degree below `L` against the proved cap `2^(L-1)`; the carry-free window `q_set(L)`, least `q` with every `P` of degree below `L` under `q^L`, against its closed form `(q+1)^(L-1) < q^L`; the order window `q_ord(L)`, least `q` with evaluation increasing on all of `M*` to degree `L`, and the overtaking pair that breaks each; the pushforward checked term for term against the design Mobius of [mrly-pairing](../mrly-pairing/README.md) verb `inverse`, with the base 2 full digit set as the control where `nu_F = mu`; and the first departing level of the base-`q` ladder against the window.
- `sequence`: the base-free ladder of `nu*`, its running maxima and its level sums by level, the census of `M*` by degree and its partial sums, the maximum coefficient at the deepest degree against the packing width, and an OEIS grep against a local dump named by the environment variable `OEIS_STRIPPED`.
- `exponent`: the growth RATE of the base-free running maximum against the design's mass rate `2`, the ratio `max/2^L`, the per-level step, the exponent `log_2(max)/L`, the same ratio split by the residue class of `L` mod `2`, and geometric mean steps over the last `4`, `6` and `8` levels.
- Domain: `M*` to degree `17`, which is `2423801` elements and `1357535` at the top degree, for the ladder and the exponent; `M*` to degree `14` for the bound table; the design Mobius to `3^16`, `4^12`, `5^11`, `6^9` and `7^8`.

## RUN

- `uv run python research/lab/carry-free-mobius/carry_free.py lemma`
- `uv run python research/lab/carry-free-mobius/carry_free.py sequence`
- `uv run python research/lab/carry-free-mobius/carry_free.py exponent`
- `lemma` runs in ten seconds, `sequence` and `exponent` in seventeen each; peak resident memory is `0.60` GB in `lemma` and `0.58` GB in the other two.
- A `0/1` polynomial is a bitmask, so every element of `M*` is packed one coefficient to thirty-two bits and multiplication of polynomials is multiplication of integers; the packing is safe because the proved coefficient cap at degree below `18` is `2^17`, far under `2^32`, so no product ever carries, and the generator prints the coefficient it actually reaches.
- The packed integer is `P(2^32)`, so its order is the base-free order and its divisibility is polynomial divisibility.

## WITNESSES

- the pushforward is exact term for term with `0` mismatches at base 2 to `n <= 2^15`, base 3 to `n <= 3^10`, base 4 to `n <= 4^8` and base 5 to `n <= 5^7`, where `108978` elements of `M*` collapse onto `32768` integers at base 2 and `76210` values collide, and `104` values collide at base 3; the base 2 line is the full digit set, where `nu_F = mu`, so the classical Mobius function is the pushforward of `nu*` at `q = 2`
- the degree-graded sums of `nu*` are `1, -2, 0, 0, ...`: the level sums read `0` at level `1` and `-1` at every level `2` to `18`, so `sum_(n <= q^L) nu_F(n) = -1` at every `L >= 2` inside a base's carry-free window
- the running maxima of `nu*` read `1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 15, 23, 45, 86, 162, 331, 741, 1665, 3173, 7508, 17753, 36147` at levels `1..18` and are absent from the local OEIS dump
- the census of `M*` by degree reads `1, 2, 5, 11, 27, 61, 144, 331, 776, 1788, 4147, 9544, 22000, 50420, 115407, 263062, 598540, 1357535` at degrees `0..17`, its partial sums `1, 3, 8, 19, 46, 107, 251, 582, 1358, 3146, 7293, 16837, 38837, 89257, 204664, 467726, 1066266, 2423801`, and both are absent from the same dump
- the maximum coefficient over `M*` at degree below `L` reads `1, 2, 3, 6, 10, 20, 35, 70, 126, 252, 462, 924, 1716, 3432` at `L = 2..15` and `24310` at degree `17`, which is `binomial(L-1, floor((L-1)/2))` term for term at those `L` and at that degree, far under the proved cap `2^(L-1)`
- the maximum of `P(q)` over `M*` at degree below `L` is exactly `(q+1)^(L-1)`, attained by `(1+x)^(L-1)`, because a `0/1` polynomial of degree `d` has `P(q) <= 1 + q + ... + q^d <= (1+q)^d` and degrees add over a product; so the carry-free window `q_set(L)` is the least `q` with `(q+1)^(L-1) < q^L` for every `L`, and it reads `3, 3, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 6, 6, 6, 7, 7` at `L = 3..14` where the generator enumerates it directly
- the order window `q_ord(L)` reads `3, 3, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 6, 6, 7, 7, 8` at `L = 3..14` and is strictly larger than `q_set` at `L = 12` and `L = 14`, where the first inversion is between two elements of the same degree, `(1+x^2)^6 = 2565726409` before `x^11 + x^12 = 2539579392` at base 6 and `(1+x^2)^7 = 781250000000` before `x^13 + x^14 = 775112083256` at base 7
- the carry-free windows are `4`, `7`, `9`, `12` and `15` at bases `3, 4, 5, 6, 7` and the level sum departs from `-1` at exactly `5`, `8` and `10` at the three bases where the departure is reachable, one level past the window at each, while bases 6 and 7 hold `-1` to the depth measured
- the first overtaking pair is `(1+x)^4 = 256` over `3^5 = 243` at base 3, `(1+x)^7 = 78125` over `4^8 = 65536` at base 4 and `(1+x)^9 = 10077696` over `5^10 = 9765625` at base 5
- the running maximum is a blunter statistic than the level sum and survives past the window: it departs at level `9` at base 3, at level `11` at base 4 and nowhere to level `11` at base 5, so `1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 15, 23, 45, 86` is shared by base 4 and base 5 although base 4 leaves the carry-free window at level `8`
- the base-free running maximum stays far under the design mass at every level measured, `max/2^L` falling to `0.079102` at `L = 11` and rising in both residue classes of `L` mod `2` only to `0.137890` at `L = 18`; it is the RATE that runs ahead, the geometric mean step reading `2.158562`, `2.186288` and `2.127878` over the last `4`, `6` and `8` levels against the mass rate `2`, while the measured `log_2(max)/L` climbs `0.697557, 0.733333, 0.764379, 0.775442, 0.804638, 0.830340, 0.841199` at `L = 12..18` and has not settled
- the step sits under `q^(Re rho) = 2.207512` at base 3 `{0,1}`, which is a bracket and not a comparison of one object with itself: base 3 leaves the base-free ladder at level `9`, reading `38` against `45`, so past level `8` the two orders differ
