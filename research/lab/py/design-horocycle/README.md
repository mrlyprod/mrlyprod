# design-horocycle

- The design horocycle of [stack](../../../notes/stack.md), `## The design horocycle`: a digit design `F` moved from the orbit onto the gcd weight, the design Eisenstein series `Theta_F f = sum_g a_F(g) psi_(f_g)` with `a_F = (mu 1_(S_F)) * 1`, its closed horocycle integral against the design novelty `nu_F = (mu 1_(S_F)) * id`, and the design meter `E_F(y) = y^2 sum_c nu_F(c) f(cy) - F(2) Z_F(2)` read against the design's own mass `A_F(1/y)`.
- `mobius` sieves `mu` to `N = 2^24` in `int8`; `totients` sieves `phi` to the same bound for the full-set control; `design` enumerates `S_F` to `N` digit by digit.
- `convolve_id` forms `nu = (w 1_(S_F)) * id` for a weight `w` on the squarefree elements: slices for `d <= sqrt N`, and for larger `d` a loop over the cofactor `e` with one vectorised scatter per `e`; checked against the divisor definition at every `c <= 300` on two designs.
- `bridge` integrates `Theta_F f` over the closed horocycle `x + ih`, `0 <= x < 1`, pair by pair over every lattice pair `(c, d)` with weight `a_F(gcd(c, d))`, by 80-node Gauss-Legendre on the two arcs where `f(h/|cz + d|^2)` is nonzero, at `h = 4^-k`, `k = 2..6`, against `h sum_c nu_F(c) g(c h^(1/2))` with `g` the transform of `lab/py/ford-horocycle`, printing the weighted pairs and the arcs they contribute. The two routes share the 80-node rule, and on every arc not clipped at `0` or `1` the lattice arc maps onto the interval of `g` node for node by `v = (x + d/c)/h`, so the agreement tests the pair bookkeeping, the gcd weight and the sum over `d mod c`, and not the quadrature. It evaluates `Theta_F f` by direct lattice summation at 5 seeded points and at 4 images of each under `SL(2, Z)`, prints the partial sums of `Z_F(1)` and `Z_F(2)` to `2^24`, and at base 3 bounds the tail by the exact reciprocal sum of the `16384` sixteen-digit elements above `2^24` plus `sum_(L > 16) (2/3)^(L-1)` for the longer ones, asserted below the radius `sum_(L > 16) 2^L/3^(L-1)` it prints the interval with, and reads the `h^(1/2)` coefficient `(C_F(h) - (pi/2) Z_F(2) F(-1))/h^(1/2)` at `k = 4..12` against the certified interval of `-(1/2) Z_F(1) F(-1/2)`, with the full set as the control where the term is absent.
- `meter` uses two balanced windows, the `C^infinity` bump `exp(4 - 1/((u-1)(2-u)))` and the `C^2` bump `64 (u-1)^3 (2-u)^3`, each multiplied by `u - u_1` with `u_1` chosen so that `F(2) = 0`, which removes the constant `F(2) Z_F(2)` and with it every tail beyond the sieve. The grid is `y = 2^-j`, `j` in `[8, 23]` in steps of `1/16`, 241 samples, windows `(1/y, 2/y]` inside `(2^8, 2^24]`.
- Three designs, base 3 `{0,1}`, base 5 `{0,1,2}` and base 10 missing `7`, and three weights on each: `mu`, a seeded random sign on the squarefree support, and `|mu|`. `E_F` is computed at `j = 12, 14, 16` a second way, as `y^2 sum_(d in S_F) mu(d) R(dy)` with `R(u) = sum_e e f(eu)`, sharing no loop with the convolution.
- The fitted slope is the least-squares slope of `log` of the per-octave root mean square of `|E_F|/y^2` against `log` of the octave's geometric mean of `A_F(1/y)`, over 15 octaves and over the lower and upper halves; the full set reads the same slope against `1/y` with `nu = phi`. The summary also prints the pointwise band of `abs(E_F)/(y^2 A_F(1/y)^(1/2))` and its sign changes. The base-phase test bins the residual of each sample from its fitted line by `j log 2/log base mod 1` into 8 bins.

## RUN

`uv run python research/lab/py/design-horocycle/design_horocycle.py` from `mrlyprod/`, verbs `bridge`, `meter`, `all` (default). `bridge` takes 4 seconds and `meter` 28 seconds on one core; `meter` peaks at 3.9 GB resident, measured with `/usr/bin/time -l`.

- stack.md `## The design horocycle`, The meter read: every number of that paragraph, the `SUMMARY` lines and the per-octave table.

## NOTE

- The meter is a smoothed rerun of the Mobius census, `E_F(y)/y^2 = sum_(d in S_F) mu(d) R_f(dy)`, not evidence independent of it; its slope is a fit on 15 octaves of an oscillating sum; residuals reach `0.41` in `log` at base 3, where the lower and upper halves read `0.18` and `0.89`. No exponent is read from it.
- The base-phase null is one random draw; it is a reading and not a test.
