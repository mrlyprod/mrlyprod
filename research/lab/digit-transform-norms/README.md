# Digit Transform Norms

- The `l^1` norms of a design's digit transform in `D` dimensions: the two numbers a missing-digit sieve reads out of a set, computed for the gasket and the carpet on the 2D grid and for their Morton codes at base `q^D`, and calibrated against the published one-dimensional constants.
- The object is `hat F(t) = (1/k) Sum_(v in F) e(<t, v>)` on `T^D` and its level-`L` product `hat F_L(t) = Prod_(j<L) hat F(q^j t)`, the normalised transform of `S_L`; the norms are `I_L = Int_(T^D) |hat F_L|`, the shifted grid sum `Sigma_N(x) = Sum_(i in (Z/q^N)^D) Prod_(j<N) |hat F(q^j (x + i/q^N))|`, and the exponents `alpha_1 = D + lim (1/L) log_q I_L` and `alpha_1*`, the exponent of the sup-over-box sum a large sieve consumes.
- The threshold throughout is `D/2`: below it a Farey-point large sieve saves a power of the level, at or above it the same route saves nothing.
- Conventions: `Y = q^L` is the side and `X = q^(DL)` the box, every exponent printed here is in `Y` units, and a one-dimensional exponent `27/77` in `X` units is the same statement as `D x 27/77` in `Y` units.

## WHAT IT COMPUTES

- The one-digit sum. For a design that is the full residue cube less one vector, `Sum_(v in F) e(<t, v>/q) = q^D [t = 0] - e(<u, t>/q)`, so `|hat F(t/q)|` is `1` at `t = 0` and `1/k` at the other `q^D - 1` points and `Sum_(t in (Z/q)^D) |hat F(t/q)| = 2` exactly, for every base and every dimension; the row prints the exact `2`, the scan that confirms it, and `log_q 2`, which is `1` for the gasket and `0.630930` for the carpet against the base-10 truth `27/77 = 0.350649`, above it in one case and below it in the other, so the per-digit number is arithmetic and not a bound.
- The direct grid sums `Sigma_L(0)` and their exponents at `L = 1..10`. A finite-level grid sum is neither a floor nor a ceiling on `alpha_1`; the table is a trend, and the base-10 rows are its control.
- The sandwich. Substituting `t = (i + y)/q^N` in `I_(N+M)` gives `q^(-DN) min_x Sigma_N(x) I_M <= I_(N+M) <= q^(-DN) max_x Sigma_N(x) I_M`, hence `(1/N) log_q min_x Sigma_N <= alpha_1 <= (1/N) log_q max_x Sigma_N` for every `N`, and `Sigma_N` has period `q^(-N)` in each coordinate, so the scan runs over one cell.
- The slack. `|hat F(t) - hat F(t')| <= Lip |t - t'|_inf` with `Lip = (2 pi/k) Sum_(v in F) |v|_1`, since the derivative in coordinate `c` is `(2 pi i/k) Sum_v v_c e(<t, v>)`; the factor at digit `j` moves at `q^j Lip`, the product of factors bounded by `1` moves at their sum, and there are `q^(DN)` terms, so `Lip(Sigma_N) <= Lip (q^N - 1)/(q - 1) q^(DN)`, and a grid of `m^D` shifts on the cell leaves half a step `h/2 = 1/(2 q^N m)`. `Lip` is `4 pi/3` for the gasket and `4 pi` for the carpet; the slack is derived, never fitted.
- The window bound. `G(w)` is the supremum of `|hat F|` over the box of side `2 q^(-n_d)` behind a window `w` of `n_d` digit-vectors, bounded above by a sub-scan of `m^D` cell centres plus `Lip h/2`; the transfer matrix carries a window to its `q^D` successors with weight `G`, and `Sum_a sup_box |hat F_L| << lambda^L` with `lambda` its Perron root, so `alpha_1* <= log_q lambda`. The moment version raises `G` to a power `s` and reads `g(s) = log_q lambda(s, n_d)`.
- The criterion. A pair `(b, a_0)` carries the one-dimensional sieve when `g(s) < (1/5)(1 + c/2)(2 - s)` for some `s` in `[3/2, 2)`, `c = log(b-1)/log b`; the rows print `g`, the criterion and the margin at `s = 1, 3/2, 235/154, 1.6, 1.7, 1.8`.
- The contraction rate. For `gcd(d, q) = 1` the per-digit rate `max_t Prod_(l < ord_d(q)) |hat F(q^l t/d)|^(1/ord_d(q))` over every nonzero `t` in `(Z/d)^D`, against `c(q,k)^(1/m_d)` with `m_d = floor(log_q(d/2)) + 1` computed by an integer loop, and against the weaker `c(q,k)^(1/ord_d(q))`.
- The entropy floor. `Int_(T^D) log|hat F(q^j t)| dt` is independent of `j` and Jensen gives `I_L >= exp(L Int log|hat F|)`, hence `alpha_1 >= D + (1/log q) Int_(T^D) log|hat F|`; for the gasket the inner integral is closed by Jensen's formula, `Int_0^1 log|a + e(u)| du = log max(|a|, 1)`, leaving `2 Int_0^(1/3) log(2 cos(pi u)) du = 0.323065947219`, so the soft bound reaches `0.881123` only and a certificate is not decoration.

## THE CERTIFICATE

- Certified rows are interval arithmetic with directed rounding, not floats with a margin. Every argument of the scan is an integer multiple of `1/Q`, so a table of `Q` enclosures of `cos(2 pi j/Q)` is built once with `mpmath.iv` at 96 bits and each endpoint pushed one ulp outward.
- `k^2 |hat F(t)|^2 = Sum_(d in F - F) mult(d) cos(2 pi <d, t>)` is a table lookup per difference; every add, multiply, square root and divide rounds outward through `nextafter`, so the printed pair encloses the true value. The kernel is checked against 40-digit `mpmath` on 1200 sampled arguments, no point outside its enclosure and the widest enclosure `1.5e-08`.
- The gasket rows scan `min_x Sigma_N` and `max_x Sigma_N` over `m^D` shifts, subtract and add the slack, and print `alpha_1 >` truncated down and `alpha_1 <` rounded up, each digit checked against `q^(N e)` before it prints.
- The carpet row bounds the Perron root by the Collatz-Wielandt test: any positive `w` with `A^T w <= mu w` componentwise gives `rho(A) <= mu`, so a float power iteration supplies `w` and one certified pass supplies `mu`; the unweighted row sum, which is the same bound at `w = 1`, prints beside it and does not reach the threshold at any window length reached here.
- Safe rounding everywhere: lower bounds truncate down, upper bounds round up, and no digit is printed past what the enclosure establishes.
