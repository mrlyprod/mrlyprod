# Mobius and primes on the Kummer sets

At a prime `p >= 5` the integers `k` with `p` not dividing `C(3k, k)` form a two-state carry automaton and not a digit design; at every prime `p > a` the Kummer set `{k : p does not divide C(ak, bk)}` keeps exactly `((p+1)/2)^L` of the first `p^L` integers and its transform is a product of `b(a - b)`-square matrices of digit kernels, `2 x 2` at `(3, 1)`; there a matrix transfer certificate clears `1/5` from `p = 13567831`, and from there the Mobius function cancels on the set and its primes are counted with constant `p/(p+1)`.
