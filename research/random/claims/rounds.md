# The rounds

- 2026-10-26 [Proved] Every design round `R = t_c L F` on the `n x n` torus is a bijection of the `2^N` grids with its Feistel step `F` an involution and undo `F L^-1 t_c`, and it is an even permutation whenever it writes `|A| >= 2` cells and `N >= 3`; with `|A| = 1` its sign is `(-1)^w`, `w` the weight of the one mess bit, so rounds and stamps generate a subgroup of `A_(2^N)` unless some round writes one cell with an odd-weight mess; a cell reading `k` of the cells of `B` has `w = 2^(|B| - k) sum_{s in S} C(k, s)`, odd only when it reads all of `B`. Witness: ../lab/rs/rounds, verb all, bijection, involution, undo, sign and weight checked on every state of 7176 catalog cells, 0 faults; the carpet at `3 x 3` reads all 8 cells of `B`, `sum_{s in S} C(8, s)` is 148, 107, 150, 128 for primes, binary, fibonacci, odds, and the odd rounds are exactly the 9 single-cell `binary` carpet cells.
- 2026-10-26 [Proved] A uniform even permutation of `M >= 2` points has mean cycle count `H_M + (-1)^M / (M (M - 1))`, and for `M >= 3` mean fixed-point count exactly 1; both checked over every even permutation of `M = 2..8` points (A001710). Witness: ../lab/rs/rounds.
- 2026-10-26 [Proved] Five design laws each force the group `<T, h>` of a round and every stamp to differ from `A_(2^N)`: a read-closed spread-invariant cell set, a proper spread-invariant module of the mess classes, a self-complementary rule, a kept parity of a cell partition (each a block system by the subspace criterion of Caranti, Dalla Volta and Sala 2009), and a coset partition permuted by the spread with every read inside its part (a product action). Witness: ../lab/rs/rounds.
- 2026-10-26 [Verified] In both catalogs no primitive cell meets law I, II, III or IV, and no primitive product or affine cell shows a lone prime cycle in its 512 stamped rounds and 512 random words; the product law, run on every cell, holds in 491 nonlinear and 28 `odds` cells at `3 x 3` and in 926 and 256 at `4 x 4`, and the 12 primitive `odds` cells at `3 x 3` meet none of laws I to V. Witness: ../lab/rs/rounds, verbs three and four, 0 faults.
