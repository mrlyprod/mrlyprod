# The major arcs

- 2026-10-30 [Proved] The major arcs cut to the frequencies whose denominator divides `Q` are the local model: `sum_(c mod Q) (G_k(-c, Q)/Q) e(cm/Q) = rho_Q(m) = #{r mod Q: r^k = m (mod Q)}` for every integer `m`, so `sum_(q | Q) sum_(a mod q, (a,q)=1) (G_k(-a,q)/q) sum_(m<M) x(m) e(am/q) w(m) = sum_(m<M) x(m) rho_Q(m) w(m)`, with `G_k(a, q) = sum_(r mod q) e(a r^k/q)`. Witness: ../notes/major-arcs.md.
- 2026-10-30 [Proved] For `B = b^t` and a point `gamma = c/q` with `q | B - 1`, the frequencies `alpha` with `B^J alpha = gamma (mod 1)` sum to `(G_k(-c, q)/q) e(m gamma) rho_(B^J)(m)`, so the tree of `gamma` cut at depth `J` contributes exactly `(G_k(-c,q)/q) sum_m x(m) e(m gamma) rho_(B^J)(m) w(m)`: a Gauss sum at the orbit point times the design twisted by `gamma` summed against the `B`-adic local density. Witness: ../notes/major-arcs.md.
- 2026-10-30 [Verified] Replacing the depth-`J` tree by `K_J U(gamma; M)`, `K_J = E_(n mod B^J) prod_(i<J) y(digit_i(n^k))/phi(0)` and `y(t) = x(t) e(t gamma)`, costs an absolute error that is nearly constant in `M` and grows with `J`: for Thue-Morse at `1/3` and `M = 2^24` it is `0.386`, `2.331` and `4.230` at `J = 1, 4, 8`, which is `2.0e-3`, `7.2e-3` and `1.1e-2` of the tree, against `2.5e-2`, `8.1e-2` and `9.9e-2` at `M = 2^12`. The arc term `(G(-c,q)/q) K U(gamma; N^k)`, `K = lim_J K_J`, is therefore the model's definition, not a theorem. Witness: ../lab/rs/major-arcs.
- 2026-10-30 [Proved] For `B = p^t`, `K = (1 - 1/p) sum_(j<t) p^(-j) r_0^floor(kj/t) E[R(p^(kj mod t) w)] / (1 - r_0^k/B)`, `w` uniform on the `k`-th power units of `Z_p`, `r_0 = B/sigma`, convergent iff `eta(gamma) < 1/k`. For Thue-Morse at `1/3`, `K = (4/3)(1 - e(1/3))` and `A = (4/3) e(-1/3)`, so the period-2 model of `S(N) = sum_(n<N) t(n^2)`, its only arcs below `1/4` of period 1 or 2, is `1 + (4/3) sum_(1<=m<N^2) t(m) cos(2 pi (m - 1)/3)/sqrt(m)`. Witness: ../lab/rs/major-arcs.
