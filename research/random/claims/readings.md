- 2026-10-29 [Proved] A monomial reading keeps the dilation identity of its design: if `x(bm) = x(m)` at every `m`, as for Thue-Morse `t` and Rudin-Shapiro `r` at `b = 2` and the ternary parity `t3` at `b = 3`, the reading `y(n) = x(c n^k)` has `y(bn) = y(n)` at every `n`, so its dilation test at `(b, 0)`, its kernel test and its dilation square return exactly `1`; the battery at `N = 2^15` reads `t(n^2)` as mean `-0.0150`, shift `0.0280` at `h = 15`, dilation `1.0000` at `(2, 0)`, kernel `1.0000`, square `+1.0000` and LZ78 `1.214` bits per bit at `2^18` against the control's `1.215`, though `t(n^2)` is normal (Drmota, Mauduit and Rivat 2019). - 2026-10-29 [Proved] Rigidity: for `P` of degree `k >= 2` and `chi(u) = lambda u + mu` with rational `lambda > 0`, `lambda != 1`, `P(chi(u)) - lambda^k P(u)` is constant if and only if `P = q (u - u_0)^k + q_0` with `u_0 = mu/(1 - lambda)`; so a battery pair of a polynomial reading is a near-head at base `b`, `P(psi(n)) = b^L (P(phi(n)) + q) + s` with `0 <= s < b^L`, exactly when `P` is a monomial plus a constant centred at the fixed point of `psi phi^(-1)`: `-r/(B - 1)` for the dilation `(B, r)`, `B` a power of `b`, and `0` for the kernel pair; and for `b` prime such a reading of a signed design keeps `y(phi^(j+1) n) = +-y(phi^j n)` at every `n >= max(0, u_0)` for every large `j` along the integer contraction `phi(n) = lambda n + (1 - lambda) u_0`, `lambda` a power of `b`, which exists exactly when the denominator of `u_0` is prime to `b`. Witness: ../notes/readings.md.
- 2026-10-30 [Proved] Near-head limits: at a near-head of `t` or `t3` the raw mean of `y(psi(n)) y(phi(n))` tends to `x(s) kappa`, `kappa` the limit of the mean of `x(m + q) x(m)` along `m = P(phi(n))`, which exists and lies within `eps_J` of the sum over the classes of `n` modulo `b^J` whose addition stays inside `J` digits, `eps_J -> 0` because the roots of a polynomial in the `b`-adic integers have measure `0`, and for `r` the boundary factor `(-1)^((m + q)_0 s_(L-1))` stays inside the class sum, so `r` along `n` at `(2, 1)` has mean `0`, not `r(1) = 1`; of the 143 near-head battery pairs of 13 polynomial readings of `t`, `r` and `t3`, 91 are exact, `+-1`, 29 lie strictly between `0` and `1` and 23 are `0`, every raw mean at `N = 2^22` (`3^14` at base 3) inside its enclosure; `t` along `n^2 + 4` reads `-1/2` at `(2, 0)`, a twisted identity of period 4, and the pair law `x(n^2 + c) x(n^2)` along the squares reads `0, -1, 1, -1/2, -1/2, 1/2, 1/6, -2/3` for `t` and `-1/3, -1/3, 1/3, 1/3, -5/9, -5/9, -1/9, 1/3` for `t3` at `c = 1..8`, exact for `t` at `c <= 6` and for `t3` at `c = 1`, the others within `1.40e-7` of the values shown at `J = 26` digits (`16` at base 3), within `7.95e-8` for `t` at `c = 7, 8`.
