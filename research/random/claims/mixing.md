# The mixing

- 2026-10-26 [Proved] With a uniform stamp on all of `F_2^N` after every key-independent round `h_t`, the difference of two inputs after round 1 is a fixed nonzero difference that takes every value as the inputs vary, and from there a Markov chain with kernels `P_t(a, b) = #{y : h_t(y) + h_t(y + a) = b} / 2^N`, each doubly stochastic with the uniform law on the nonzero differences stationary; the distance of the output pair from a uniform pair of distinct grids is the distance of the difference from uniform, so `d(r) = max over a != 0 of TV(e_a P_2 ... P_r, U)`. Witness: ../lab/rs/mixing.
- 2026-10-26 [Proved] For a schedule of period `k` with period product `Q`, `d(1 + k s) <= (1/2) sqrt(2^N - 1) sigma_2(Q)^s`; the chain of a round is irreducible exactly when the group generated by the round and all stamps is 2-transitive, and a reducible chain keeps `d(r) >= 1 - |C| / (2^N - 1)` forever for a closed class `C`, so imprimitive cells and primitive products never mix. Witness: ../lab/rs/mixing.
- 2026-10-26 [Proved] Every design round that writes two cells or more has `sigma_2(P) = 1`, because a difference supported on the fill passes as a point mass; `sigma_2(P^j) = 1` once two cells `p` have `p, sigma(p), ..., sigma^(j-1)(p)` all in the fill, and `d(j + 1) = 1 - 1/(2^N - 1)`, the largest distance any law on the nonzero differences has from uniform, once one cell does, its start being a point mass after `j` steps; so `d(2) = 1 - 1/(2^N - 1)` for every round that writes a cell. Witness: ../lab/rs/mixing.
- 2026-10-26 [Refuted] The guess that `sigma_2(P) = 1` exactly when the group of the round is imprimitive: all 147 alternating `3 x 3` cells, which are primitive and 2-transitive, have `sigma_2(P) = sigma_2(P^2) = 1`, and `k*`, the least `j` with `sigma_2(P^j) < 1`, is 3, 5 or 6. Witness: ../lab/rs/mixing.
- 2026-10-26 [Proved] An affine round has a permutation matrix as kernel, so its difference is deterministic and `d(r) = 1 - 1/(2^N - 1)` for every `r`; its chain is irreducible, and its group 2-transitive, exactly when the linear part permutes the nonzero vectors in one cycle. Witness: ../lab/rs/mixing.
- 2026-10-26 [Verified] Of the 4320 `3 x 3` cells, exactly 147 nonlinear cells (the alternating cells of the round census) and 12 affine cells (its 12 primitive affine cells, `odds` with `net` under `shift` and `drop` and with `void` under `slide`) have an irreducible chain, the 12 affine ones never mixing; every reducible nonlinear cell keeps `d >= 0.500978`; 0 kernel rows differ from the direct table, and the dense numpy rebuild finds the same 147 and 12. Witness: ../lab/rs/mixing, ../lab/rs/mixing/dense.py.

Witness: ../lab/rs/mixing, ../lab/rs/mixing/dense.py.

- 2026-10-26 [Proved] Three inputs give a doubly stochastic chain on the ordered pairs of distinct nonzero differences whose first coordinate lumps to the 2-wise chain, so every eigenvalue of `P` is one of `P_3` and `sigma_2(P_3^j) >= sigma_2(P^j)`. Witness: ../lab/rs/mixing.
- 2026-10-26 [Conjecture] Three inputs mix at the 2-wise rate: on five alternating `3 x 3` cells the 3-wise chain on 260610 states has `sigma_2(P_3^(k*)) = sigma_2(P^(k*))` to six digits and `abs lambda_2` within `3e-3`, and its distance from eight starts trails the 2-wise one by at most three rounds; Lanczos gives lower estimates only. Witness: ../lab/rs/mixing.
- 2026-10-26 [Verified] Stamps drawn from the three neighbourhood designs `{495, 186, 341}` break the Markov property on `0x004a` net primes shift: uniform stamps give the kernel exactly, the designs put the law of `d_2` given `d_1` at distance 0.1667 from it from a void start, and from a uniform pair the law of `d_3` given `(d_1, d_2) = (0x084, 0x101)` at distance 0.5 from the law given `d_2` alone. Witness: ../lab/rs/mixing.
- 2026-10-26 [Proved] The light cone: if every round reads within `l_infinity` distance `m` and its spread maps balls of radius `t` into balls of radius `t + s`, then `d(r) >= 1 - (2^(w^2) - 1)/(2^(n^2) - 1)` with `w = min(n, 2(m + s)(r - 1) + 1)`, so a local schedule needs at least `1 + (n - 1)/(2(m + s))` rounds to reach `1/2`; every catalog spread and every translation at any scale has `s = 0`; every schedule whose spreads permute the cells needs `log_9(n^2)` cone rounds; and a single-spread cone that reaches a fixed set short of the torus is a read-closed spread-invariant set, law I; a cone can also stall and move, as in the product cell `0x0054` void primes shift, which never covers and has no law-I set. Witness: ../lab/rs/mixing.
- 2026-10-26 [Verified] The exact cone of the level-`L` carpet covers the torus, counting rounds after the first with the support map applied once a round, in 8, 15, 30, 61, 123 rounds under `slide` at `L = 2..6`, every start, the board `{i + j even}` in 5, 9, 17, 33, 65 under `shift` and in 4, 6, 7, 8, 10 under `hyper (2i + j + 1, 3i + 2j)`; the digit rotation and the cat map are certified never to cover (a start in a closed set). Witness: ../lab/rs/mixing, ../figures/research-random-mixing.ts.

- 2026-10-26 [Proved] With stamps drawn from a set of `k` grids, the output pair of two fixed inputs after `r` rounds takes at most `k^r` values, one for each stamp sequence, which the uniform law on the `2^N (2^N - 1)` ordered pairs of distinct grids weighs at most `k^r / (2^N (2^N - 1))`, so the worst pair's distance from that law is `d(r) >= 1 - k^r / (2^N (2^N - 1))` whatever the rounds. Witness: ../notes/mixing.md.
