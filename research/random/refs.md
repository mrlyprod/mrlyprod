| Coppersmith and Grossman 1975 | Generators for certain alternating groups with applications to cryptography, SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics 29, 624-627. Cited as unread: the journal page is paywalled. The statement used here is the one its readers attribute to it, read in Babinkostova et al. 2012: DES-like components can in principle generate the alternating group, all even permutations of the block space. Nothing on this page depends on its proof; the sign law below is proved from scratch | https://doi.org/10.1137/0129051 |
| Even and Goldreich 1983 | DES-like functions can generate the alternating group, IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 29, 863-865. Cited as unread: paywalled. Its two secondary accounts disagree in wording: Babinkostova et al. 2012 say it shows DES-like functions are contained in the alternating group, Aragona, Caranti and Sala 2015 that the group they generate is the alternating group. Both halves are re-proved or re-measured here for the design family and neither is taken from it | https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.1983.1056752 |
| Caranti, Dalla Volta and Sala 2009 | On some block ciphers and imprimitive groups, Applicable Algebra in Engineering, Communication and Computing 20, 339-350, arXiv:0806.4135. Read: Section 2.1 states that any block system of the translation group `T(V)` is the set of translates of a proper vector subspace of `V`, so a group containing all translations is imprimitive exactly when some proper nonzero subspace has its cosets permuted; Theorem 4.4 proves primitivity for translation-based ciphers whose bricks are weakly `2^r`-uniform and strongly `r`-anti-invariant under a proper mixing layer. The design rounds here are not translation-based in their sense (the mess is not a brick layer), so only the subspace fact is used | https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.4135 |
| Caranti, Dalla Volta and Sala 2009b | An application of the O'Nan-Scott theorem to the group generated by the round functions of an AES-like cipher, Designs, Codes and Cryptography 52, 293-301, arXiv:0812.1629. Cited for the O'Nan-Scott route from primitivity to the alternating group for AES-like ciphers; the same step for design schedules is a conjecture here | https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.1629 |
