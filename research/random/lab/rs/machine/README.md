# Machine

- The counter-mode design machine against Rule 30 + slide, Rule 30 and ChaCha8 in one harness: an enumerated catalog of design cells, a family of three-offset spreads, a two-stage screen, a nine-test battery with `R*`, and every load, control, diagnosis and timing of the chapter.
- The grid is the `n x n` torus, `n = 16` or `32`, bit `n i + j` the cell `(i, j)`, bit-sliced across 64 counters per `u64` word. The seed fills the whole grid through splitmix64 with seed `0x6d616368696e65`; a 32-bit counter is XORed into a 4 x 8 block; the output is the raw 8 x 8 block at the origin, 64 bits per counter.
- The three loads put the block's corner at the antipode spot (within two cells of `(n/2 + 2, n/2)`, as near 16 of its cells in the split as the window allows), at `(n - 4, n - 8)` (upstream of the slide) and at `(n/2 + 2, 0)` (below the output).
- A round is `t_c L F`: the Feistel step writes each split cell `a` with `x_a + g(count)`, the count over the live cells of the read side in its neighbourhood; the spread `L` is a torus map (`id`, `shift`, `drop`, `slide`, `turn`, `flip`) or a sum `x(p - v0) + x(p - v1) + x(p - v2)` over three distinct offsets; the stamp of round `t` is the split translated by `(t mod n, floor(t/n) mod n)`.
- Rule 30 + slide writes `x_(p-1) + (x_p OR x_(p+1))` on the row-major line of the torus, then applies the slide `(i, j) -> (i + 1, j + 1)`, with the machine's seed, counter cells and output cells. Rule 30 untransported runs on the ring of `n^2` cells, counter on cells `n^2/2 + 16` to `n^2/2 + 47` or on the last 32 cells (near load), output on cells 0 to 63.
- ChaCha8 is the RFC 8439 block function at 8 rounds, key and nonce from splitmix64 on the seed, the counter in word 12, output words 0 and 1. The crate's tests check the quarter round and the ChaCha20 block against RFC 8439 sections 2.1.1 and 2.3.2; `chacha.py` is a second implementation that checks both the RFC vector and the crate's ChaCha8 outputs.
- The battery at round `r`, over `2^20` counters unless stated: monobit `|z| < 4.5`; avalanche over `2^14` base counters and all 32 flips, maximum deviation from `1/2` under `4/2^7`; Walsh on the constant and the 20 weight-one masks under `4.5/2^10`; the full Walsh spectrum, all `2^20` masks of the low 20 counter bits for each output bit, `2^26` coefficients, under `(sqrt(2 ln 2^27) + 1)/2^10 = 7.118/2^10`; cube sums `d = 2..20`, `|z| < 4.5`; Berlekamp-Massey on `2^16` stream bits, `|L - 2^15| <= 10`; rank of 64 x 64 matrices, chi-square under 27; rank of 512 x 512 and 1024 x 1024 matrices of consecutive stream bits (256 and 64 of them), chi-square on the classes full, one short and at least two short under 24 and no matrix more than 10 short. `R*` is the first round where all nine pass and keep passing four more rounds. The six-test `R*` drops the last three and is printed beside it.
- `C = R* n^2 k/64` neighbour reads per output bit, `k = 8` for the carpet and 3 for Rule 30; the strict count adds the spread's reads per cell, 1 for a map and 3 for a sum.

## RUN

- `cargo run --release --manifest-path research/random/lab/rs/machine/Cargo.toml -- <verb>`, single-threaded, prints only.
- `calibrate` (8 s): splitmix64, xoshiro256+ low bit and ChaCha8 through the nine tests. `chacha` prints ChaCha8 outputs for `chacha.py` and its scalar speed: `cargo run ... -- chacha | python -I chacha.py`.
- `slide30` (130 s): Rule 30 + slide at the three loads and both sides; it caches its `T*` in `data/research/random/lab/rs/machine/slide30.txt`.
- `family [radius]` (96 s at radius 1): every triple of distinct offsets in the box, screened at both sides; at radius 1 the first four, in enumeration order, of eight triples tied at the top of the second screen go through the nine tests at both sides; the best by the worse side of `C(Rule 30 + slide)/C`, ties in enumeration order, goes to `branch.txt` with its rank, order, bijection and impulse checks.
- `headline` (221 s): both machines and Rule 30 + slide at the three loads and both sides, Rule 30 untransported, one run of the speeds and the three headline lines. `speed` (2 s): ns per output byte at the antipodal `R*` of the slide machine, the branch-3 machine and Rule 30 + slide at both sides, and of ChaCha8 scalar, with `R*` fixed at the values `headline` prints and no battery; it times each 7 times and prints the median with min and max. `diagnose` (under 1 s): active cells per round at side 32. `bad` (24 s): symmetries, the all-zero fill, the counter inside the output and the fold.
- `best` reruns the catalog and its shortlist through the nine tests; `screen`, `fscreen <level> <radius>`, `sources`, `controls`, `rule30`, `mess <g>...`, `tail <x>...` and `trace` run one section.

## WITNESSES

- `calibrate`: splitmix64 and ChaCha8 pass all nine at round 1 (full Walsh 5.84 and 5.76, rank 512 chi-square 0.02 and 1.76, rank 1024 3.41 and 0.48); xoshiro256+ low bit fails linear complexity (`L = 256`) and both large ranks, every matrix at rank exactly 256; the largest of `2^26` ideal coefficients has median 5.73 and exceeds the bar with probability `7.35e-5`.
- `chacha`, `chacha.py`: the RFC 8439 2.3.2 block from both implementations; ChaCha8 agrees on 8 of 8 counters.
- `slide30`: `T*` 68, 84, 83 at side 16 and 141, 133, 142 at side 32 (antipode, upstream, below).
- `family 1`: 84 triples, none excluded by a translation symmetry of the split; 57 pass the second screen at both sides; the first four, in enumeration order, of eight triples tied at the top of the second screen give `R*` (16, 32) of (15, 22), (15, 23), (15, 21), (17, 21) for `(-1,-1)(-1,0)(1,1)`, `(-1,-1)(0,-1)(1,1)`, `(-1,0)(-1,1)(1,-1)`, `(-1,0)(-1,1)(1,1)`, with six-test `R*` 13, 14, 13, 15 at side 16; the first three tie on the worse side at 1.70 and the first goes to `branch.txt`; spread rank `n^2` at both sides, `L^n = I`, `R^-1 R = I`.
- `headline`: slide machine `R*` 24, 24, 24 and 45, 37, 49; branch-3 machine 15, 16, 15 and 22, 17, 22, with measured cones 18, 12, 21 at side 32; ratios at the antipode 1.06, 1.18 (slide) and 1.70, 2.40 (branch-3); Rule 30 untransported 177, 97, 561, 97.
- `speed`: ns/byte at `R*`, median of 7 runs with min to max, on a shared laptop, for the slide machine, the branch-3 machine and Rule 30 + slide: 25.4 (21.2 to 54.2), 15.6 (15.5 to 15.6), 26.8 (26.7 to 26.9) at side 16 and 128.1 (127.9 to 128.5), 77.0 (76.8 to 77.3), 215.7 (214.9 to 227.4) at side 32; ChaCha8 scalar 4.5 (4.5 to 4.5).
- `best`: the 10 shortlisted cells at side 16 through the nine tests; the slide machine reads `R*` 24 and the other nine 24, 26, 27, 34, 35, 36, 36, 43, 51, each equal to its six-test reading.
- `diagnose`: `beta = 0.991`; mean active cells at round 24: 280.1 (slide), 511.9 (branch-3), 54.0 (Rule 30 + slide).
- `bad`: all-zero fill 25, counter inside the output 28, fold 23.
