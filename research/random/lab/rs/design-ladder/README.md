# design-ladder

- The generator of the design ladder: one binary, eight verbs, no dependencies, single-threaded, every verb under thirty seconds.
- A signed design at base `b` is `x(n) = prod v(n_i)` over the base-`b` digits of `n`, `v = (-1)^code`; at level `L` the product runs over `L` digits, leading zeros included, so a code with `v(0) = -1` is the code `v v(0)` up to the global sign `v(0)^L`. A digit-restricted design is `A_L(n) = [every one of the L digits of n lies in D]`. Thue-Morse is the base-2 signed design of code `{1}`; `t3` is the base-3 signed design of code `{2}`, the parity of the ternary digits equal to `2`.
- Exact shift correlations of a signed design come from the block recursion `C(bq + s) = (al_s C(q) + al_(b-s) C(q + 1))/b`, `al_s = sum_(r < b - s) v(r) v(r + s)`, in rational arithmetic; the recursion is the exact identity `S(bN, bq + s) = al_s S(N, q) + al_(b-s) S(N, q + 1)` on the sums `S(N, h) = sum_(n < N) x(n) x(n + h)`.
- Exact dilation and kernel sums: for `g(n) = x(An + a) x(Bn + r)` the digit split `n = bm + d` gives `g(bm + d) = v(s1) v(s2) g'(m)` with `Ad + a = b q1 + s1`, `Bd + r = b q2 + s2`, so the vector of sums over the `A B` states obeys `S(bN) = M S(N)` with an integer matrix `M`, iterated exactly in `i128`. An upper bound `rho <= norm(M^64)^(1/64)` on the spectral radius is printed; `rho < b` forces every mean to `0`.
- Cellular automata run bit-packed with the support tracked, so a single-seed run of `T` steps costs about `T^2/64` word updates; seeds sit on `n >= 0`, zeros to the left. The kernel count of a stream `s` at base `b` is the number of distinct words `(s(b^k n + r))_(n < 24)` over `r < b^k`, accumulated over `k = 0, 1, ...` while `24 b^k` fits: it stops growing at the kernel size of an automatic stream and grows as `b^k` on a stream with no structure.
- The reach of an elementary rule at a round is the set of the eight windows `(l, c, r)` in its row, the zero exterior included; an affine rule is `a l + b c + d r + e` over `F_2`, sixteen of the 256. A rule is linear on its reach with shadow `g` when it agrees with `g` on every window the orbit meets, from round 0 or from a later round.
- The exact windows of an affine orbit over all rounds and cells come from the section recursion: the cell `(2t + e, 2m + u)` of the diagram of a seed `v` is the cell `(t, m)` of the diagram of `Lambda_u(Phi^e v)`. A state is a tuple of (seed, offset) pairs; a seed is a bit vector over the shifts by at most two of the cell, the Thue-Morse sequence on `n >= 0` and the indicator of `n >= 0`; halving `T` and `n` reaches `(0, 0)` or `(0, -1)`, where the values are read. The constant of an affine rule is one tag bit.
- Rules are simulated bit-packed from the algebraic normal form of the truth table, 64 cells a word, with the background tracked for rules that send `000` to 1.
- LZ78 is the plain phrase parse; the printed rate is `c (log2 c + 1)/N` bits per bit for `c` phrases.

## VERBS

- `codes`: every base-2 and base-3 level-1 code. Signed designs: the dilation identity `x_L(bn + r) = v(0) v(r) x_L(n)` checked at every `n < b^(L-1)`, `L = 20` at base 2 and `13` at base 3; exact `C(1..b)`, the first nonzero lag, `max abs C(h)` over `h <= 4096`, and the render's `C(h)` against the exact value for `h <= 8`. Digit-restricted designs: `Pearson(A_L(n), A_L(bn + r))` for every residue. Runtime about 2 s.
- `rudin`: Rudin-Shapiro `x(2n) = x(n)` and `x(4n + 1) = x(n)` for `n < 2^20`; the sums `S(N, h)` for `h <= 64`, their maximum over every `N <= 2^20` and over `N = 2^10..2^20`; the sequence `(-1)^n` times Thue-Morse against the dilations `2^d n + r`, `d <= 3`, and against the kernel pair `(2n, 4n)`. Runtime under 1 s.
- `battery`: seven streams at `N = 2^15` pairs per test, against the mean, the shift test `max over h <= 64`, the dilation test `max over 2 <= B <= 9, r < B`, the kernel test `max over b in {2, 3}` of `Pearson(x(bn), x(b^2 n))`, the dilation square `mean of x(n) x(2n) x(3n) x(6n)` and the LZ78 rate at `2^14`, `2^16`, `2^18`. The Rule 30 centre column runs `9 2^15 + 16` steps from one cell and is checked against the first 36 terms of A051023. Runtime about 2 s.
- `moire`: the moire XOR `z = TM t3` and `y = RS t3`. Exact `C2(h) C3(h)`; its maximum on every window `[2^k, 2^(k+1))`, `k <= 20`, and the tail supremum; the empirical `C_z(h)` at `N = 2^22`, `h <= 256`; the dilation sums at `B = 2, 4, 8, 16` and `3, 9, 27` with the radius bound and the exact growth exponent along `b^k`; the kernel pairs `(2n, 4n)` and `(3n, 9n)`, the second checked against the order-4 law at every `k <= 60`; the maxima of `abs C2` by binary non-adjacent-form weight and of `abs C3` by balanced-ternary weight over `h < 2^20`; the factor complexity of `z` and `y` to `n = 64` with the joint-pair completeness check; the sums of Rudin-Shapiro along `3n + c` to `2^24`. Runtime about 4 s.
- `rounds`: the centre column `c(t)`, `t < 2^15`, of rules 90, 150, 30, 110, 254 and 18 from one cell, the Thue-Morse seed and the Cantor seed, with kernel counts at bases 2 and 3; rules 18, 22, 126 and 146 from one cell against rule 90 and Pascal mod 2 on `t < 4096`; rows 1 to 4 of rule 30 and row 4 of rule 110 on the Thue-Morse seed; the seed and a `splitmix64` control. Runtime under 1 s.
- `shadow`: the exact window set of each of the 16 affine orbits from one cell and from the Thue-Morse seed beside the set seen by simulation to `t <= 4096`; then, per seed, the rules linear on their reach grouped by shadow, the same count from simulation, the last round at which any rule meets a new window, and the rules with identical diagrams to `t <= 4096`. Runtime about 5 s.
- `escape`: three columns of every rule, for the one-cell seed (columns -1, 0, 1, `t < 2^15`), the Thue-Morse seed at its edge (the same) and in its bulk (columns `2^14`, `+1`, `+2`, `t < 2^14`). Each rule is classed linear on its reach, burn-in (last exercise at round 512 or earlier against the best affine rule; the largest found is 2, 72 and 5 on the three probes) or exercised past round 512 (the smallest found is 4094), and each column periodic (period at most 512 after a preperiod of an eighth of its length), plateau (the kernel count of the last depth equals that three depths earlier, also checked at word length 48) or growing. Prints the lists, the exercise density over interior or bulk cells at rounds `t > 2048`, and the control. Runtime about 30 s.
- `lemma`: rule 182 from one cell against `[abs(n) <= t](1 + P(t + 1, n))`; rule 22 against the block code of the rule 90 row `t - 1`; the difference field of rule 126 against rule 18 from the pair `11`; the rounds at which rule 90 does not fit the interior of rule 126; the closure of the width-7 windows of the rule 90 rows with their parity tag, against the simulation, and the rule 22 step lemma on each; rule 126 against `P(t, rho_t(n))`, rule 129 against `1 + a` with the background tracked, rule 161 against rule 129, the isolated ones of rule 126, and columns `-8..8` of rule 126 against `P(t, n_0) + P(t, n_0 + sgn(n_0))` with their kernel words. Runtime about 1 s.
