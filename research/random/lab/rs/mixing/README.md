# Mixing

- The pairwise mixing of design rounds: with a uniform stamp after every round, the difference of an input pair is a Markov chain on the `2^N - 1` nonzero differences with kernel `P(a, b) = #{x : h(x) + h(x + a) = b} / 2^N`, `h` the round without its stamp.
- The round is the one of `lab/rs/rounds`, reproduced: the same catalog, the same symmetry reduction, the same tables. `check` recounts the figure cell `0x135f` carpet primes slide (258 cycles, longest 24865, 3 fixed), the 4320 and 2856 cells and the nonlinear mean cycle counts 136.43 and 16759.65.
- The kernel is never a dense `2^N x 2^N` matrix. The mess reads only the void `B`, so a row depends on `a` through `a_B` alone: for each `a_B` the law of `mess(y) + mess(y + a_B)` over `y` in `F_2^B` is computed exactly by two Walsh-Hadamard transforms (the autocorrelation of every component `u . mess`), and the row is that law moved by `a` and the spread. At `3 x 3` every row of every cell is checked against the direct table, at `4 x 4` 32 seeded rows of each sampled cell.
- Irreducibility is one depth-first search of the support graph from one difference; a doubly stochastic chain is irreducible exactly when it reaches all. The closed classes are the components found by repeated searches.
- `sigma_2(P^j)` is the top eigenvalue of `P^j (P^j)^T` on the sum-zero space by Lanczos with full reorthogonalisation, seeded, with the Ritz residual printed. `|lambda_2(P)|` at `3 x 3` is the growth rate of seeded power iteration on the sum-zero space, 400 burn-in and 200 measured steps. At `4 x 4` (`four`, `cone`) it is the largest modulus among the Ritz values of restarted Arnoldi on the sum-zero space: 80 steps with full reorthogonalisation, the Hessenberg eigenvalues by shifted QR, restarted from the Ritz vector until the unit Ritz vector `x` has `||P x - lambda x|| <= 1e-10`, at most 40 restarts, the residual printed; `four` reruns it from a second seed.
- The distance after `r` rounds is `d(r) = max over a of TV(e_a P^(r-1), uniform)`: every start at `3 x 3`, the 16 single cells, the fill and the void at `4 x 4`.
- `triple` runs the chain on ordered pairs of distinct nonzero differences, the 3-wise chain, 260610 states at `3 x 3`, applied on the fly from the same derivative table; its first-coordinate marginal is checked against the 2-wise chain.
- `stamps` enumerates every path of a uniform input pair or a void-cell start under uniform stamps and under the three neighbourhood designs as stamps.
- `cone` propagates the support a difference can reach, `R -> sigma(R + {a in A : a reads R})`, on the level-`L` carpet `{i AND j = 0}` and the board `{i + j even}`, `L = 2..6`, over a family of spreads, from every start, 64 starts at a time as bit lanes, and runs the exact chain at `n = 4` for the schedules sampled next.
- `multi` samples 16384 difference runs per schedule at `n = 4, 8, 16` from the start that the cone reaches last, and prints the largest bit bias and the collision count per round.
- `dense.py` is the independent half: it rebuilds the `3 x 3` catalog from the definitions in numpy, builds every kernel from the direct table, counts the irreducible cells with a strong-components search, and recomputes every distance curve by dense matrix powers and, on every seventh irreducible cell, the eigenvalues and singular values.

## RUN

- `cargo run --release --manifest-path research/random/lab/rs/mixing/Cargo.toml -- <verb>`, single-threaded, prints only, writes nothing. Verbs and times: `check` 1 s, `three` 19 s, `four` 94 s, `triple 30` 41 s, `stamps` under 1 s, `cone` 28 s, `multi` 16 s.
- `uv run --project . python -I research/random/lab/rs/mixing/dense.py [all]` from the repo root, 16 s with one BLAS thread; `all` prints every irreducible cell.
