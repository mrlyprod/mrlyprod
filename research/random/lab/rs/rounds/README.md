# Rounds

- The round census: every design round on the `3 x 3` and `4 x 4` torus as a permutation of all `2^N` grids, its cycle type, its sign, and the group it generates together with every stamp.
- A grid is `N = n^2` bits, bit `n i + j` the cell `(i, j)`. A split is a cell mask `A` (fill, written) with `B` its complement (void, read). The catalog is enumerated, never picked: at `3 x 3` every mask `1..510`, the base-3 level-1 codes; at `4 x 4` every two-letter base-2 word `k1 (x) k2`, `k1, k2` in `1..15`, the full word dropped, 224 masks with 14 Kronecker squares among them.
- The mess: a cell `a` of `A` reads the cells `a + d` of `B` for `d` in a neighbourhood, the base-3 level-1 design `carpet 495` (8 offsets), `net 186` (4) or `void 341` (4) with its centre popped, and writes `[count in S]` with `S` the Source `primes {2,3,5,7}`, `binary {1,2,4,8}`, `fibonacci {0,1,2,3,5,8}` or `odds {1,3,5,7}`. `odds` makes the mess linear and is the control.
- The spread is a torus map moving the bit at `p` to `sigma(p)`: `id`, `shift (i, j+1)`, `drop (i+1, j)`, `slide (i+1, j+1)`, `turn (j, n-1-i)`, `flip (j, i)`. The round is `R = t_c . L . F` with `F(x) = x + mess(x)`, `L` the spread and `c = A` the split's own design as stamp.
- De-duplication before counting: a split is kept once per orbit of the torus symmetries (translations times the eight square symmetries) that commute with the spread; the three neighbourhoods are square-symmetric, so a symmetry in that centraliser conjugates the whole round and keeps its cycle type and its group.
- Every round is checked to be a bijection, `F` an involution, the undo `F . L^-1 . t_c` its inverse, and its sign against the sign law: even when `|A| >= 2`, else `(-1)^w` with `w` the weight of the one mess bit, itself checked against `w = 2^(|B| - k) sum_{s in S} C(k, s)` for a cell reading `k` cells. `three` and `four` first print `sum_{s in S} C(k, s)` for every neighbourhood and Source.
- The group `G = <T, h>` with `h = L . F` and `T` all `2^N` stamps: for every `v != 0` the smallest subspace containing `v` closed under every derivative `h(x + u) + h(x)` is computed, first by random draws that can only prove it is the whole space, then exhaustively over every `x` and every generator of the subspace; a proper closure is a block system and is re-verified as one. A cell with no block is primitive.
- A primitive cell is then searched for an element with a cycle of prime length `p <= 2^N - 3` that is the only cycle of length divisible by `p`, so that a power of it is a `p`-cycle: first the 512 elements `t_c . h`, then 512 random words of length 2 to 4. Jordan's theorem then gives the alternating group, the symmetric one when a generator is odd.
- The laws, each checked against the verdict: a read-closed set (law I), the mess module (II), the complement symmetry (III), the parity of a design partition (IV, run where I to III fail on an imprimitive nonlinear cell and on every primitive cell), the product decomposition (V, on every cell). A cell where law I, II, III or IV holds and the group is primitive, or where the product law holds or the mess is linear and a lone prime cycle is found, is a fault. For nonlinear and `odds` cells apart, each catalog prints the cells where the product law holds and how many primitive cells meet each of laws I to V.
- `pairs` runs the two-step rounds `F_B . F_A` with the identity spread, a split and its complement written in turn, the generalised Feistel network of the classic two-half case.
- `null` checks the uniform even-permutation null by enumerating every even permutation of `M <= 8` points.

## RUN

- `cargo run --release --manifest-path research/random/lab/rs/rounds/Cargo.toml -- all`, about a minute single-threaded, prints only, writes nothing; it ends the two catalogs with their total cells and faults.
- The verbs `three`, `four`, `pairs`, `null` run one section; `blocks` prints every `3 x 3` cell with its group, its first law and its block's kept parities; `word k1 k2` prints the 72 cells of one `4 x 4` word.

## WITNESSES

- `three`: one written cell reading all 8 others (carpet) has `sum_{s in S} C(8, s)` primes 148, binary 107, fibonacci 150, odds 128, odd only for binary; net and void read 4, weight `2^4` times 10, 11, 15, 8. 4320 cells, 0 faults. Nonlinear 3240: alternating 147 (135 by a stamped round, 12 by a word), primitive product 6, imprimitive 3087 (first law: closed 2901, mess 183, complement 3); odds 1080: imprimitive 1068, affine primitive 12. The product law holds in 491 nonlinear cells, 6 of them primitive, and in 28 odds cells; of the 153 primitive nonlinear cells only those 6 meet a law (V), and of the 12 primitive odds cells none meets laws I to V. No cell unexplained, none undecided. Odd rounds 9, all imprimitive.
- `four`: a cell reads at most 8 of the 15 others, so every single-cell weight carries `2^7` and is even. 2856 cells, 0 faults. Nonlinear 2142: alternating 198 (192 by a stamped round, 6 by a word), primitive product 3, imprimitive 1941 (first law: closed 1802, mess 113, complement 8, parity 18, each of the 18 printed with its kept parities); odds 714, all imprimitive. The product law holds in 926 nonlinear cells, 3 of them primitive, and in 256 odds cells; of the 201 primitive nonlinear cells only those 3 meet a law (V). No cell unexplained, none undecided. Odd rounds 0.
- `all`: both catalogs, 7176 cells, 0 faults.
- `pairs`: 12 splits at `3 x 3` up to symmetry and complement, alternating 12 of 108 nonlinear cells (binary 8, primes 4); 23 words at `4 x 4`, alternating 10 of 207 (binary 6, fibonacci 3, primes 1); every other cell imprimitive.
- `null`: the mean cycle count of the even permutations of `M = 2..8` points equals `H_M + (-1)^M / (M (M - 1))` and their mean fixed-point count is 1 from `M = 3`.
- `word 7 7`: the level-2 carpet split `0x135f` with the carpet neighbourhood, `primes` and `slide` is alternating, 258 cycles, longest 24865, 3 fixed points, the cell the figure redraws and re-counts.
