# dimension-one-ladder

- Regenerates the coprime terms of the three base-three mask designs, the moment ladder of the ternary gasket, and the occupied-ray census.
- Terms: `A(level)` for the carpet, sponge and Vicsek plus by Mobius over the quotient box with a subset-lattice inner count, checked against brute force and the kept `terms/` files.
- Ladder: exact carry matrices for the even moments `E_2K(G_a)`, a direct convolution as second builder, factored characteristic polynomials, the strongly connected component of the zero state and the energy cap, a Sturm bracket for `lambda_10`, the rungs `beta_0^(2K)` to `2K = 20`, the Fourier identities and master bound on every prime `5..199`, the dyadic tail and the order-10 main range.
- Census: every ray of the gasket at one level by exact enumeration, per-octave counts, `Z`, and the multiplier distribution at level 13.
- Blocks: the coding `S_j = [w_(j+k-1) = y][w_(j-1) != y]` of the walks of the component of `0`, distance-`k`-free and injective on the closed walks, checked on every occupied ray to level 12; for every `k = 2` ray of a box, the distance-2-free blocks its code language misses, the minimal ones, the distribution of the shortest missed length and the blocks missed by every off-shift ray, so `mu < phi` on a ray reads off a missed block.
- Rates: every primitive ray of a box, its live direction automaton, the mass rate `mu`, the Perron root of the component of `0`, by power iteration inside a Collatz-Wielandt bracket, the first returns when the live set is acyclic off the start, exact divisibility of the characteristic polynomial by `x^k - x^(k-1) - 1` and `x^k - x - 1` through modular remainders under the bound `(2 + sqrt 2)^n`, and the exact characteristic polynomial up to 70 live states; `radii.py` factors those polynomials and reads off the minimal polynomial of every rate; `mu` is at most the whole-box radius `rho` of the pincer paper.

## RUN

- `cargo run --release --manifest-path research/coprime/lab/rs/dimension-one-ladder/Cargo.toml` runs the carpet and plus to level 18, the sponge to level 16, the census at level 13..16, about two minutes on eight cores.
- `... --manifest-path research/coprime/lab/rs/dimension-one-ladder/Cargo.toml -- terms carpet 20` or `census 13 14` picks one job; the kept rows reach level 20 for the two planar designs and level 18 for the sponge, and print from `terms/` labelled stored only.
- `uv run python research/coprime/lab/rs/dimension-one-ladder/ladder.py` runs the ladder in about a second.
- `... -- radii 243` prints the mass-rate census of the box `1 <= a <= b <= 243` in about two seconds on eight cores, `... -- ray 1 756` one ray, with the whole-box radius `rho` of the carry box `[0, a) x [0, b)` beside `mu`; `uv run python research/coprime/lab/rs/dimension-one-ladder/radii.py <census output>` factors the exact polynomials with sympy and searches the least multipliers below `3^13`, about seven seconds.
- `... -- blocks 243 9` prints the missed-block census of the `k = 2` rays of the box to block length 9 with the coding-lemma check on every occupied ray, about a second, `... -- blocks 243 9 probe 1100010001100100110011` adds a path-DP test of one block on every off-shift ray; `... -- blocks 4 9 9` prints one ray with its lettered component, its minimal missed blocks and the length to which every distance-`k`-free block is realised, and `... -- blocks 1 19692 13 probe 1100010001100100110011` adds the path DP of one block on that ray.
