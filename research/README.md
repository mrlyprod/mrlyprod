# Research

- [Mobius and primes with no two equal adjacent digits](adjacent/README.md): The integers with no two equal adjacent base-`b` digits keep exactly `(b-1)^L` of the integers with `L` digits, the dimension of one missing digit, and form no digit design; a run expansion of their transform carries a chain certificate below `1/5` from base `1153`, and from there the Mobius function cancels on the set and its primes are counted, `A(x)/log x` of them, with constant `1`, where a design of alternating halves inside the set gave primes only at even bases from `61270` and at a lower count, by a theorem on digit automata of which the Kummer set is the two-state case.
- [The Apollonian gasket](apollonian/README.md): The circle face of the stack: the Descartes reflection as exact integer arithmetic on `(k, k x, k y)`, the strip packing's line-tangent circles proved to be exactly the Ford circles so the Farey stack is the packing's shadow with brightness `floor(Q sqrt(2/k))`, the curvature census, root quadruple excluded, whose exponent by ratio lands at `1.305` against the rigorous residual dimension, the eight residues mod 24, and why no design of base at most 100 carries that dimension.
- [Loops in arcs](arcs/README.md): A design drawn in quarter-circle arcs, its deleted cells turned once, and the closed loops the arcs make level by level.
- [Sparse arrays](arrays/README.md): A fractal sparse array is a digit design in the balanced base of its generator's coarray, so its coarray weight is a product over digits and, from order 2 on, its fragility is exact: a sensor is essential exactly when every digit has a unique-difference partner, the fragility is `(u/L)^r`, the power bound of Yang and coauthors is tight exactly when the generator's essential sensors all have such partners, and the bound of Cohen and Eldar exactly when every sensor has one; at a compressed base the essential count can leave the product law.
- [The automata](automata/README.md): An elementary cellular automaton is a three-dimensional design read as a rule and Life a nine-dimensional one: what the identity buys, and what it cannot.
- [What base 3 hides](bases/README.md): What base 3 hides: an Eisenstein L-value where base 2 hid pi, and the collapse theorem that turns any multiplicatively dependent family of bases into one design in base `r^lcm(e_i)`.
- [Beneath a design](beneath/README.md): The object beneath a design as three slots, accept, place and glue: the memory dial priced by a transfer matrix with `kappa` zero on exactly the non-empty product rules over `19563` live classes, the Collatz carry landed on that dial as a four-state transducer of unbounded radius whose zero-carry sets are the golden and supergolden rules at `3n + 1` and `7n + 1`, and the radix dial that keeps every count, carries the gasket, the dragons and the Koch curve as codes with a twist vector, and loses the fill law the moment a digit turns; the Mobius meter of every width-three rule to `2^30` against its own mass, the memory zeta as a matrix ladder with one pole comb per eigenvalue, and the design census up to similarity and up to affine conjugacy.
- [A design is a Boolean function](bijection/README.md): Designs are Boolean functions up to cube symmetry; the strongest theorem in the tree.
- [Two bases](cobham/README.md): What a second multiplicatively independent base does to a design: Cobham and Cobham-Semenov at their sources, every proper dim 1 design proved base-locked by density alone, the semilinear designs of dim at least 2 pinned between a proved count condition and the block designs with the diagonal as the standing counterexample, the global planar budget Refuted twice and replaced by one budget per axis for product designs, the base-2 gasket against the base-3 gasket counted exactly to `3^24`, and a three-base object on the line whose budget is negative, with five members and no sixth below a height of `38170` decimal digits, and the sumset of the base-3 and base-4 designs `{0, 1}`, Erdos problem 125, counted to `3^22`, its two-base energy ratio proved subpolynomial and unbounded, and its open upper density shown positive once one orbit of an irrational rotation misses, infinitely often, a target of measure at most `190/M`.
- [Complexity](complexity/README.md): Boolean complexity of the catalog, and the Laplacian spectra of the fractals it builds.
- [Structure against noise](connectivity/README.md): Self-similar designs raced against matched random cell sets on components and boundary.
- [The coprimality spine](coprime/README.md): The coprimality spine: exact base-local factors on every design, the census behind them, the window at dimension one with the band automaton's critical out-degree and its algebraic block rate, and primes on a design, where the least base below the quarter threshold is 21 at one missing digit and 32 at two, the one-digit family closes at 34 and the two-digit family closes at 21 against the third.
- [The core](core/README.md): What a design is, the headline counts, and the three genera.
- [Crop](crop/README.md): The circle on a design: the corner disc count, its log-periodic main term, and the crossing shell read as a rooted tree whose transfer operator is derived from the geometry.
- [Cuts](cuts/README.md): The six-gasket theorem: a diagonal cut through one parity solid is Sierpinski all the way down.
- [Dilated stacks](dilations/README.md): The effective receptive field of a dilated convolution stack is a digit-design representation count: Stern's diatomic sequence for three taps at base 2, with Fibonacci peaks and `2L + 1` single-path lags, and a depth limit that is absolutely continuous exactly when the block polynomial vanishes at the right roots of unity, which the two common residual blocks break at every gain but at most one.
- [Complex dimensions](dimensions/README.md): Complex dimensions: every design in the lattice class, the carpet not Minkowski measurable by earlier theorems (Deniz et al., Lapidus, Pearse and Winter) with an explicit profile added here, the sponge's non-measurability found in none of the sources read, and the arithmetic pole at `s_0 + 2 pi i/log 3` certified genuine.
- [Dimers on a design](dimers/README.md): Domino tilings of a plane design: the colour imbalance read off the code, tilings that never switch off, codes that tile late, and the carpet's growth constant bracketed.
- [Erdos problems](erdos/README.md): The open Erdos problems that are questions about digit designs, ranked by the odds of a checked result and by who reads them, and problem 124 attacked: sums of powers of several bases, where a bitset certificate makes each complete level a finite computation, every base set up to `10` with `sum 1/(d-1) > 1` and `gcd = 1` is certified at the first four levels, and the two-part route of the literature is proved unable to reach any base set below `sum 1/(d-1) = 2`, while two window criteria fall to explicit incomplete multisets.
- [The stack is an RH-observable](farey/README.md): The stack's moire is a Farey resonance diagram; each scale `n` adds exactly `phi(n)` bright nodes, the stack is an address rather than a construction (any depth evaluates in closed form, which buys rendering and provably nothing toward RH), and restricting the Farey sequence to a digit design empties a fixed sixth of the line at base 3 `{0,1}` when both coordinates are restricted, while restricting the denominator alone leaves the Franel-Landau shape reading the same.
- [The restricted Franel identity](franel/README.md): Franel's identity on the Farey fractions whose denominators lie in a digit design: the exponential sums are dilated Mertens sums, the square-root threshold is equivalent to their square-root cancellation in mean square over the dilates, the dilates are regular languages read by long multiplication, their masses need no Mobius input, and the design's repunits refute the uniform law that would give a rate in `d`.
- [The digit-sum twist on a design](gelfond/README.md): Under the per-digit certificate (W), which holds at every one-missing-digit base from `92317`, twisting a missing-digit design with two consecutive digits by its digit sum costs its transform a fixed share of its mass at every frequency once `norm((b - 1) alpha) > 0`, so the major arcs of the unconditional dissection need no L-function, the Mobius and von Mangoldt sums over the design save a power of `x`, and the primes of the design equidistribute in the classes of their digit sum mod `q` coprime to `b - 1`; with the design read in progressions mod every `N` coprime to `b`, the digit sum of its primes is uniform mod every `q` on the classes prime to `gcd(q, b - 1)`.
- [The hexagon moire](hexagon/README.md): The moire of the stacked diagonal slices: the exact cut-ink laws, the doubling sign law, the quarter-line bands, and the ghost star, whose cell-frame decay is a closed form at every band width, `-1/4` at the arm and `-1/8` in the limit, every layer-pair constant an exact rational, and no frame-free coefficient.
- [Information](information/README.md): A render as data: the rearranged SVD reads a design's code out of noise and peels a magic word, while the fractal codebook loses to deflate.
- [Integers](integers/README.md): The census the other way round: which integers the whole registry writes, which it never writes, and which it writes thousands of times.
- [Mobius and primes on the Kummer sets](kummer/README.md): At a prime `p >= 5` the integers `k` with `p` not dividing `C(3k, k)` form a two-state carry automaton and not a digit design; at every prime `p > a` the Kummer set `{k : p does not divide C(ak, bk)}` keeps exactly `((p+1)/2)^L` of the first `p^L` integers and its transform is a product of `b(a - b)`-square matrices of digit kernels, `2 x 2` at `(3, 1)`; there a matrix transfer certificate clears `1/5` from `p = 13567831`, and from there the Mobius function cancels on the set and its primes are counted with constant `p/(p+1)`.
- [Magic words](magic/README.md): A different design at every scale: the word grammar, what collapses by block reduction, and what only the letter order can see.
- [Design masks](masks/README.md): A Toeplitz attention mask whose offsets form a digit design reaches every offset in two hops exactly when its digit sumset covers the digits; the sparsest ties the strided mask in density and loses to it on tiny Shakespeare, which reads the nearest offsets.
- [Method](method/README.md): How the results here are produced and checked, worked through on the odd-side fill polynomial.
- [The Mobius meter across digit designs](mobius/README.md): The Mobius meter across digit designs: exact transfer between scaled columns, the 47-column cancellation census, a power saving on the dense columns under GRH, the pair route with its major-arc lemma and its `l^1` threshold `1/4`, and square-root cancellation as the open exponent.
- [The fractal mirror](multilayers/README.md): Light through a stack of glass laid on a design: the level recursion that makes `b^k` layers cost `(b - 1)k` multiplications, the Born drift `m(P_D)` of the digit polynomial, the fixed-contrast step law that reads the two-variable lift `Q_D` instead, the cyclotomic designs that still go dark, the deep drift that misses `m(Q_D)` at weak contrast, and the conjectured split between critical stacks, whose passband shrinks as `1/sqrt(k)`, and the rest, which darken geometrically.
- [Pi out of the stack](pi/README.md): Pi recovered three disjoint ways: the density of coprime points in the stacked set, one design's fill fluctuation, and one design across rising odd sides.
- [Progression-free digit designs](progressions/README.md): A digit design with `0` among its digits is free of three-term progressions exactly when its digit set is and the signed set `F + F - 2F` closes no carry walk, carries living in `{-1, 0, 1}`, and three levels decide; the no-carry case is the digit lift of a progression-free subset of `Z_b`; the census to base `40` by the criterion against brute force finds the half-base construction beaten at bases `19`, `35`, `37` and `39`, every winner a cyclic lift and a carry that leaves `0` never paying, and the dimension `log 2/log 3` of the Stanley sequence first passed at base `37` by ten digits; the planar block of Elsholtz, Hunter, Proske and Sauermann is run as a discrete block problem whose potential is a peeling order, every exact grid optimum bounding every planar block from above, so no planar block has area past `19/49` and the route cannot bring the constant below `2.3381`; the grid search tracks `7/24 + 1/m` to `m = 48` and every grid set found above `7/24` dies under dilation, while peelable blocks lifted by a slice give `r_3(Z_m^n)` the constants `4.362, 5.196, 5.477, 6.633, 7.483, 7.745` at `m = 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13`, above every published constant read, and a `36`-point block in `Z_5^3` gives `3.301`, above the `35^(1/3)` read for `F_5^n`.

- [The algebra of the stack](stack/README.md): The algebra of the stack: stacking is Dirichlet convolution and the group knows nothing new, the exact spun stack is a Gaussian Farey whose Fourier `L^2` discrepancy obeys Franel's identity one field up and is equivalent to the Riemann hypothesis for `zeta(s) L(s, chi_-4)`, its hexagonal twin puts `L(2, chi_-3)` in a proved node constant, the layers are a dilation system whose symbol is `zeta(1 + s)` so no reweighting moves the line, and the lean is the operation outside the Dirichlet group that still lands a closed form.
- [Which designs tile](tilings/README.md): Which levels of a dimension-one design tile the integers: a closed condition on the zero set of the digit transform decides T1 at every level, a horizon decides T2, the guess that the digit set must tile its residues fails at base 12, a level fails T2 first at base 18, spectral levels agree with the tiling verdict on every case the census decides, and a design whose every level tiles has a spectral limit measure at prime-power size, at `pq` digits, at four digits or fewer, and on every case of the census, while a spectral limit measure whose levels are sets needs integer orthogonal sets of every size `|F|^L`, which proves the converse at five and seven digits, and at eleven and thirteen digits at every base whose `{2, 3}`-part is below the size, with `0` in `F` and levels that are sets, and decides it on every set of its prime-size census, on every 11-set at base 60 and on every 11-set and 13-set at base 120.
