# Which designs tile

Which levels of a dimension-one design tile the integers: a closed condition on the zero set of the digit transform decides T1 at every level, a horizon decides T2, the guess that the digit set must tile its residues fails at base 12, a level fails T2 first at base 18, spectral levels agree with the tiling verdict on every case the census decides, and a design whose every level tiles has a spectral limit measure at prime-power size, at `pq` digits, at four digits or fewer, and on every case of the census, while a spectral limit measure whose levels are sets needs integer orthogonal sets of every size `|F|^L`, which proves the converse at five digits with `0` in `F` and levels that are sets and decides it on every set of its prime-size census.

## Lost

- Levels that tile exactly when the digit set tiles its residues: false at base 6 by `{0, 2}`, and the repair by the gcd false at base 12 by `{0, 1, 8, 9}` (The guess is false).
- The prime-size converse through `p` integers pairwise incongruent mod `b` whose differences reach non-prime-power orders: the non-tiling `{0, 1, 251, 375, 501}` at base 510 has five such integers, and the covering bound replaces the route (The converse at prime size).
