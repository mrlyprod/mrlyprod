- 2026-10-02 [Proved] At an odd base `base = 2 fill - 1` the half interval `F = {0..fill-1}` has `2 S_F` equal to the design on the even digits, and at a prime base `p` its set `S_F` is `{k >= 1 : p does not divide C(2k,k)}`, since Legendre's formula makes `v_p(C(2k,k))` the number of carries in `k + k`.
