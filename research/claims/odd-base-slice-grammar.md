- 2026-08-28 [Verified] The odd-base generalisation rests on a choice of solid: Cook's predicate "at most one coordinate in the middle third" and the `bang dim 3, code 23` rule "at most one odd coordinate" agree at `base = 3` (20 of 27) and nowhere else, at `base = 5` filling `4^3 + 3 * 4^2 = 112` of 125 against `3^3 + 3 * 2 * 3^2 = 81 = 4k^3 - 3k^2` at `k = 3`, and there is no canonical base-5 Menger sponge.
