- 2026-10-05 [Proved] Let `S` be read by a digit automaton at base `b` whose start state keeps at most `b` digits and every live state at most `fill`, split below every `x` with `L` digits into at most `N_0` blocks per scale, each of at most `fill^j` integers with transform a product of the automaton matrices against an end vector, plus at most `N_0` points, with `A_S(x) >= fill^(L-1)/N_0`; with a shifted-grid certificate `alpha_1 < 1/5` from every state against every end vector and unit vector; with a contraction `c_E < 1` of the live rows and of the start row over `m_0` steps whose first frequency lies at distance at least `3/(8b)` from `Z`; and with `(b/phi(b))` times the block counts coprime to `b` equal to `kappa` times the block size within `E_0`. Then `abs(M_S(x))` and `abs(psi_S(x) - kappa A_S(x))` are at most `C A_S(x) exp(-c sqrt(log x))`; the Kummer set `(3, 1)` is the two-state case. Witness: notes/adjacent.md, section The theorem on a digit automaton.
