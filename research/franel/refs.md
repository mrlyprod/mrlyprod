Wiley answers automated fetches with a 403, so the bibliographic record is resolved on the Crossref entry for the DOI, and the `3/4 + eps` statement is restated independently in the Zhang 2024 and Porritt 2018 rows | https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s2-43.2.193 |
