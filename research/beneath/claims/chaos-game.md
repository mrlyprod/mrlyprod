# The chaos game

- 2026-10-30 [Proved] For a design at base `b` with digit set `F`, the game `x_n = (x_(n-1) + a_n)/b` from `x_0` in `[0,1)^dim` has `x_n = sum_(j=1..n) a_(n+1-j) b^(-j) + b^(-n) x_0`, and for `n >= k` the point `x_n` lies in the level-`k` cell of the word `a_n a_(n-1) ... a_(n-k+1)`: each draw is pushed on as the new leading digit. (witness: ../notes/chaos-game.md, The game is a shift register; ../lab/py/chaos-game verb `register`, `241768` exact checks, `0` mismatches)
- 2026-10-30 [Proved] Law 1: with independent draws of law `p` on `F`, every `p(a) > 0`, the level-`k` cell of `x_n` has law exactly `p^k` on the `card F^k` cells for every `n >= k` and every start in `[0,1)^dim`; for `n < k` its total variation distance from that law is at least `1 - (max p)^(k-n) > 0` when `card F >= 2`, and at least `1 - card F^(n-k)` for uniform draws, so the uniform mixing time at level `k` is exactly `k` for every `eps < 1 - 1/card F`. (witness: ../notes/chaos-game.md, Exact forgetting)
- 2026-10-30 [Verified] Law 1 by exact enumeration of every draw word on `27` designs, every non-empty digit set at `(b, dim) = (2,1), (2,2), (3,1)`, the base-3 carpet and `bang dim 3, code 126`, uniform and weighted, four starts: `1328` of `1328` cases with `n >= k` exactly the product law, `872` of `872` cases with `n < k` at positive distance, `436` of `436` uniform ones at distance at least `1 - card F^(n-k)`. (witness: ../lab/py/chaos-game verb `forget`)
- 2026-10-30 [Proved] For every draw sequence `dist(x_n, K_F) <= b^(-n) dist(x_0, K_F)`, and with draws of law `p` the Wasserstein distance from the law of `x_n` to the self-similar measure `mu_p` is at most `b^(-n) integral |x_0 - z| d mu_p(z)`, by coupling with a stationary copy. (witness: ../notes/chaos-game.md, Exact forgetting)
- 2026-10-30 [Proved] Law 2: a linear de Bruijn sequence of order `k` over `F`, `m = card F`, played as the draw schedule puts `x_k, ..., x_(m^k + k - 1)` one in each level-`k` cell, counting only the points `x_n` with `n >= k`, as for the random painting time, no schedule paints every cell with fewer draws, and the schedules of length `m^k + k - 1` that do are exactly the linear de Bruijn sequences (under the same count); a de Bruijn cycle paints every cell once per lap of `m^k`. (witness: ../notes/chaos-game.md, Perfect painting)
- 2026-10-30 [Verified] De Bruijn schedules paint every cell exactly once on two laps in `54` of `54` (design, `k`) cases; the perfect cyclic paintings, counted as Eulerian circuits by brute force, number `1, 1, 2, 16` at `m = 2`, `k = 1..4`, `2, 24` at `m = 3`, `6, 20736` at `m = 4`, each `(m!)^(m^(k-1))/m^k`. (witness: ../lab/py/chaos-game verb `paint`)
- 2026-10-30 [Proved] The expected number of uniform draws until every level-`k` cell is painted is `E[T] = sum over non-empty M inside F^k of (-1)^(card M + 1) Z_(F^k minus M)(1/m)`, `Z_W` the counting series of the width-`k` rule `W` of the memory dial; each term is the expected time before the free game first plays a window of `M`, and at `k = 1` the sum is the coupon collector's `m H(m)`. (witness: ../notes/chaos-game.md, The random painting time)
- 2026-10-30 [Verified] `E[T] = 19/2`, `18850259/711620 = 26.489220`, `89259/3640 = 24.521703`, `55.068831`, `58.632878` at `(m, k) = (2,2), (3,2), (2,3), (4,2), (2,4)`, the alternating sum and a recursion over painted sets agreeing as rationals at these and at `k = 1`, `m = 2, 3, 4`; against the coupon value `(k-1) + m^k H(m^k)` the excess is `+0.166667, +0.028506, +0.778846, -0.022833, +1.541214`, so for `k >= 2` that value is neither an upper nor a lower bound.
