# Boundary drift

- 2026-10-30 [Proved] Contact lemma: for a self-similar design with tile of `f` cells, `A` face-adjacent tile pairs and the same face-contact count `c` on every axis (`c` positions where the high and the low face of the tile are both occupied, `f != c`), level `k` has `E_k = A (f^k - c^k)/(f - c)` face-adjacent pairs and boundary per cell `2 dim - 2A/(f - c) + (2A/(f - c)) (c/f)^k`; the copies of level `k` meet across a tile pair in exactly `c^k` pairs because both faces are digit-wise products. Witness: ../notes/connectivity.md, section "The boundary law"; `../lab/py/boundary-drift`, which recounts the cross-copy pairs `A c^(k-1)` on the digit rule at every level from 2.
