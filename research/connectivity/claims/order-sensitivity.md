- 2026-10-17 [Proved] Noncommutation of the letter matrices is necessary for order sensitivity and not sufficient. In the frame of row vectors `(comp, H, V, fill)`, with `H` and `V` the maximal horizontal and vertical runs, the six letter classes act by `T_unit : (comp, H, V, fill) -> (fill, fill, fill, fill)`, `T_diagonal -> (2 fill, 2 fill, 2 fill, 2 fill)`, `T_row -> (H, H, 2 fill, 2 fill)`, `T_column -> (V, 2 fill, V, 2 fill)`, `T_gasket -> (comp, H + fill, V + fill, 3 fill)` and `T_full -> (comp, 2 H, 2 V, 4 fill)`, so that `comp(A_w) = (1, 1, 1, 1) T_(c_1) ... T_(c_level) e_1^T` and `comp(A_a (x) A_b) - comp(A_b (x) A_a) = (1, 1, 1, 1) (T_a T_b - T_b T_a) e_1^T`, which forces `T_a T_b != T_b T_a` whenever the two orders differ; 14 of the 15 class pairs fail to commute, the exception being the units against the diagonals with `T_diagonal = 2 T_unit`.
