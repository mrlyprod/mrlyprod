# Mobius and primes with no two equal adjacent digits

The integers with no two equal adjacent base-`b` digits keep exactly `(b-1)^L` of the integers with `L` digits, the dimension of one missing digit, and form no digit design; a run expansion of their transform carries a chain certificate below `1/5` from base `1153`, and from there the Mobius function cancels on the set and its primes are counted, `A(x)/log x` of them, with constant `1`, where a design of alternating halves inside the set gave primes only at even bases from `61270` and at a lower count, by a theorem on digit automata of which the Kummer set is the two-state case.
