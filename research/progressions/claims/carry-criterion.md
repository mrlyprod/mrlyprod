# The carry criterion

- 2026-10-17 [Proved] Let `b >= 2`, let `F` be a set of digits of `{0, .., b - 1}` with `0 in F`, and let `S = F + F - 2F` be the signed set `{f + g - 2h : f, g, h in F}`. The digit design `(b, F)` holds a three-term progression `x + z = 2y`, `x != z`, exactly when `F` holds one or one of four closing patterns holds: `b in S` and `-1 in S`; `b, -b - 1, 1 in S`; `-b in S` and `1 in S`; `-b, b + 1, -1 in S`. A progression is a closed walk from `0` to `0` in the graph on the carries `{-1, 0, 1}` with an edge `c -> c'` exactly when `b c' - c in S`, the carries bounded by `abs(x_i + z_i - 2 y_i) <= 2(b - 1)`, and it is nontrivial exactly when the walk leaves `0` or `F` holds a progression. Witness: ../notes/carry-criterion-census.md, section The criterion.
- 2026-10-17 [Proved] A digit design with `0 in F` is progression-free exactly when its level `3` is, the four closing patterns being walks of length `2` or `3`; the criterion reads only the six memberships of `1, -1, b, -b, b + 1, -b - 1` in `S`. Witness: ../notes/carry-criterion-census.md, section The criterion, bullet Three levels decide.
- 2026-10-17 [Proved] `F` is progression-free mod `b`, in the sense that `f + g = 2h` mod `b` has no solution in `F` besides `f = g = h`, exactly when `F` is progression-free in the integers and neither `b` nor `-b` lies in `S`; so the no-carry case of the criterion is exactly the digit lift of a progression-free subset of `Z_b`, and that lift is progression-free at every level. Witness: ../notes/carry-criterion-census.md, section The census, bullet The lift of a cyclic set.
- 2026-10-17 [Verified] The criterion against brute force on every integer-progression-free `F` with `0 in F`: to level `5` at bases `3..12`, `0` mismatches, the first level holding a progression never past `3`; at level `3` at bases `2..27` on `293820` digit sets, `0` mismatches; at level `3` on every maximiser at bases `28..40`. The cyclic column of the census, recomputed by a direct search mod `b`, equals the no-carry column at every base `2..40` and matches A304884 at `3..40`, whose term `2` at `n = 2` falls outside the convention the record states; the half-base column equals `r_3(ceil(b/2))`, A003002. Witness: ../lab/rs/carry-census.
