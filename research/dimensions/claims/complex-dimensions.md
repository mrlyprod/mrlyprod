# Complex dimensions

- 2026-08-28 [Conjecture] Every one-base design is lattice, so the Lapidus-Maier machinery is not out of reach but empty: `zeta_level(s) = 1/(1 - fill base^(-s))` puts the complex dimensions on one vertical line of period `2 pi/ln(base)`, and `(ISP)_dim`, the Riemann hypothesis in the language of fractal strings, has no content on the degenerate case, several incommensurable ratios being the change that gives it content; the lattice half is checked on this tree's own poles and folding tables, the `(ISP)_dim` half is a literature reading not yet checked at source, and the dichotomy is a theorem for strings only, so dimension two and above is open in general: settled inside the pluriphase class (the carpet and the interior-hole designs) and, outside it, for the Menger sponge (sponge-measurability.md line 8, Proved). Witness: ../lab/py/complex-dimensions.
- 2026-09-06 [Proved] The Sierpinski carpet is not Minkowski measurable: its complement in the open square is the disjoint union of `8^(m-1)` open squares of side `3^(-m)` whose boundaries lie in the carpet, the tube is the exact hole sum, and `eps^(log 8/log 3 - 2) V(eps) -> G(t)` with `G = t^(log 8/log 3 - 2)(1 + 4t/5 - 4t^2/7)` on `[1/3, 1/2)` and `t^(log 8/log 3 - 2)(9/8 + 3t/10 - t^2/14)` on `[1/2, 1)`, `C^1` at the seam, `379/280` at the ends, maximum `1.35561708227` at `t = 0.429638`, minimum `1.3506702097` at `t = 0.692137`, swing `0.3662%`; the non-measurability is in print: Lapidus, Pearse and Winter 2013 state the carpet's case by analogy with their gasket example ("An entirely analogous example could be provided for the Sierpinski carpet", the carpet tiling compatible with a monophase generator, Theorem 5.4); item II of the main theorem of Deniz, Kocak, Ozdemir, Ratiu and Ureyen 2013 covers it by hypotheses the carpet meets (lattice, the boundary of the convex hull in the set, `D > 1`, the open middle square as generator), the paper naming no carpet; so do Kombrink's thesis Theorem 2.38 (read only through Kombrink, Pearse and Winter 2016) and Theorem 1.1(ii) of Kombrink, Pearse and Winter 2016 with the open square. The explicit profile, its extrema and the elementary proof are this tree's own, found printed in none of the sources read. Witness: ../lab/py/complex-dimensions carpet_tube.py, ../notes/dimensions.md measurability with its hypotheses.
- 2026-09-06 [Proved] Every one-base design at `base >= 3` in `dim >= 2` removing at least one digit vector, all removed vectors interior and pairwise differing by at least 2 in a coordinate, is not Minkowski measurable: `base^(dim-1) < fill < base^dim` so `log(fill)/log(base)` is never an integer, `G(t) = t^(log(fill)/log(base) - dim) sum_j fill^(j-1) base^(-j dim) h(t base^j) > 0`, and `t^(dim - log(fill)/log(base)) G` is a polynomial on `[1/base, 1/2)`; includes the parity carpets at every odd base and dimension. Witness: ../notes/dimensions.md measurability with its hypotheses (proof), ../lab/py/complex-dimensions at `base = 3`, `dim = 2` only.
- 2026-09-06 [Refuted] The hole-sum route for the sponge: the level-1 plus hole's boundary is not in the sponge (`(1/2, 1/2, 1)` at distance `1/6`, `(2/3, 1/2, 5/6)` at distance `1/18`), the tube inside the hole is not its parallel volume, and for the open cube the volume of the neighbourhood inside `Gamma` has infinitely many elementary pieces, breakpoints accumulating at `1/6` (sponge-measurability.md line 13), where Definition 2.9 of Kombrink, Pearse and Winter 2016 asks for finitely many polynomial ones; no other strong feasible open set is examined, and the sponge is settled by their Corollary 3.2 instead (sponge-measurability.md line 8, Proved). Witness: ../notes/dimensions.md measurability with its hypotheses, qualification 2, read off the digit rule.
