Lay a periodic pattern on the unit square at every scale `n = 1..N` at once, drop the opacity, and add the layers: that is the stack, and everything about it is a weight on the scales. [The Farey page](../../farey/notes/farey.md) says what the plain stack lights - [the Farey fractions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farey_sequence), `phi(n)` new nodes per scale, a genuinely RH-equivalent object rendered exactly and with no route to a proof. This page is the algebra of the stack itself: what happens when stacks are stacked inside one another, spun, restricted to a chosen set of scales, or built from a different design or a deeper level of the same one, and which of those operations leave the arithmetic of `zeta`. Three of them stay inside one abelian group whose series is a ratio of [zetas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_zeta_function), and the ones that leave it are the only place a new theorem can live.

## Chapters

- [Stacking, selecting and spinning](stacking-and-spinning.md): Stacking a stack is Dirichlet convolution on the layer weights, so stacks form one abelian group; the five natural selections of scales have closed-form nodes; and a spun stack shares nodes exactly at rational rotations, which turns the exact spun stack into a Gaussian Farey set.
- [Franel and the Eisenstein twin](franel-and-eisenstein.md): The Fourier `L^2` discrepancy of the Gaussian Farey set obeys Franel's identity one field up and is equivalent to the Riemann hypothesis for `zeta(s) L(s, chi_-4)`, the hexagonal twin over `Z[omega]` puts `L(2, chi_-3)` in a proved node constant, and the spun picture is read only where it carries a theorem.
- [Levels, dilations and nodes](levels-dilations-nodes.md): One law gives the correlation of any two layers across designs and levels with a closed Gram determinant, the layers form a dilation system whose symbol is `zeta(1 + s)` so no reweighting moves the line, and stacking the edges and corners of each layer gives the node stacks.
- [The lean and the novelty meter](lean-and-meter.md): Translating each layer by a drift leaves the Dirichlet group and still lands a closed form, every stack inside the group renders `zeta` exactly and `1/zeta` only as a picture, and the smoothed novelty meter reads the zeros of `zeta` without certifying or refuting the Riemann hypothesis.
- [The design horocycle and the moire limit](horocycle-and-moire.md): The horocycle sampled on the Ford circles of a design's integers, with its design meter read on three designs, and the local limit of the overlay of two `dim 2` designs at sides `N` and `N + h` under the difference filter, computed exactly from window integrals.
