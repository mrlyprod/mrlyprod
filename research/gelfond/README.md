# The digit-sum twist on a design

Under the per-digit certificate (W), which holds at every one-missing-digit base from `92317`, twisting a missing-digit design with two consecutive digits by its digit sum costs its transform a fixed share of its mass at every frequency once `norm((b - 1) alpha) > 0`, so the major arcs of the unconditional dissection need no L-function, the Mobius and von Mangoldt sums over the design save a power of `x`, and the primes of the design equidistribute in the classes of their digit sum mod `q` coprime to `b - 1`; with the design read in progressions mod every `N` coprime to `b`, the digit sum of its primes is uniform mod every `q` on the classes prime to `gcd(q, b - 1)`.
