## The two-position lemma

- **The lemma. Proved.** Let `F` keep two consecutive digits, put `x_0 = beta/(b + 1)` and let `G_F(x_0) = sup of abs(g_F(v))` over `norm(v) >= x_0`. Then for every real `u` and `alpha`, `abs(g_F(u + alpha) g_F(b u + alpha)) <= fill G_F(x_0)`, and `G_F(x_0) <= min(G_1, G_2, G_3)` with the pair form `G_1 = fill - 4 sin^2(pi x_0/2)`, the kernel form `G_2 = m + K_b(x_0)` and the variance form `G_3 = max(fill sqrt(1 - 16 x_0^2 V_F), min(m + K_b(x_f), fill - 4 sin^2(pi x_f/2)))`. So with `c = c(b, F, beta) = 1 - min(G_1, G_2, G_3)/fill`, which is positive whenever `beta > 0` because `G_1 < fill`, `sup_t abs(hat w_k(t)) <= fill^k (1 - c)^(floor(k/2)) <= (1 - c)^(-1/2) fill^k b^(-gamma k)` at every `k`, with `gamma = log(1/(1 - c))/(2 log b)`.
- **Proof.** Locating: put `v_1 = u + alpha` and `v_2 = b u + alpha`. Then `b v_1 - v_2 = (b - 1) alpha`, so `beta = norm(b v_1 - v_2) <= b norm(v_1) + norm(v_2) <= (b + 1) max(norm(v_1), norm(v_2))`, and one of the two phases has `norm(v_i) >= x_0`; its factor is at most `G_F(x_0)` and the other at most `fill`. The pair form: with `d, d + 1` in `F`, `abs(g_F(v)) <= fill - 2 + abs(1 + e(v)) = fill - 2 + 2 abs(cos(pi v)) = fill - 4 sin^2(pi norm(v)/2)`, which falls as `norm(v)` rises. The kernel form: `g_F = D_b - g_E` with `D_b(v) = sum_(n < b) e(n v)` and `abs(g_E) <= m`; at `x = norm(v)` in `(0, 1/b]`, `abs(D_b(v)) = sum_(n < b) cos(2 pi (n - (b - 1)/2) x)`, each term falling in `x` because `2 pi abs(n - (b - 1)/2) x < pi`, so its supremum over `[x_0, 1/b]` sits at `x_0 <= 1/(2(b + 1)) < 1/b`, and on `[1/b, 1/2]` it is at most `1/sin(pi/b)`. The variance form: `abs(g_F(v))^2 = sum_(d, d' in F) cos(2 pi (d - d') x) = fill^2 - 2 sum_(d, d') sin^2(pi (d - d') x)`, and at `x <= x_f` every `abs(d - d') x <= 1/2`, where `sin^2(pi y) >= 4 y^2`, while `sum_(d, d') (d - d')^2 = 2 fill^2 V_F`; so `abs(g_F(v)) <= fill sqrt(1 - 16 x^2 V_F)`, falling in `x`, on `[x_0, x_f]`, and past `x_f`, which is at most `1/b`, the pair and kernel forms at `x_f` apply. The product: pair the positions `2i, 2i + 1` of `hat w_k(t) = prod_(j < k) g_F(b^j t + alpha)` with `u = b^(2i) t`, bound an odd last factor by `fill`, and use `floor(k/2) >= (k - 1)/2`.
- **The dichotomy is exact. Proved.** On the lattice the lemma cannot hold: at `t = -alpha` every phase is `b^j t + alpha = -(b^j - 1) alpha`, an integer because `b^j - 1` is a multiple of `b - 1`, so `abs(hat w_k(-alpha)) = fill^k` at every `k`. With two consecutive digits the twisted transform loses a fixed fraction of its mass at every frequency exactly when `alpha` is off the lattice.
- **The consecutive pair is needed. Verified.** At base 5, `F = {0, 2}` and `alpha = 1/8`, off the lattice with `beta = 1/2`, every phase `5^j (3/8) + 1/8 = (3 * 5^j + 1)/8` lies in `Z/2`, where `abs(g_F) = 2`, so `abs(hat w_k(3/8)) = 2^k = fill^k` at every `k`; the generator reads `1` to twelve digits at `k = 4, 8, 12` (verb `lemma`).
- **The constants. Verified.** At base 10 missing `7`: `c >= 0.1500` and `gamma >= 0.0352` at `alpha = 1/2`, `c >= 0.0463` at `1/7`, `0.0353` at `1/4`, `0.0224` at `1/5`, `0.0055` at `1/10`, and `5.55 * 10^-5` at `1/900` (`beta = 0.01`), each from the variance form, which beats the pair form by a factor `66` at `alpha = 1/2` and `61` at `1/900`. At large base the kernel form carries `beta = 1/2` and `0.2`, `c >= 0.3633` and `0.0644` at `base 92317`, and the variance form carries small `beta`, `c >= 6.666 * 10^-7` at `beta = 10^-3`: to leading order it gives `c = 8 x_0^2 V_F`, and since `V_F` is about `b^2/12` and `x_0` about `beta/b`, that is `c` about `(2/3) beta^2` at small `beta` and large base, where the pair form alone decays like `beta^2 b^(-3)` (verb `lemma`).

## The twisted peel

- **The peel. Proved.** For every `alpha`, every `i >= 1` and every real `s`, the twisted grid sum `Sigma_i(s) = sum_(a < b^i) abs(hat w_i(s + a/b^i))` obeys `Sigma_i(s) <= B_b(F) Sigma_(i-1)(b s)`, so `Sigma_i(s) <= B_b(F)^i`, and under (W) `Sigma_i(s) <= fill^i b^(i alpha_1)`: the certificate of [mobius](../../mobius/notes/mobius.md) with `C_F = 1`, at every shift, for the twisted transform. Proof: `hat w_i(t) = g_F(t + alpha) hat w_(i-1)(b t)` and `hat w_(i-1)` has period `1`. Write `a = a' + r b^(i-1)` with `a' < b^(i-1)` and `r < b`; then `b (s + a/b^i) = b s + a'/b^(i-1) + r`, so the second factor depends on `a'` alone, and `s + a/b^i + alpha = (T + r)/b` with `T = b (s + alpha) + a'/b^(i-1)`, so the sum over `r` is `sum_(r mod b) abs(g_F((T + r)/b)) <= B_b(F)`. The twist moves only the shift `T`, and `B_b(F)` is a supremum over every shift.
- **The hybrid bound transfers. Proved.** The hybrid `l^1` bound of [mobius](../../mobius/notes/mobius.md) reads the transform through three things: the split `hat F_k(t) = hat F_(i_1)(t) hat F_(k - i_1 - i_2)(V_1 t) hat F_(i_2)(y t/V_2)`, the certificate at a shift or at `s = 0`, and `abs(g_F') <= pi b (b - 1)` at one position. The split holds for `hat w_k = prod_(j < k) g_F(b^j t + alpha)` with the same three factors read for `hat w`, the derivative of `g_F(v + alpha)` in `v` has the same bound, and the shifted grids its proof integrates over are covered by the peel above, as is `int_0^1 abs(hat w_i) <= b^(-i) B_b(F)^i`. So under (W), `sum_(a in R(D, H)) abs(hat w_k(a/y)) <= (1 + pi b) fill^k (V_1 V_2)^(alpha_1)` at every `alpha`, with `R(D, H)`, `V_1` and `V_2` as there.
- **The chain and the window do not transfer as written.** The digit-uniform chain of [coprime](../../coprime/notes/coprime.md), which carries the untwisted certificate down to `base 584`, telescopes a run of positions into one Dirichlet kernel, `prod_(j < l) D_b(b^j u) = D_(b^l)(u)`, which needs consecutive arguments in ratio `b`; the twisted arguments are `b^j t + alpha`, and `b (b^j t + alpha) = b^(j+1) t + b alpha` differs from `b^(j+1) t + alpha` mod `1` unless `beta = 0`. The window certificates of the same page bound a supremum over the one-parameter family of shifts `s`, while a twisted window sees the shift vector `(b^j s + alpha)_j`. Neither is refuted; neither is carried, so only the per-digit (W) serves here.
- **The readings. Verified.** At base 10 missing `7` the grid supremum of `sum_(r mod b) abs(g_F((T + r)/b))` reads `24.702183` on `2 * 10^5` shifts, certified below `24.702781` by the Lipschitz margin, against the floor `2(b - 1) = 18` and the step 3 bound `39.6623`. Over `16` classes of `alpha`, `12` shifts and depths `1..5`, `960` checks, the one-step identity holds to `1.40 * 10^-11` and `Sigma_i(s)/B^i` and `Sigma_i(s)/(B Sigma_(i-1)(b s))` stay below `0.999914`. The twist raises the unshifted mass, `Sigma_5(0)^(1/5)` reading `21.7151` at `alpha = 1/2` and `22.3176` at `1/7` against `19.5656` untwisted: it moves mass toward the shifted supremum, which (W) already pays (verb `peel`).

## The power saving

- **The theorem. Proved.** Let `F` keep two consecutive digits and satisfy (W), so `B_b(F) <= fill b^(alpha_1)` with `alpha_1 < 1/5`, and let `beta = norm((b - 1) alpha) > 0`. With `c = c(b, F, beta)` and `gamma` of the two-position lemma, put `sigma = gamma (1 - 4 alpha_1)/(5 - 4 alpha_1)`, `eta = min(1/5 - alpha_1, sigma)` and `delta_0 = alpha_b eta/4`. Then for every `delta < delta_0` there is an effective `C`, depending on `b`, `F`, `beta` and `delta` alone, with `abs(M_F(x; alpha)) <= C A_F(x) x^(-delta)` and `abs(psi_F(x; alpha)) <= C A_F(x) x^(-delta)` at every `x >= 2`. Since `c` rises with `beta`, the same `C` serves every `alpha` with `norm((b - 1) alpha) >= beta`. The bullets below are the proof; the blocks, the dissection and the minor arcs are those of the unconditional dissection of [mobius](../../mobius/notes/mobius.md) and its von Mangoldt form on [coprime](../../coprime/notes/coprime.md), and only the depth of the blocks and the major arcs change.
- **The blocks, at power depth.** The block split of [mobius](../../mobius/notes/mobius.md) writes `S_F` below `x` as at most `fill + 1` blocks `P y + D_k`, `y = b^k`, at each scale `k < L`, the shorter elements included, and the point `x`. On a block `s_b(P y + t) = s_b(P) + s_b(t)` for `t < y`, so its twisted sum is `e(alpha s_b(P)) Sigma(P, k)` with `Sigma(P, k) = sum_(t in D_k) e(alpha s_b(t)) mu(P y + t) = y^(-1) sum_(a mod y) hat w_k(a/y) S_P(-a/y)`, by orthogonality on `Z/y` applied to `t -> 1_(D_k)(t) e(alpha s_b(t))`, whose transform at `a/y` is `hat w_k(a/y)`, and `S_P(theta) = sum_(P y <= n < (P+1) y) mu(n) e(n theta)`; for `Lambda` read `Lambda` in both. Put `tau = eta/4`. The scales with `b^k < x^(1 - tau)` weigh at most `2 (fill + 1) fill^(k_0)`, `k_0` the largest of them, with `fill^(k_0) < x^((1 - tau) alpha_b)`, which is `<< A_F(x) x^(-tau alpha_b)` by the mass floor `A_F(x) >= fill^(L-1) - 1 >= (x/b)^(alpha_b) - 1`, times `log x` for `Lambda`. Every other block has `r = x/y <= x^tau`.
- **The dissection.** On such a block put `Q = y^(3/5)` and `Z = y^zeta` with `zeta = 2 gamma/(5 - 4 alpha_1)`, which is below `2/5`: each of `G_1`, `G_2`, `G_3` is at least `min(fill - 1, m + b/pi) >= fill/pi`, so `gamma <= log(pi)/(2 log 3) < 0.53` at every base, `zeta < 0.26` and `zeta < gamma/2`; give each `a mod y` its Dirichlet fraction `l/d`, `d <= Q`, and its height `h = abs(a d - l y) <= y^(2/5)`, and cut three regions: A is `d >= y^(2/5)`, B is `d < y^(2/5)` with `max(d, h) >= Z`, and C is `d < Z` and `h < Z`, the union of the C1 and C2 of [mobius](../../mobius/notes/mobius.md).
- **The minor arcs.** For `mu` the bullet The minor-arc input of [mobius](../../mobius/notes/mobius.md) (Basak, Robles and Zaharescu, Theorem 1.4, at both approximations) and for `Lambda` the bullet The minor arcs, at source of [coprime](../../coprime/notes/coprime.md) ([Maynard 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.07711), Lemma 4.2, at a coarser fraction) give, with `x^(o(1))` there replaced by the ratio `r`: on A, `x d^(-1/2) <= x y^(-1/5) = x^(4/5) r^(1/5)` and `(x d)^(1/2) <= x^(4/5)`, so `abs(S_P(a/y)) <<_eps x^(4/5 + eps) r^(1/5)`; on B the same term plus `x (log x)^4 max(d, h)^(-1/2)`, the second approximation's `x (h/y)^(1/2) <= x^(7/10) r^(3/10)` lying below `x^(4/5) r^(1/5)` because `r <= x`.
- **Region A.** By the peel at `s = 0` its residues carry at most `B_b(F)^k <= fill^k y^(alpha_1)`, so region A is `<<_eps y^(-1) fill^k y^(alpha_1) (r y)^(4/5 + eps) r^(1/5) <= fill^k r^(1 + eps) y^(alpha_1 - 1/5 + eps)`.
- **Region B.** Group its residues into the classes `D <= d < 2D`, `H <= h < 2H` or `h = 0` of [mobius](../../mobius/notes/mobius.md), at most `(log_2 y + 2)^2` of them, each with `16 b^2 D (D + H) <= 32 b^2 y^(4/5) <= y` once `y >= (32 b^2)^5` and `max(D, H) > Z/2`. By the transferred hybrid bound a class weighs at most `y^(-1) abs(S_P) (1 + pi b) fill^k (32 b^2 max(D, H)^2)^(alpha_1)`. The first term of `abs(S_P)` with `max(D, H) <= y^(2/5)` gives `<<_eps fill^k r^(1 + eps) y^((4/5) alpha_1 - 1/5 + eps)`, below region A's exponent, and the second with `max(D, H) > Z/2` and `2 alpha_1 - 1/2 < 0` gives `<< fill^k r (log x)^4 Z^(2 alpha_1 - 1/2) = fill^k r (log x)^4 y^(-zeta (1/2 - 2 alpha_1)) = fill^k r (log x)^4 y^(-sigma)`.
- **Region C, with no L-function.** It holds at most `sum_(d < Z) phi(d) (2Z/d + 1) <= 3 Z^2` residues, `abs(S_P) <= y` for `mu` and `<= y log(2x)` for `Lambda`, and the two-position lemma gives `abs(hat w_k(a/y)) <= (1 - c)^(-1/2) fill^k y^(-gamma)` at every residue. So region C is at most `3 (1 - c)^(-1/2) fill^k Z^2 y^(-gamma) log(2x) = 3 (1 - c)^(-1/2) fill^k y^(2 zeta - gamma) log(2x)`, and `gamma - 2 zeta = sigma` by the choice of `zeta`. No Lemma A', no input (M), no Perron integral and no exceptional zero enters: the whole region, C1 and C2 together, is paid by the lemma.
- **Assembly.** The three regions give `abs(Sigma(P, k)) <<_eps fill^k r^(1 + eps) y^(-eta + eps) (log x)^6` with `r <= x^tau` and `y >= x^(1 - tau)`, and the blocks at these scales, at most `fill + 1` per scale, carry `(fill + 1) sum_(k < L) fill^k <= (fill + 1) fill^L <= (fill + 1) fill (A_F(x) + 1)`. So they weigh `<<_eps A_F(x) x^(tau (1 + eps) - (1 - tau)(eta - eps)) (log x)^6`, and at `tau = eta/4` the exponent is at most `-3 eta/4 + eta^2/4 + 2 eps <= -eta/2 + 2 eps` since `eta <= 1/5`; the discarded scales save `x^(-tau alpha_b) = x^(-eta alpha_b/4)`. Every `delta < alpha_b eta/4 = delta_0` follows on taking `eps` small, every step that asked `y` large holding for `x` past an effective point and the rest absorbed into `C`.
- **Where it holds. Proved.** At one excluded digit (W) holds at every `base >= 92317` through the step 3 constant, a half line by the proved floor of the ladder on [mobius](../../mobius/notes/mobius.md); so the theorem is Proved at every one-missing-digit set from `base 92317`. At `m > 1` it holds wherever `PB_b(m) < fill b^(-4/5)`, which admits `m <= 176` at `base 10^7`; the wall itself is certified in interval arithmetic at 200 bits, `1/5 - alpha_1 >= 1.87123612 * 10^-8` at `92317` and `<= -4.82092732 * 10^-8` at `92316` (verb `lemma`).
- **Below `92317` on the scan. Verified.** On the float-scanned chord wall of [mobius](../../mobius/notes/mobius.md) (W) holds from `base 39363` at every excluded digit and from `28352` at `e_0 in {0, b - 1}`, so the theorem holds there on that scan, which is never Proved.
- **The exponent. Verified.** `delta_0 >= 4.678 * 10^-9` at `base 92317` at `beta = 1/2` and at `beta = 0.2`, where region A binds through `1/5 - alpha_1 >= 1.871 * 10^-8`, and `delta_0 >= 3.47 * 10^-10` at `beta = 10^-3`, where region C binds; on the chord wall `delta_0 >= 6.741 * 10^-8` at `base 39363`, `beta = 1/2`; at `base 10^6`, `delta_0 >= 2.819 * 10^-4` at `beta = 1/2` and `4.163 * 10^-5` at `beta = 0.2`. Each is printed with `c` and `gamma` read at the extreme excluded digit, whose variance is the least, and floored at four digits. Region A binds only just above the wall: bisection between `92317` and `10^6` puts the switch to `sigma` at `100084` at `beta = 1/2` and at `93409` at `beta = 0.2`, and `sigma` binds at `10^6` at both (verb `lemma`).
- **The paper.** The theorem, the two-position lemma, the twisted peel and the corollary are written up for an outside reader as [A Power Saving for the Digit Sum on Missing Digits](../papers/digit-sum-twist-power-saving.md).

## Which region needs what

- **The ledger of inputs. Proved,** by the proof above set beside the dissection of [mobius](../../mobius/notes/mobius.md).
- Region A: the certificate (W) through the peel at `s = 0` and the minor-arc exponent `4/5`, unchanged by the twist; it asks `alpha_1 < 1/5` and sets the wall.
- Region B: (W) through the transferred hybrid bound and the second approximation, unchanged; it asks `alpha_1 < 1/4`, and with `Z` a power of `y` its saving `Z^(2 alpha_1 - 1/2)` becomes a power.
- Region C, the old C1 and C2: only the two-position lemma, which asks two consecutive digits and `beta > 0`; in the untwisted dissection C1 needed Lemma A' of [coprime](../../coprime/notes/coprime.md) and C2 needed the input (M) for `mu` or (M_Lambda) for `Lambda`, the zero-free regions of Chang and Martin and of Koukoulopoulos and the finite family of exceptional characters, and none of them is used.
- The blocks: depth `tau log_b x` in place of the `kappa sqrt(log x)` of [mobius](../../mobius/notes/mobius.md), because no region now caps `Z` at `exp(C_0 sqrt(log x))`.
- The certificate: only the per-digit (W) serves, so the theorem's wall at one excluded digit is that of (W), `92317` by proof and `39363` on the float scan, against `584`, `301` and `115` for the untwisted bound on the chain and window certificates.
- So the twist trades the base for the shape: the untwisted bound is Proved from `base 584` and holds from `base 115` on the window certificates, at `exp(-c sqrt(log x))`; the twisted one is Proved from `base 92317`, at `x^(-delta)`.
