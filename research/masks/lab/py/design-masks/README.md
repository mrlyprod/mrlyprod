# design-masks

- Attention masks drawn from MrlyMath designs against dense, local-window and random masks at equal density, on two synthetic sequence tasks; then Toeplitz masks whose offsets form a one-dimensional digit design, against strided, random-offset and window masks, by exact 2-hop coverage and on character-level tiny Shakespeare.
- Masks come from the `mrly` binary, built with `cargo build -p mrly --release`: `tile.build.create_2d` draws a recipe at side `81` from one group of the Universe catalog (Fractal, Magic, Special, Mosaic, General) at seeds `1, 2, 3` and `tile.build.build_2d` builds it; `tile.build.random_tile_6d` and `tile.build.build_6d` give a hex cell at seeds `1, 2, 3`, on where a triangle is a fill or a cube face, tiled periodically where shorter than `81` and center-cropped to `81 x 81`; `math.two.create` gives the base-3 codes `127` and `239` at level `4`.
- A draw with density outside `[0.03, 0.97]` is skipped. Built masks are cached as `masks.json` under `data/`, so the binary runs once; the cache is disposable and rebuilds on the next call.
- The attention allowed set is `causal AND (M OR diagonal)`, rows queries, columns keys; effective density is its share of the `3321` causal pairs.
- Each design mask gets a random twin, a uniform random subset of the strict lower triangle of the same size plus the diagonal, drawn fresh per seed, and a local window, the narrowest causal band `t - w..t` with at least as many pairs, so at or above the design's density; dense is the full causal set.
- `census` prints per mask the recipe, raw density, effective density, the share of all pairs within `1` and `2` hops of `M OR I`, the share of causal pairs within `2` hops of the allowed set (what a two-layer model can route at all), the Fiedler value (second-smallest Laplacian eigenvalue) of `M OR M^T` and of the symmetrised allowed set, and the same numbers for the window and the seed-1 random twin; for the base-3 codes it asserts the mask is the level-th Kronecker power of its `3 x 3` corner tile and prints the tile and its fill.
- `sweep` trains a pre-norm causal decoder, `2` layers, `4` heads, width `64`, learned positions, vocab `32`, AdamW at lr `3e-3`, linear warmup `50` steps then cosine to zero, batch `64`, `3` seeds per condition, on two tasks; a seed fixes the initial weights and the data for every condition, so comparisons are paired. The conditions train as one stacked ensemble on `mps` (cpu if missing), `6` conditions per group.
- Copy: tokens uniform on `23` symbols, a lag `n` in `1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, 55` written as a token at position `0`, target at `t` the token at `t - n`, loss on `t >= n`.
- Recall: `20` key-value pairs at positions `0..39`, keys distinct, then `41` key queries, loss on their values.
- Loss is the mean cross-entropy of the last `20` steps; accuracy is on `4` fresh batches. Results go to `results.json` under `data/`; a group already there is skipped unless `--fresh`.
- `table` reprints the summary from the results: per condition its pair count, the window's excess over its design, each seed's loss, the mean, the standard deviation (ddof `1`) and the accuracy for both tasks; per design mask the paired seeds where its loss is lower than its random twin's, its window's and dense; the won:lost count of the means; the window excess over every design; and per task the design-minus-twin loss over the `19` masks, its mean, sd and t, the masks lower or higher on all `3` seeds and the two-sided sign test on those counts.
- `lags` prints copy accuracy per lag for every condition over its offset share, the share of queries `t >= n` that see `t - n` in one hop; the pooled correlation of accuracy with the offset share and with two lag-token coverages; accuracy by offset-share bin; for the offset share the within-run correlation, each run's lag profile centered, a by-condition cluster bootstrap of the pooled correlation (`2000` draws, seed `0`), and the correlation without windows, for designs alone and for twins alone; the blind-default bound `1 + 8/23`, the summed accuracy of a model that cannot read the lag token and copies at one favoured lag, the conditions at or under it and the correlation above it; the design-minus-twin offset gap against the accuracy gap per (design, lag), its correlation, sign agreement and clear violations; and the share of queries that see position `0` per design.
- `offsets`: a Toeplitz mask allows key `j` for query `i` when `i - j` lies in an offset set `D`; the digit design `D(F)` is the `n < 81` with all base-`b` digits in `F`. It runs every `F` with `0` in `F` and `2 <= |F| < b` at bases `2, 3, 4, 5, 9` (`|F| <= 4` at `9`, none at `2`), de-duplicates equal sets, and prints per set its offset count, pairs, density, 1-hop and 2-hop offset coverage (share of `0..80` in `D` and in `D + D`), causal pairs within `2` hops and the first gap, the least offset outside `D + D`. Beside it, at the same offset count: strided `{0..l-1}` plus the multiples of `l` (the Sparse Transformer pattern), `l` nearest in count; `200` uniform random offset sets; `200` random sets with the same pairs, uniform by exact counting; the window `0..k-1`. Then the digit criterion check, the Pareto front, the full-coverage designs, the strided masks, the counting bound and the 2d masks that are Toeplitz. Writes `offsets.json`.
- `basis`: the integer program over all offset sets at side `81`, minimise pairs subject to `D + D` holding `0..80`, HiGHS through `scipy.optimize.milp` at node limit `3000`, so deterministic; prints the LP relaxation, the best set, its recheck and the solver's dual bound, which is read from HiGHS and not re-derived. Writes `basis.json`.
- `shakespeare`: the `sweep` decoder on character-level tiny Shakespeare (`65` characters, last `10%` held out), `600` steps, batch `64`, `3` paired seeds, `7` conditions per stacked group. Conditions: dense; the three lowest-density digit designs with full 2-hop coverage, the strided mask nearest the lowest in pairs and the Cantor offsets `d3-02`, each with a random-offset twin of equal offset count and pairs drawn per seed and the narrowest window at or above its pairs; `general-2` with its 2d twin and window. Validation loss is the mean over one fresh validation batch at each of the last `20` steps. `--seeds` and `--only` (condition names) pick a subset; the full `600`-step run on seeds `1, 2, 3` writes `shakespeare.json`, any other run `shakespeare-<steps>-<seeds>.json`, so a subset never overwrites it.
- `offsets_table` reprints the cache its `--steps` and `--seeds` name, `shakespeare.json` by default: per condition pairs, density, validation nats per seed, mean, sd, bits, training loss and, for offset masks, each seed's 2-hop coverage, offsets `1..4` held and count of offsets in `0..8`; the ranking; the offset-mask runs grouped by the least offset they miss, against the same seed's window; the share of rows where the 2d twin of `general-2` holds offset `1`; and paired differences, design minus twin, strided, window and dense, with mean, sd, t, df, two-sided p, seeds lower and each seed's value, and the per-seed mean over the three designs, so `n` is the seed count; last, design minus strided over every cached seed at that step count.
- `figure` writes `research/masks/figures/research-masks.json`: the first kept mask of each family as `81` rows of `81` zeros and ones.

## RUN

- `uv run --project . python -I research/masks/lab/py/design-masks/masks.py census` from the repo root, `0.7` seconds; the first call runs the binary and caches the masks.
- `uv run --project . python -I research/masks/lab/py/design-masks/masks.py sweep`, `1400` seconds on an Apple M2 (`mps`): `18` groups of up to `6` conditions times `3` seeds, `79` seconds a group, peak resident `0.46` GB; it ends by printing `table`.
- `uv run --project . python -I research/masks/lab/py/design-masks/masks.py table`, `0.7` seconds; `lags`, `0.9` seconds; `figure`, `0.6` seconds.
- `uv run --project . python -I research/masks/lab/py/design-masks/masks.py offsets`, `1.5` seconds; numpy only, scipy is imported for `basis`.
- `uv run --project . python -I research/masks/lab/py/design-masks/masks.py basis`, `119` seconds, `--nodes` the dial.
- `uv run --project . python -I research/masks/lab/py/design-masks/masks.py shakespeare --seeds 4,5,6 --only strided-7,d3-01,d5-013,d5-012`, the replication, `115` seconds, one group, peak resident `0.64` GB; it writes `shakespeare-600-4-5-6.json`.
- `uv run --project . python -I research/masks/lab/py/design-masks/masks.py shakespeare --group 7`, `515` seconds on an Apple M2 (`mps`): `3` groups of `7, 7, 5` conditions times `3` seeds, `188` seconds a full group, peak resident `0.60` GB; it ends by printing `offsets_table`, which alone takes `0.8` seconds. Nothing was dropped: all `19` conditions, `3` seeds, `600` steps.
- `--steps` (`300` for `sweep`, `600` for `shakespeare`), `--batch 64`, `--lr 0.003`, `--evals 4`, `--group 6`, `--nodes 3000` and `--fresh` are the dials.
- The steps are half the planned `600`: at `45` ms per condition step the `600`-step sweep would take about `48` minutes; `--steps 600` runs it.
- Training on `mps` is not bit-reproducible: a gap at the third decimal, such as `3.207` against `3.211`, is inside the seed spread and would not survive a rerun.
- The MPS allocator is capped at half the device memory; one process trains at a time.
- Reads `target/release/mrly` and `tinyshakespeare.txt`; writes `masks.json`, `results.json`, `offsets.json`, `basis.json`, `shakespeare.json` and the `shakespeare-<steps>-<seeds>.json` caches under `data/research/masks/lab/py/design-masks/` and the figure JSON.

## PREDICTIONS

- Written before the full `shakespeare` run, after a `25`-step smoke run whose losses were seen and not saved, from the offset sets alone; the twins are fixed by their seeds, so which twin holds offset `1` was known: all `3` twins of `d3-01`, seed `2` of `d5-013`, seed `3` of `d5-012`, seed `2` of `strided-7`, none of `d3-02`.
- Winner: dense or a window ends lowest, and `window-12` and `window-13` end within `0.02` nats of dense.
- Strided beats the best digit design: `d3-01 - strided-7` between `+0.01` and `+0.08` nats, positive on all `3` seeds, because `d3-01` lacks offset `2` and `5..8` and `strided-7` holds `0..6`.
- Near offsets decide: every offset mask without offset `1` ends at least `0.1` nats above the same seed's window at its density; so the three designs beat their twins pooled, while `d3-01` against its twins, which all hold `1`, lands within `0.03` either way.
- Cantor: `d3-02`, all offsets even and so blind to the previous character, loses to `window-8` by at least `0.1` nats on all `3` seeds.
- `general-2`, which sees every odd offset and no positive even one, beats its 2d twin and loses to `window-24` on all `3` seeds.

## OUTCOME

- Winner: hit on the first half, `window-8` ends lowest at `1.8861`; missed on the second, `window-12` and `window-13` end `0.0603` and `0.0549` below dense, not within `0.02`.
- Strided over the best digit design: hit, `d3-01 - strided-7` `+0.0499`, per seed `+0.0735`, `+0.0367`, `+0.0393`; the replication on seeds `4, 5, 6` gives `+0.0604`, `+0.0635`, `+0.0515`.
- Near offsets: hit, the `12` runs without offset `1` end at least `0.2858` above their windows; designs beat twins, per-seed mean `-0.1507`, t `-3.70`, df `2`, p `0.0658`; `d3-01` against its twins `+0.0035` on the mean, its seeds from `-0.0330` to `+0.0591`.
- Cantor: hit, `d3-02 - window-8` `+0.3292`, sd `0.0081`, on all `3` seeds.
- `general-2`: hit, below its 2d twin by `0.2458` (t `-9.30`), which is the offset-`1` effect, and above `window-24` by `0.0339` on all `3` seeds.
