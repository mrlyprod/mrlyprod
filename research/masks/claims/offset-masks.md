# Offset masks

- 2026-10-18 [Proved] Let `F` be a set of base-`b` digits holding `0` and `D(F)` the integers whose base-`b` digits all lie in `F`. For every side `T >= b`, the Toeplitz mask with offsets `D(F)` cut to `[0, T)` reaches every offset `0..T-1` in two hops exactly when `F + F` holds every digit `0..b-1`; otherwise the least offset it misses is the least digit outside `F + F`. Witness: ../notes/masks.md, section Offset masks.
- 2026-10-18 [Proved] A Toeplitz mask with `k` offsets reaches at most `k(k+1)/2` offsets in two hops, so full 2-hop coverage at side `81` needs at least `13` offsets. Witness: ../notes/masks.md, section Offset masks.
