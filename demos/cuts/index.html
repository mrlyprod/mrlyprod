demos / cuts

Every diagonal cut is the same size

A plane x + y + z = s slides through the level-L solid of mrly_bang_d3_126 and meets exactly 3^L cells at every height it meets at all. The binary digits of the height say which corners each scale may use, so every cut is a Sierpinski gasket, and the two central heights together fall into six of them tiling a hexagon. Drag the bar chart to move the plane.

The profile is the coefficient list of the digit polynomial, so a height is counted without building a single cell; the points are enumerated on the plane itself and projected down the (1,1,1) axis in Rust, one circle per cell, coloured by height and by top-scale corner. The section view is the crate's own triangular mesh through the same solid.
