The stack lights the Farey fractions

Scale n draws a line at every k/n. A reduced fraction a/b is drawn by every scale divisible by b, so its brightness is the floor of Q over b. Scale n lights phi(n) nodes never seen before, and phi(n) = n - 1 exactly when n is prime.


  
The nodes come from the Stern-Brocot walk of the Farey sequence and the totients from a sieve, both in Rust; the page only stacks bars. The primes are read off the totients as the scales of maximal novelty.
