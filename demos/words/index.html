The words

One design per level. Same letters in a different order, a different set. Build a word letter by letter, first letter outermost, and every number below comes back out of the crates as a product over the letters.

the word

The first letter alone grows level by level in the sponge.

the pair

Side, fill, density and dimension are products over the letters, so the readout is exact at any length even where the raster is not: the plane draws to side 243 and the cube to side 128, and a shorter render is labelled k of L letters because it is the box cover of the whole word at that scale and never a shallower word. Words with two or more letters only; a word of one repeated letter is the ordinary fractal under another name. Links: the sponge grows one cube design level by level, moire stacks one design over its scales.
