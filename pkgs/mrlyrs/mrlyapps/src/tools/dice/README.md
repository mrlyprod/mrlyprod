# dice

Seven dice in one cup: d2, d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 and d20. Rolls come from a seeded generator, so a round can be replayed - reset with the same seed and the same faces come up in the same order. The last eight rolls stay on screen, newest first, alongside a counter of every roll this round.

## Using

- Tap to roll. Every roll lands with a blip.
- Switching the die deals a fresh round on the same seed.
- The face can be drawn as tiles, digits or emoji; the tile skin shows pips for 1 through 6 and digits above that.
- The emoji skin only works on the grid surface, not the canvas.
- Reset takes a seed of your choosing, or the clock picks one for you.
