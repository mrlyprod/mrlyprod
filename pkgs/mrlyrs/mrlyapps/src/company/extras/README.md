# extras

The strip of small print: social links, a donate and a help button, the legal pages, a cycling tagline and the copyright line. All of it is fixed content baked into the app; the only moving part is the tagline, which steps through five lines one at a time.

## Using

- Three social links - instagram, reddit and twitter - all point at mrlyprod.
- **donate** opens the donation page; **help** starts an email to the help address.
- Privacy and terms lead to the two company pages.
- **cycle** steps the tagline to the next of the five lines and wraps after the last.
- The line you left it on is saved, so the strip picks up where it stopped.
- The copyright line closes the strip.
