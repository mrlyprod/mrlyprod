# identity

The identity card: who the system thinks you are, on one read-only screen. It shows three facts - your handle, your id and whether you are verified - and offers no buttons, no settings and no verbs at all.

## Using

- The handle is the id with an @ in front.
- *verified* shows as a plain yes or no.
- There is nothing to press: the app has no actions, and anything sent at it is refused.
- The card stores nothing of its own; it just reads the identity it is handed and draws it.
