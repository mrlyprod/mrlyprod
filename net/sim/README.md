# SIM

- MrlyNet is the OS; a simulation is a config (`world.json`: apps, actors, rules and picks) plus players on top of it. This folder is the players; it holds no app, no verb, no reducer.
- `goose.ts`: `goose(moves, draw, skip?)`, a seeded random player that fills a legal verb's args from its schema (`fill`); a verb it cannot fill (a bare `pattern`) it skips; it proves every verb callable.
- `bots.ts`: `round(table, players, at, apps)`, one act per player, drawn from the world's seed, its tick and the player's id (`drawFor`), `nav.open` skipped (`SKIP`); a `Table` is `{ world, list, call }`, whatever host holds the world.
- With no app holding a `bot`, each act is the goose's over every legal verb; with one, a player picks an app of the world and plays its `bot` (`Bot`: `Seen` `{ state, me, actors, at, draw, world }` in, a `Step`, `{ verb, args }` or `null`, out), or the goose over that app's verbs when it has none; a step that is not a legal move fitting its schema is skipped, and so is `null`; the round's `at` joins the draw, so a round where every player rests does not freeze the next.
- `run.ts`: `run(table, plan, players, apps)` plays `plan.rounds` rounds, round `i` at the start's `at` plus `i` beats (`BEAT`, a second of world time, never the wall clock); `playersOf(world, n)` is the config's `actors`, each with the kind the booted world gives it (`../sdk/kinds.ts` `kindOf`), or bots `bot-1` to `bot-N` when it names none.
- The hosts hold what a player needs and nothing more: `../hosts/local/sim.ts` wraps the world folder in a `guarded` table and writes the report; `../shell/world.ts` takes the round through its `players` option, wired by the site's host; `../test/gym.ts` plays it in memory for the app tests.
- `Bot`, `Seen` and `Step` are declared in `../sdk/system.ts` (an app's `bot` is typed there as `Hooked`) and re-exported here, so the sdk never imports this folder.
- Imports: `sim` reads `../kernel`, `../sdk` and `../spec`; `../hosts`, `../doors` (`mrlynet sim`, `mrlynet goose`) and the tests read `sim`; `kernel`, `sdk`, `shell` and `spec` never do (`../test/imports.test.ts`).
- Tests: `goose.test.ts` (every shape of schema filled, each act passing it, the unfillable verb skipped); the round and the run are proved where they are played: `../hosts/local/sim.test.ts`, `../shell/world.test.ts`, `../doors/gym/gym.test.ts`.
