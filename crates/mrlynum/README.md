# mrlynum

The instruments of Mrly: primes, divisors, series, recurrences, spectra, lattices, networks. Plain numbers and byte grids go in, and counts, fractions, rates and measurements come out; every answer is exact where the integers allow and a stated approximation where they do not.

One arm reads number: primes with their ranks, gaps and square shapes, factorizations with their divisors and Mobius values, the classic sequences, and the exact rule hidden in any sequence handed over. The other arm measures shape: the tallies of a grid, the network of its filled cells, the Fourier spectrum of a field, the Walsh spectrum of a boolean function, and the visible lattice its fractions draw.

## Instruments

- **prime** tests, lists and studies primes: rank, gap, twin, and the rectangles, splits and squares a number makes.
- **factor** splits a number into primes, divisors, totient, radical and Mobius value.
- **classics** lists the evens, odds, powers of two, Fibonacci, Catalan and prime sequences, with exact gcd and fraction reduction.
- **blend** adds, convolves, shifts and differences sequences, then recovers the exact recurrence and its growth rate.
- **series** sums harmonic, zeta, beta and Dirichlet series, counts visible lattice points, and pins the Bernoulli fractions.
- **lattice** lists the reduced fractions of a window as nodes, counts coprime pairs, and estimates pi from them.
- **census** counts the cells of a grid and its exposed faces.
- **graph** lifts a grid into a network of nodes and branches and takes its census: tips, junctions, components and fractal dimension.
- **fft** transforms in one and two dimensions and centres a field's magnitude spectrum.
- **boolean** takes a truth table's Walsh spectrum, nonlinearity, balance and avalanche.
